# Nether Souls

Is the Low Fantasy [[TTRPG]] I'm planning to replace my current [[Dungeons & Dragons]] campaign with.


## Magic

### Animism
The preactise of binding spirits to objects, getting their powers. Spirits can be willing or not but unwilling spirits are harder to bind and control. Some animists believe in freeing spirits from their bindings after their service is done, others believe in keeping them bound forever.

> Classes reprsentation: [[Druids]], [[Shamans]], [[Witches]].

### Arcana
The study and manipulation of the fundamental forces of nature. Arcanists bend and weave the arcanes symbols to create magical effects though arcane formulas. Knowledge of the arcane let's their users to manipulet those basic blocks to tailor the effects as they want.

> Classes reprsentation: [[Wizards]]

### Conduit
Those that want or need power but are not ablte to produce it, may turn to powerful entities to gain it. This power, still, is not theirs but borrowed and has strings attached. If the Conduit's patron is displeased, they may take back the power or worse.

> Classes reprsentation: [[Warlocks]], [[Clerics]].
