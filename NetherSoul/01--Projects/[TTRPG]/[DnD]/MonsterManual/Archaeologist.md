### Actions 
***Multiattack.*** The archeologist makes two attacks. 

***Fisticuffs.*** Melee Attack Roll: +5, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage and 4 (1d6) Force damage, and the target is Grappled (escape DC 13). 

### Reaction 
***Duck and Cover (3/Day).*** *Trigger:* The archeologist is hit by an attack. *Response:* It increases his AC by 2 against that attack. If that causes the attack to miss, he can use Fisticuffs against the attacker.
