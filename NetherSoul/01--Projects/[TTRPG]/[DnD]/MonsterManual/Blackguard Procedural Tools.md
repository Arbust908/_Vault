

# Procedural Tools
Blackguard is a gargantuan metropolis, and not every square inch of it has yet been fully detailed.

If your players interact with an NPC you didn't expect them to, or enter an establishment you haven't thought up, you can roll on the following tables to quickly create an interesting location from scratch at the table.

Make Your Own. While these tables provide a guideline, you can always ignore them, pick the option you like the most, or create your own. 
When you run Blackguard at your table, it becomes your city, no one else's.

## Establishments
