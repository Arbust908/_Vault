***Magic Resistance.*** Archon has [[Advantage]] on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Reincarnation (1/Day Mythic Trait).*** If Archon would be reduced to 0 hit points, his current hit point total instead resets to his maximum hit points he regains and uses Herald Beasts, and regains any expended uses of Legendary Resistance.


### Actions
***Multiattack.*** Archon makes four attacks, one with each of his possible attacks.

***Felling Axe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +14, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 20 (3d8 + 7) slashing damage plus 9 (2d8) necrotic damage. If this attack reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points, it dies immediately.

***Spiked Mace.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +14, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 20 (3d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) necrotic damage, and the target is [[Stunned]] until the start of its next turn.

***Shock Spear.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +14, reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 20 (3d8 + 7) piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) lightning damage, and the target's speed is reduced to 0 until the end of its next turn.

***Thorn Net.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +14, range 30/90 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d8 + 7) piercing damage plus 18 (4d8) lightning damage, and the target is [[Grappled]] and [[Restrained]] (escape DC 22).

***Spellcasting.*** Archon casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 22):

**At Will:** [[Animal Friendship]], [[Animal Messenger]], [[Speak with Animals]], [[Find Steed]]
**3/Day Each:**  [[Awaken]], [[Greater Restoration]], [[Plane Shift]], [[Lightning Bolt]]
**1/Day Each:** [[Animal Shapes]], [[Reincarnate]]

### Bonus Actions
***Herald Beasts (Recharge 5-6).*** The Beast Herald summons 1d4 Beasts of CR 1 or lower.

***Charge.*** Archon moves up to his speed and the next attack he makes deals an additional 18 (4d8) bludgeoning damage.
