***Magic Resistance.*** Baba Yaga has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Foresight.*** Baba Yaga has Advantage on D20 tests.

***Language Expert.*** Baba Yaga knows when she hears a lie or a couched truth.

***Reincarnation (1/Day Mythic Trait).*** If the Witch Crone would be reduced to 0 hit points, her current hit point total instead resets to her maximum hit points, she immediately recharges and uses Beating Hearts, and regains any expended uses of Legendary Resistance.


### Actions
***Multiattack.*** Baba Yaga makes four Witch's Claw attacks and uses Beating Hearts if she can. She can replace an attack with a use of spellcasting.

***Witch's Claw.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +18, reach 5 ft., range 30/120 ft., *Hit:* 23 (3d8 + 10) slashing damage plus 27 (6d8) necrotic damage and the target becomes Prone and is cursed.

***Beating Hearts (Recharge 5-6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 26, each creature of Baba Yaga's choice in a 60-foot Cone. Failure: 52 (8d12) force damage. Success: Half damage.

***Spellcasting.*** Baba Yaga casts one of the following spells, using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 26):

**At Will:** [[Alter Self]], [[Bestow Curse]], [[Detect Magic]], [[Detect Thoughts]], [[Dream]], [[Speak with Dead]], [[Transport via Plants]], [[Reincarnate]]
**3/Day Each:** [[Befuddlement]], [[Blight]] (level 7 version), [[Contagion]], [[Dominate Monster]], [[Finger of Death]], [[Plane Shift]]
**1/Day Each:** [[Imprisonment]], [[True Polymorph]]

### Bonus Actions
***Cackling Hexes.*** Each creature within a 60-foot Emanation cursed by Baba Yaga takes 36 (8d8) psychic damage.

***Hag's Step.*** Baba Yaga teleports to an unoccupied space she can see within 60 feet.

***Fresh Elixer (1/Round).*** Baba Yaga casts a spell with her spellcasting trait.

***Snicker-Snack.*** Baba Yaga makes a Witch's Claw attack, if she gets a natural 20 on the D20 test, she cuts the head of the creature. The creature dies if it can’t survive without the lost head.

### Mythic Actions
*The following legendary actions can be used for 1 Hour after the Mythic Trait has been activated*

***Hither & Yon.*** Baba Yaga uses Hag's Step and makes two Witch's Claw attack.

***Stolen Breath (1/Round).*** Constitution Saving Throw: DC 26, each creature of Baba Yaga's choice in a 60-Emanation. Failure: The target is suffocating until the end of Baba Yaga's next turn. Success: The target is immune to Stolen Breath for the next 24 hours.
