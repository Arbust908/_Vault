***Magic Resistance.*** Erlking has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects..

***Thaw Anew (1/Day Mythic Trait).*** If Erlking would be reduced to 0 hit points, his current hit point total instead resets to his maximum hit points and he regains any expended uses of Legendary Resistance.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** Erlking makes four Ice Hammer attacks. He can replace one attack with Whirlwind of Frost, if available.

***Ice Hammer.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +11, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 21 (3d10 + 5) bludgeoning damage plus 21 (6d6) cold damage and the target is pushed 15 feet away from Erlking

***Whirlwind of Ice. (Recharge 5-6)*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18, each creature in a 60-foot Emanation. *Failure:* 33 (6d10) cold damage and 33 (6d10) bludgeoning damage.

### Bonus Actions
***Turn To Ice.*** Constitution Saving Throw: DC 18, each creature of Erlking's choice in a 30-foot Emanation that isn't blinded. 

*Failure By 5 or More:* The target is instantly petrified.

*Failure:* The target is restrained and repeats this saving throw at the end of its next turn, becoming petrified on a failure.

*Success:* The target is no longer restrained by this feature.

### Legendary Actions
*Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature’s turn, Erlking can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Erlking regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*

***Frozen Ground (1/Round).*** A 30-foot emanation originating from Elrking becomes difficult terrain for hostile creatures until the end of his next turn.

***Lunge.*** Erlking moves up to half his speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks and makes an attack with Ice Hammer.

### Mythic Actions
The following legendary actions can be used for 1 Hour after the Mythic Trait has been activated

***Flurry of Strikes.*** Erlking makes two Ice Hammer attacks.

***Look at Me (1/Round).*** Erlking uses Turn To Ice, but instead of an emanation he targets a single creature of his choice.
