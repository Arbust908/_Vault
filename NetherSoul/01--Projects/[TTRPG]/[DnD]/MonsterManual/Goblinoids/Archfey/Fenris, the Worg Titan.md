***Magic Resistance.*** Fenris has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Resurrection (1/Day Mythic Trait).*** If Fenris would be reduced to 0 hit points, its current hit point total instead resets to its maximum hit points and it regains any expended uses of Legendary Resistance.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** Fenris makes three attacks, using Felling Claw and Grappling Bite in any combination. It can replace an attack with Deathly Howl.

***Felling Claw.*** Melee Attack Roll: +15, reach 10 ft. Hit: 34 (4d12 + 8) slashing damage plus 22 (4d10) psychic damage and the target falls prone.

***Grappling Bite.*** Melee Attack Roll: +15, reach 10 ft. Hit: 34 (4d12 + 8) piercing damage plus 22 (4d10) psychic damage and the target is grappled. Fenris can only grapple one creature with this attack at a time.

***Deafening Howl (Recharge 5-6).*** Constitution Saving Throw: DC 22, each creature in a 60-foot Emanation. Failure: 44 (8d10) psychic damage and the target is Deafened. *Success:* Half damage only.

### Bonus Actions
***Lunge.*** Fenris makes its next attack with Advantage.

***Bite Down.*** Fenris makes a Grappling Bite attack against a creature it is grappling and deals an additional 21 (6d6) force damage if it hits.

### Legendary Actions
*Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature’s turn, Fenris can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Fenris regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*

***Fey Step (1/Round).*** Fenris teleports to an unoccupied space it can see within 30 feet.

***Pounce.*** Fenris moves up to its speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks and makes an attack.

### Mythic Actions
*The following legendary actions can be used for 1 Hour after the Mythic Trait has been activated*

***Terrifying Presence (1/Round).*** Constitution Saving Throw: DC 22, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation. Failure: 22 (4d10) psychic damage.

***Maul (1/Round).*** Fenris makes two Grappling Bite attacks against a creature it is grappling.
