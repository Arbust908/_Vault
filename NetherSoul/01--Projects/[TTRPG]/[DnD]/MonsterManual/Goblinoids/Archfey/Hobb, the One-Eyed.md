***Magic Resistance.*** Hobb has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Inevitable Return (1/Day Mythic Trait).*** If Hobb would be reduced to 0 hit points, his current hit point total instead resets to his maximum hit points and he regains any expended uses of Legendary Resistance.

***Evasion.*** If Hobb is subjected to an effect that allows him to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, he instead takes no damage if he succeeds on the save and only half damage if he fails. Hobb can’t use this trait if he has the Incapacitated condition.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** Hobb makes four attacks using Rediron and Vile Eye in any combination, then uses Aura of Irons if it is available.

***Rediron.*** Melee Attack Roll: +16, reach 10 ft. Hit: 29 (4d10 + 7) slashing damage plus 22 (4d10) fire damage and the target is moved 15 feet to an unoccupied space of Hobb's choosing.

***Vile Eye.*** Ranged Attack Roll: +16, range 600 ft. Hit: 44 (8d10) psychic damage, and the target is poisoned until the end of their next turn.

***Aura of Irons (Recharge 5-6).*** Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 24, each creature in a 60-foot Emanation. Failure: 22 (4d10) slashing damage plus 22 (4d10) fire damage and the target suffers from the Burning hazard. Success: Half damage only.

### Bonus Actions
***Restraining Grapple.*** Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 24, one medium or smaller creature Hobb can see within 10 feet. Failure: The target is Grappled (escape DC 24) and Restrained.

***Quick Movement.*** Hobb takes one of the following actions; Disengage, Dash, or Hide.

### Reactions
***Parry and Riposte.*** Trigger: Hobb is hit by a melee attack roll while holding a weapon: Response: Hobb adds 9 to his AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss. If it misses, Hobb can make an attack with Rediron.

### Legendary Actions
*Legendary Action Uses: 3 (4 in Lair). Immediately after another creature’s turn, Hobb can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Hobb regains all expended uses at the start of each of his turns.*

***Command the Troops (1/Round).*** Allied creatures within 60 feet can move up to half their speed and make a melee attack.

***Direct Control.*** Hobb makes an Rediron attack from a space within 60 feet that is occupied by one of his allies.

***Feint.*** Hobb moves up to his Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks, and makes one Rediron attack.

### Mythic Actions
*The following legendary actions can be used for 1 Hour after the Mythic Trait has been activated*

***Red Hot Weapons (1/Round).*** All creatures within 10 feet of Hobb take 14 (4d6) fire damage.

***Rise Above It (1/Round).*** Hobb ends a condition affecting him.

***Double Tap.*** Hobb makes two attacks with Rediron.
