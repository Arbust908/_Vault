### Traits
***Abduct.*** The bugbear needn’t spend extra movement to move a creature it is [[grappling]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The bugbear makes three Spirit Beasts attacks.

***Spirit Beasts.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +7 (with [[Advantage]] if the target is [[Grappled]] by the bugbear), reach 10 ft. or range 120 ft. _Hit:_ 18 (3d8 + 4) Force damage.

***Spellcasting.*** The bugbear casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):

**At Will:** [[Bane]], [[Detect Magic]], [[Mage Hand]], [[Thaumaturgy]]
**2/Day Each:** [[Conjure Woodland Beings]] (level 4 version), [[Cure Wounds]], [[Invisibility]]
**1/Day Each:** [[Conjure Elemental]], [[Tree Stride]]

### Bonus Actions
***Quick Grapple.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, one medium or smaller creature the bugbear can see within 10 feet. *Failure:* The target is [[Grappled]] (escape DC 15).

**Fey Step.** The bugbear magically teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.

### Reactions
***Vengeful Magic (3/Day).*** The bugbear casts [[Hellish Rebuke]] in response to the spell’s trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
