### Traits
***Abduct.*** The bugbear needn’t spend extra movement to move a creature it is [[grappling]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The bugbear makes two Scimitar attacks.

***Scimitar.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +9 (with [[Advantage]] if the target is [[Grappled]] by the bugbear), reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) piercing damage.

***Marksman's Bolt (Recharge 5-6).*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, a creature between 50 and 500 feet away. *Failure:* 90 (20d8) force damage. *Success:* Half damage.

### Bonus Actions
***Quick Grapple.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 16, one medium or smaller creature the bugbear can see within 10 feet. *Failure:* The target is [[Grappled]] (escape DC 16).

***Hunker Down.*** The bugbear drops [[Prone]] and takes the [[Hide]] action.
