### Traits
***Abduct.*** The bugbear needn’t spend extra movement to move a creature it is [[grappling]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The bugbear makes two Handaxe attacks. It can replace one attack with Snatch & Grab.

***Handaxe.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll*: +5 (with [[Advantage]] if the target is [[Grappled]] by the bugbear), reach 10 ft. or range 20/60 ft. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

***Snatch & Grab.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5 (with [[Advantage]] if the target is [[Grappled]] by the bugbear), reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage. If the target was [[Grappled]] by the bugbear, it becomes [[Restrained]] and the bugbear can move up to its speed.

### Bonus Actions
***Quick Grapple.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 13, one medium or smaller creature the bugbear can see within 10 feet. *Failure:* The target is [[Grappled]] (escape DC 13).

### Reactions
***Punch Down.*** *Trigger:* A creature grappled by the bugbear hits it with an attack. *Response:* The bugbear makes a Handaxe attack.
