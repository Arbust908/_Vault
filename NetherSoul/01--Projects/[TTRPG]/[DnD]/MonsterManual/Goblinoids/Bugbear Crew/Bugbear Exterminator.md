### Traits
***Abduct.*** The bugbear needn’t spend extra movement to move a creature it is [[grappling]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The bugbear makes three Morningstar attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Blinding Flash.

***Morningstar.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +10 (with [[Advantage]] if the target is [[Grappled]] by the bugbear), reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) piercing damage plus 11 (2d10) force damage.

***Blinding Flash (Recharge 5-6).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 18, all hostile creatures in a 30-foot [[Emanation]] of the bugbear. *Failure:* 27 (5d10) force damage and the target is [[Stunned]] until the start of their next turn. *Success:* Half damage.

### Bonus Actions
***Quick Grapple.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 18, one medium or smaller creature the bugbear can see within 10 feet. *Failure:* The target is [[Grappled]] (escape DC 18).
