### Traits
***Abduct.*** The bugbear needn’t spend extra movement to move a creature it is [[grappling]].

***Legendary Resistances (3/Day).*** If the bugbear fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

***Evasion.*** If the bugbear is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, the bugbear instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the save and only half damage if it fails. It can’t use this trait if it's [[Incapacitated]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The bugbear makes three Morningstar attacks. It can replace one with a use of Paralyzing Squeeze.

***Flaming Morningstar.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +11 (with [[Advantage]] if the target is [[Grappled]] by the bugbear), reach 15 ft. *Hit:* 17 (2d10 + 6) piercing damage plus 18 (4d8) fire damage.

***Paralyzing Squeeze.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 19, a creature grappled by the bugbear. *Failure:* 36 (8d8) necrotic damage and the target is [[Paralyzed]] until the start of their next turn. *Success:* Half damage.

### Bonus Actions
***Quick Grapple.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 19, one medium or smaller creature the bugbear can see within 10 feet. *Failure:* The target is [[Grappled]] (escape DC 19)
