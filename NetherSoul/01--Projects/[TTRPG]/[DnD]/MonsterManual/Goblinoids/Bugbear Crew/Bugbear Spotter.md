### Traits
***Abduct.*** The bugbear needn’t spend extra movement to move a creature it is [[grappling]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The bugbear makes three Javelin attacks. It can replace those attacks with Spellcasting.

***Javelin.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +7, reach 10 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage.

***Spot Weakness.*** An allied creature the bugbear can see within 30-feet may use their reaction to move 15 feet without provoking [[Opportunity Attacks]] take an action (exclusing Multiattack) of their choice. If the target is a bugbear, it can use Quick Grapple. 

***Spellcasting.*** The bugbear casts one of the following spells, using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15):

**At Will:** [[Bless]], [[Command]]
**3/Day:** [[Hold Monster]], [[Dispel Magic]]

### Bonus Actions
***Quick Grapple.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 15, one medium or smaller creature the bugbear can see within 10 feet. *Failure:* The target is [[Grappled]] (escape DC 15).

***Spot Weakness.*** An allied creature the bugbear can see within 30-feet may use their reaction to move up to 15 feet without provoking Opportunity Attacks and take an action (excluding Multiattack) of their choice. If the target is a bugbear, it can also use Quick Grapple.
