### Traits
***Cruel.*** The goblin deals an additional 2 (1d4) damage when it hits an attack with advantage.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The goblin makes three Chaotic Blast attacks and uses Chaotic Aura. It can replace one attack with a use of Spellcasting.

***Chaotic Blast.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +11, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 29 (5d8 + 7) force damage and non-Fey creatures within 10 feet of the target take 12 (5d4) force damage.

***Chaotic Aura.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 19, each non-Fey creature within a 10-foot [[Emanation]] originating from the goblin. *Failure:* The target takes 10 (3d6) force damage.

***Spellcasting.*** The goblin casts one of the following spells, using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 19):

**At Will:** [[Moonbeam]], [[Shocking Grasp]], [[Thaumaturgy]]
**3/Day Each:** [[Blink]], [[Fear]], [[Grease]], [[Scorching Ray]] (level 4 version), [[Sleep]], [[Telekinesis]]
**1/Day Each:** [[Cone of Cold]] (level 8 version), [[Disintegrate]], [[Fireball]] (level 6 version), [[Teleport]]

### Bonus Actions
***Nimble Escape.*** The goblin takes the [[Disengage]] or [[Hide]] action.

***Fey Step.*** The goblin teleports to a point it can see within 30 feet of itself.

### Reactions
***Protective Magic (3/Day).*** The goblin casts [[Counterspell]] or [[Shield]] in response to the spell’s trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
