### Traits
***Cruel.*** The goblin deals an additional 2 (1d4) damage when it hits an attack with advantage.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The goblin makes two attacks, using Scimitar or Shortbow in any combination. It can replace one attack with a Burgle.

***Scimitar.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) poison damage

***Shortbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +6, range 80/320 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) poison damage.

***Burgle.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, one Medium or smaller creature the goblin can see within 5 feet. _Failure:_ The goblin takes a weapon or object out of the target's hands.

### Bonus Actions
***Nimble Escape.*** The goblin takes the [[Disengage]] or [[Hide]] action.
