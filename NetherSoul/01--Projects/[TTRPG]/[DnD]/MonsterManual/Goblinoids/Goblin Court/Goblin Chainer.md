### Traits
***Cruel.*** The goblin deals an additional 2 (1d4) damage when it hits an attack with advantage.

### Actions
***Handaxe.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) poison damage

***Ball & Chain.*** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 14, 15-foot [[Emanation]] centered on the goblin. _Failure:_ The target takes 14 (4d6) bludgeoning damage and falls [[Prone]].

### Bonus Actions
***Nimble Escape.*** The goblin takes the [[Disengage]] or [[Hide]] action.
