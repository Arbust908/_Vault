### Traits
***Cruel.*** The goblin deals an additional 2 (1d4) damage when it hits an attack with advantage.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The goblin uses Heckle twice. It can replace one use with Spellcasting.

***Heckle.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 14, one creature the goblin can see within 60 feet. *Failure*: The target takes 13 (2d8 + 4) psychic damage and has Disadvantage on its next attack roll.

***Spellcasting.*** The goblin casts one of the following spells, using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):

**At Will:** [[Thaumaturgy]]
**1/Day Each:** [[Bane]], [[Hideous Laughter]], [[Grease]]

### Bonus Actions
***Nimble Escape.*** The goblin takes the [[Disengage]] or [[Hide]] action.

### Reactions
***Insult.*** *Trigger:* A creature the goblin can see hits it with an attack roll. *Response:* Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 13, the triggering creature. Failure: The attack misses instead.
