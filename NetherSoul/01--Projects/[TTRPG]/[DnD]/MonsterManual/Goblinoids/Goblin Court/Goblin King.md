### Traits
***Cruel.*** The goblin deals an additional 2 (1d4) damage when it hits an attack with advantage.

***Otherworldly Restoration.*** If the Goblin King dies while in Faerie, their body dissolves into notes and tunes. They gain a new body in 7 days, reviving with all their Hit Points in the Labyrinth.

***Implacable.*** The goblin cannot be moved against its will.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The Goblin King uses Fencing or Heckle in any combination up to 3 times total, and starts Singing

***Fencing.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +8, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) psychic damage and the target is moved along the ground to a point within 10 feet of the goblin.

***Heckle.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, one creature the goblin can see within 60 feet. *Failure:* The target takes 14 (2d8 + 5) psychic damage and has Disadvantage on its next attack roll.

***Singing.*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 16, creatures within a 10-foot [[Emanation]] originating from the Goblin King. *Failure:* The target is [[Charmed]] until the end of its next turn.

### Bonus Actions
***Nimble Escape.*** The goblin takes the [[Disengage]] or [[Hide]] action.

### Reactions
**_Parry and Riposte._** _Trigger:_ The goblin is hit by a melee attack roll while holding a weapon: _Response:_ The Goblin King adds 3 to its AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss. If it misses, the goblin can make a Fencing Attack.
