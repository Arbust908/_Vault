### Traits
***Cruel.*** The goblin deals an additional 2 (1d4) damage when it hits an attack with advantage.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The goblin makes two Moonblind attacks and breaks into a Dance. It can replace one Moonblind attack with a use of Spellcasting.

***Moonblind.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +8, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft. *Hit:* 23 (4d8 + 5) force damage and the target must succeed a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or be [[Blinded]] until the start of the goblin's next turn.

***Dance.*** The goblin creates a 30-foot Emanation originating from itself. The goblin's allies have [[Advantage]] on attack rolls and saving throws until the start of the goblin's next turn, provided the goblin isn’t [[Incapacitated]].

***Spellcasting.*** The goblin casts one of the following spells, using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16):

**At Will:** [[Thaumaturgy]], [[Moonbeam]] (level 4 version)
**3/Day Each:** [[Cure Wounds]], [[Faerie Fire]], [[See Invisibility]], [[Shatter]], [[Thunderwave]]
**1/Day Each:** [[Bestow Curse]], [[Call Lightning]] (level 5 version), [[Fear]] [[Hypnotic Pattern]], [[Lightning Bolt]] (level 5 version)

### Bonus Actions
***Nimble Escape.*** The goblin takes the [[Disengage]] or [[Hide]] action.

### Reactions
***Vanishing Insult.*** *Trigger:* A creature the goblin can see hits it with an attack roll. *Response:* Wisdom Saving Throw: DC 16, the triggering creature. Failure: The attack misses instead and the target is teleported to a point the Moonmage can see within 30 feet.
