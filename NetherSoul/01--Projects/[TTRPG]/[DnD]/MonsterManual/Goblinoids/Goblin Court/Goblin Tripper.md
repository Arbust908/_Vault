### Traits
***Cruel.*** The goblin deals an additional 2 (1d4) damage when it hits an attack with advantage.

### Actions
***Billhook.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +4, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d10 + 2) slashing damage.

***Shortbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +4, range 80/320 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

### Bonus Actions
***Nimble Escape.*** The goblin takes the [[Disengage]] or [[Hide]] action.

***Reposition.*** The goblin takes the [[Dash]] action.

### Reactions
***Trip.*** *Trigger:* The goblin hits a medium or smaller creature with an attack using its Billhook. *Response:* *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 11. *Failure:* The target falls prone.
