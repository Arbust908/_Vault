***Regimented.*** The hobgoblin deals an extra 7 (2d6) damage to a creature it hits with an attack while the hobgoblin is within 5 feet of an [[Ally|Allied]] [[Creature]] that isn't [[Incapacitated]].

***Aura of Authority.*** While in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the hobgoblin, the hobgoblin and its allies have [[Advantage]] on attack rolls and saving throws, provided the hobgoblin isn’t [[Incapacitated]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The hobgoblin makes three attacks, using Longsword, Javelin, or Order in any combination.

***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d8 + 3) slashing damage, and one allied creature within 60 feet that can see the hobgoblin may take a Reaction to move up to their speed without provoking [[Opportunity Attacks]].

***Javelin.*** *Ranged Attack Roll*: +6, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage, and the target’s [[Speed]] decreases by 10 feet until the start of the hobgoblin’s next turn, unless its speed is already reduced.

***Order.*** An allied creature in within 60 feet that can hear the hobgoblin can use their reaction to move up to their [[Speed]] without provoking [[Opportunity Attacks]] and make an attack roll.

### Reaction
***Parry.*** *Trigger:* The hobgoblin is hit by a melee attack roll while holding a weapon: *Response:* The hobgoblin adds 3 to its AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.
