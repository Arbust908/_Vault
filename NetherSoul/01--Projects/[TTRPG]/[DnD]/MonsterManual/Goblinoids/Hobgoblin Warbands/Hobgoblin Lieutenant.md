### Traits
***Regimented.*** The hobgoblin deals an extra 7 (2d6) damage to a creature it hits with an attack while the hobgoblin is within 5 feet of an [[Ally|Allied]] [[Creature]] that isn't [[Incapacitated]].

***Aura of Authority.*** While in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the hobgoblin, the hobgoblin and its allies have [[Advantage]] on attack rolls and saving throws, provided the hobgoblin isn’t [[Incapacitated]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The hobgoblin makes two attacks, using Longsword or Longbow in any combination.

***Longsword.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) slashing damage.

***Longbow.*** *Ranged Attack Roll*: +4, reach 5 ft. or range 150/600 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) piercing damage.
