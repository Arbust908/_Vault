### Traits
***Regimented.*** The hobgoblin deals an extra 7 (2d6) damage to a creature it hits with an attack while the hobgoblin is within 5 feet of an [[Ally|Allied]] [[Creature]] that isn't [[Incapacitated]].

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The hobgoblin makes two attacks, using Pike or Javelin in any combination.

***Pike.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +4, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d10 + 2) slashing damage.

***Javelin.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll*: +4, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

### Reaction
***Pike Wall.*** *Trigger:* A creature moves within 10 feet of the hobgoblin: *Response:* The hobgoblin makes a Pike attack against the creature.
