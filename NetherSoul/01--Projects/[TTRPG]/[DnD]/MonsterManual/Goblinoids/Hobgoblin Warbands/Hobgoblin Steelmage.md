***Legendary Resistance (1/Day).*** If the hobgoblin fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

***Regimented.*** The hobgoblin deals an extra 7 (2d6) damage to a creature it hits with an attack while the hobgoblin is within 5 feet of an [[Ally|Allied]] [[Creature]] that isn't [[Incapacitated]].

### Actions
***Multiattack (Recharge 5-6).*** The hobgoblin makes five Flurry of Steel attacks.

***Flurry of Steel.*** *Melee Attack Roll*: +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 16 (3d8 + 3) force damage, and the hobgoblin can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see within 5 feet of a hostile creature.

***Spellcasting.*** The hobgoblin casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):

**At Will:** [[Detect Magic]], [[Light]], [[Blade Ward]]
**2/Day Each:**  [[Compelled Duel]], [[Cloud of Daggers]]
**1/Day Each:** [[Mass Cure Wounds]], [[Dimension Door]]

### Bonus Actions
***Elemental Summons. (1/Day)*** The hobgoblin summons a [[Fire Elemental]]. It can use a bonus action on each of its turns to command the elemental.

### Reactions
***Protective Magic (3/Day).*** The hobgoblin casts [[Counterspell]] or [[Shield]] in response to the spell’s trigger, using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.
