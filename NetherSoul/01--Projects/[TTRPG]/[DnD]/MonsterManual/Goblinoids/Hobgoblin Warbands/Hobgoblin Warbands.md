### Hobgoblin Warbands
[[Hobgoblins]] are known to marshal unregulated and unorganized groups of [[Goblin Court|Goblins]] or [[Bugbear Crew|Bugbears]] into a more regimented unit of creatures as they take command. 

But even for a group of seasoned adventurers, an entire warband of Hobgoblins is a terrifying prospect; as that martial command quickly elevates each member of their ranks to become a deadly killing machine perfected for the battlefield.

A Hobgoblin Warband is a versatile and mutable unit that can scale up and down into various formations. Within its ranks, you'll find:

- The [[Hobgoblin Regular]] (CR 1/2) makes up the regular rank-and-file soldier in a hobgoblin warband.
- The [[Hobgoblin Archer]] (CR 1) is best deployed behind the pikemen and used to lay down volleys on the enemy.
- The [[Hobgoblin Wolfrider]] (CR 2) rides a [[Warband Dire Wolf]] into battle, designed to break the enemy's defenses attack their rear.
- The [[Hobgoblin Pikeman]] (CR 2) is a front-line infantryman specializing in maintaining a shield-wall and keeping the enemy at bay.
- The [[Hobgoblin Lieutenant]] (CR 3) that stands among the soldiers in the shield-wall to improve the resolve of his regulars.
- The [[Hobgoblin Vanguard]] (CR 4) specializes in breaking through enemy lines and advancing the hobgoblin soldiers in the unit.
- The [[Hobgoblin Commander]] (CR 6) can command a large warband of hobgoblins, issuing orders that'll maintain unit cohesion and advance their goals.
- The [[Hobgoblin Steelmage]] (CR 8) can serve alongside the commander in the back-ranks and sling spells into the enemy's back rank.
- The [[Hobgoblin General]] (CR 12) is a legendary figure in the army that can not only boost morale and resolve, but take on powerful heroes all by himself.
