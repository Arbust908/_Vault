### Trait
***Blacksteel Armor.*** Any acid, cold, fire, lightning and thunder damage dealt to you by an attack is reduced by 3.

### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The Mage Hunter makes three attacks.

***Rapier.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +7, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage.

***Oldfield.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +7, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) piercing damage.

### Bonus Actions
***Humming Leadwave. (1/Day)*** _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 14, each creature that starts its turn, or enters, a 15-foot Emanation originating from the Mage Hunter for the next minute. *Failure:* The creature loses concentration on any spells it's concentrating on, and cannot cast spells until the start of its next turn.

### Reactions
***Quick-Release Leadwave. (1/Day)*** *Trigger:* The Mage Hunter sees a creature trying to cast a spell within 60 feet. *Response:*  _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 14. *Failure:* The triggering creature is stunned until the start of its next turn, and the spell it was attempting to cast dissipates with no effect. If the spell was cast with a spell slot, the slot isn’t expended.
