### Traits
***Anti-Mage.*** Spellcasters that begin their turn within 10-feet of the mage hunter take 12 (5d4) psychic damage.

***Blacksteel Armor.*** Any acid, cold, fire, lightning and thunder damage the mage hunter takes is reduced by 7.

***Evasion.*** If the mage hunter is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, the mage hunter instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the save and only half damage if it fails. It can’t use this trait if it has the Incapacitated condition.

***Legendary Resistances (3/Day).*** If the mage hunter fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

***Magic Resistance.*** The mage hunter has Advantage on saving throws against spells.

### Actions
***Multiattack***. The mage hunter makes four attacks.

***Rapier.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +15, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 8) slashing damage and 27 (6d8) force damage and the target is knocked prone.

***Oldfield.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +15, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 17 (2d8 + 8) piercing damage and 27 (6d8) force damage and the target is pushed 15 feet away from the mage hunter.

### Bonus Actions
***Sustained Leadwave.*** Constitution Saving Throw: DC 22, each creature that starts its turn in or enters a 15-foot Emanation originating from the mage hunter for the next minute. Failure: The creature loses concentration on any spells it's concentrating on, and cannot cast spells until the start of its next turn.

***Exploding Ammunition.*** The mage hunter deals an additional 35 (10d6) force damage on their next attack.

***Stilled with Lead.*** Constitution Saving Throw: DC 22, a creature the mage hunter can see within 60 feet. Failure: Any spells affecting the creature are dispelled.

### Reactions
***Quick-Release Leadwave.*** *Trigger:* The mage hunter sees a creature trying to cast a spell within 60 feet. Response: Constitution Saving Throw: DC 22. *Failure:* The creature is stunned until the start of its next turn, and the spell it was attempting to cast dissipates with no effect, without expanding a spell slot.

### Legendary Actions
Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature’s turn, the mage hunter can expend a use to take one of the following actions. They regain all expended uses at the start of each of their turns.

***Recharge the Device.*** The mage hunter regains a use of their reaction.

***Fan the Hammer.*** The mage hunter makes an attack with the Oldfield.

***Move.*** The mage hunter moves to a point they can see and attacks with its Rapier.

***Throw Leadwave (1/Turn).*** The mage hunter activates their sustained leadwave and throws it to a point they can see within 20 feet. The Emanation originates from that point until picked up and thrown as an action or by using an attack.
