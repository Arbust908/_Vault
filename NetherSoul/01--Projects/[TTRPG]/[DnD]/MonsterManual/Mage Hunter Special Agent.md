### Traits
***Anti-Mage.*** Spellcasters that begin their turn within 10-feet of the mage hunter take 12 (5d4) psychic damage.

***Blacksteel Armor.*** Any acid, cold, fire, lightning and thunder damage the mage hunter takes is reduced by 7.

***Magic Resistance.*** The mage hunter has Advantage on saving throws against spells.

### Actions
***Multiattack***. The mage hunter makes four attacks.

***Oldfield.*** *Ranged Attack Roll:* +10, reach 5 ft, range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) piercing damage and 18 (4d8) force damage.

### Bonus Actions
***Sustained Leadwave.*** Constitution Saving Throw: DC 17, each creature that starts its turn in or enters a 15-foot Emanation originating from the mage hunter for the next minute. Failure: The creature loses concentration on any spells it's concentrating on, and cannot cast spells until the start of its next turn.

### Reactions
***Quick-Release Leadwave.*** *Trigger:* The mage hunter sees a creature trying to cast a spell within 60 feet. Response: Constitution Saving Throw: DC 17. *Failure:* The creature is stunned until the start of its next turn, and the spell it was attempting to cast dissipates with no effect, without expanding a spell slot.
