### Actions

_**Spellcasting.**_ The mage casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13, +5 to hit with spell attacks):

**At Will:** [[Mage Hand]], [[Prestidigitation]]
**1/Day Each:** [[Color Spray]], [[Command]], [[Calm Emotions]], [[Enlarge + Reduce]], [[Mage Armor]] (included in AC)

### Bonus Action
***Disperse Matter. (3/Day)*** _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 13, one Medium or smaller creature the sorcerer can see within 30 feet. _Failure:_ The target takes 2d10 force damage and shape-shifts into a cloud of dispersed matter until the start of the sorcerer's next turn. On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage and does not shape-shift.

While shape-shifted in this way, a creature can’t move or take actions, and other creatures can freely enter and occupy its space. It has immunity to all damage, as well as the grappled, restrained and prone conditions. When the effect ends, the target reforms in its space, or in the nearest unoccupied space if that space is occupied.
