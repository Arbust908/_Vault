### What is The Broadsheet
The Broadsheet is a digital magazine exploring a flintlock-fantasy by gaslight setting; the grand  industrialized metropolitan city of BLACKGUARD, the capital of the Atroxian Republic.

**Flintlock-Fantasy.** It’s not magic, but firearms that give Atroxia an edge over other nations.
**By Gaslight.** Gaslit lanterns line the city’s streets, bathing them in soft, yellow light.
**Industrialized.** A technological revolution is taking the nation by storm, impacting every aspect of life, and not only for the better.
**Metropolis.** Blackguard is a massive metropolis, with numerous boroughs that feel like distinct cities unto themselves.

#### What’s In a Broadsheet?
In this issue of the Broadsheet, you’ll find an adventure that can be run in about one session, a detailed gazetteer about a single borough in the city, and a chapter on setting guidance for running a game in Blackguard, the City of Smoke.

**Doing Without.** If you’re on a tighter budget, you can always pick and choose what elements you’re using, and replace referenced material with material you make or find elsewhere. These references are meant to expand your stories, not constrict them.

### Using the Adventures
Adventure anthologies, such as the one we’re presenting in the Broadsheet, are a resource for game masters that can be used in a variety of different ways. Either as one-shot adventures, filler episodes in a larger campaign, or strung together into a campaign of their own.

#### As A Whole Campaign
You can use the resources presented in the setting guides to create additional adventures in Blackguard to expand the scope of your adventure beyond the presented anthology. If you play once a week, and one session out of four is the anthology story, then you may enjoy treating the anthology as the B-Plot to the grander campaign you and your players are creating together.

#### As a Modular Anthology
If you plan on running each adventure as written with no additional content, you might find that the overall campaign works best as an episodic story. In this case, consider beginning each modular adventure by indicating that it’s been a month or so since we last checked in with the heroes, and discussing what the characters have been up to.

#### As a One-Shot
If you’re running an adventure from the Broadsheets as a one-shot, you may want to skip elements that hint at a larger plot not resolved in the adventure. Alternatively, you can include those elements in the hopes of the one-shot turning into a longer campaign.

# Bank Holiday
> It's holiday season in Blackguard. Midwinter, the day between the months of Evernight and Wintersdusk, is a festive jubilee  across the world, with common folk setting floating lanterns aloft to bring light to the only day of the year when the sun doesn't rise.

BANK HOLIDAY is an action thriller adventure where the party's routine errand to the bank quickly turns into a violent siege when organized gangsters seizes control of the bank, killing security and taking hostages.

**Level Scale.** This adventure is designed for four 3rd-level player characters. They should advance to 4th level at the end of the adventure.

**Party Size.** To adjust the difficulty of the adventure, adjust the number of gangsters robbing the bank up or down. Make sure to adjust the number of gangsters in each encounter.

#### Adventure Premise
Over the course of the adventure, the party will be cut off from the world outside and outgunned by the well-prepared bank robbers.
To stop the crisis, the player characters must stick to the shadows, moving through ventilation shafts to navigate between offices and back corridors, using the bank itself to their advantage. 

Only the player characters can stop this siege from ending in disaster. It's up to them to decide who survives, and whether the truth behind a twenty-year-old tragedy at the root of it all ever comes to light.

### Running The Adventure
Familiarize yourself with the ADVENTURE SUMMARY on this page to understand the backstory and the bank robbers' motives. 
Run the INTRODUCTION as written to set up the stakes and introduce every element of the adventure  to the players.

Use the CHARACTER ROSTER to get to know the NPCs in the adventure, including the robbers, the hostages, and their NPC ally: Managing Director Bastian Penborough.

The BANK LAYOUT will provide you with guidance for how to run this massive building as the players move through it.
Finally, use the ROBBER ENCOUNTERS to continually raise the stakes as the bank robbers move through the bank.

### Plot Hooks
Use the following hooks to get your party to visit the bank to run this adventure:

**Registering an Account.** If the characters recently acquired a house, (Issue #2) or a large fortune, a bank account makes large purchases easier with bank cheques.

**Apply for a Loan.** The party may need to borrow money to repair their home, buy magic items, or for other large purchases.

**Secret Meeting.** While investigating the Skull of the Paladin (Issue #1), or seeking answers about the haunting at the Knowlton Estate or the events on board the Night Train, a contact could point the characters to set-up an account at the bank. Doing so, they'll inevitably run into trouble which will get them a meeting with Bastian Penborough (pg. 8), who offers to look into matters before finding the party to explain his findings.

### Adventure Summary
The bank robbers are not here for money. They are searching for a specific deposit, hidden among the bank’s records and safe boxes, and they are prepared to kill civilians to get it.

#### The City Plaza Bombing
Nearly twenty years ago, during the Siege of Blackguard, a bomb detonated in Destroyer Square, killing hundreds of civilians. 
At the time, the city was under attack by southern secessionist forces, and responsibility for the bombing was swiftly laid at their feet. The incident became part of the accepted history of the war. But the truth was less pretty.

Cardinal Hargrave. In the days following the bombing the Cardinal of Atroxia for the Church of the Mother, Patrick Hargrave, deposited a single document into a safe deposit box at the First Bank of Blackguard. 

It was a signed confession of his role in the attack, identifying the true architect of the plot: Llewelyn Rowe, leader of the Totalitarian Party and CEO of the Rowe Corporation. (Issue #1)

#### The Bank Robbers
The takeover of the First Bank of Blackguard is driven by two distinct and separate motivations, as personified by its two leaders: 
Eliza Harrow (pg 10) is a renegade sorcerer who lost her daughter in the bombing and believes the goal is to find proof of Rowe's involvement.
Maribel Vance (pg 11) secretly works for Rowe. She intends to destroy the confession, and is using Eliza as a scapegoat to be killed before escaping.

#### The Crew’s Plan
The attackers’ plan is simple and efficient.

First, they seize the bank floor, kill or neutralize security, and take hostages to establish control and prevent outside interference. 

Next, they locate the internal records identifying the correct safe deposit box, and the password for the arcane lock protecting it.

Once the deposit is accessed, the confession is destroyed and Eliza is eliminated.

Finally, the crew exits through a prepared route via the upper floors and the roof, leaving behind a clear narrative of a failed bank robbery led by a renegade sorcerer.

## The Bank Layout
The First Bank of Blackguard (pg. 30) is the city’s most prestigious financial institution. A massive stone building, it is the symbol of Bankstreet and, for most average Atroxians, the primary reason to visit this part of the city at all.

#### Floors
The bank's functions are divided across four floors:

- 1st Floor: The Banking Hall
- 2nd Floor: Specialist Offices
- 3rd Floor: Secure Facilities
- 4th Floor: Executive Offices

Movement between floors is handled primarily via a central stairwell and an elevator.

#### Bank Lockdown
Glyphs of Warding across the building are designed to initiate safety protocols when the bank is under threat. When Eliza detonates a fireball within the building, the lockdown is triggered.

**During Lockdown.** All exterior doors and most interior doors are sealed with an Arcane Lock attached to the bank's rotating password system. The elevator ceases operation, but the stairwells and ventilation system remain accessible.

**Password.** The current Arcane Lock password is "Kidds Smoke Lots" 

**Open Doors.** Only two doors remain open and unlocked as the lockdown begins, having been propped open beforehand by Director Julian Mercer with his boots. These are the doors to the Records Room and the Director of Security's Office.

#### Ventilation System
The bank is threaded with vents large enough for a Medium creature to crawl through. 

**Opening a Vent.** Accessing the vents requires opening or removing a secured grate; doing so quietly takes an action and requires a DC 15 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check. 

**Traveling the Vents.** Once inside, traveling through the vents from one area of the bank to another takes 10 minutes. 
A creature can travel to any room or corridor in this way, except for the vault, safe deposit room, or elevator maintenance loft.

**Air Elemental.** The ventilation system is maintained by an air elemental bound to keep airflow clear. 
Each time the characters travel through the vents, roll a 1d6. On a 6, the elemental becomes aware of them and moves to investigate, attempting to force intruders out of the vents with its whirlwind feature if it determines they are obstructing airflow.

### 1st Floor: Banking Hall
The first floor is public-facing and houses the main banking hall, as well as smaller meeting areas.

#### Banking Hall
The banking hall is a broad stone chamber that extends from the ground floor to the domed ceiling a hundred feet above. 

Stone columns divide the open floor at regular intervals, and a long line of teller counters runs along the far wall. A gated-off area behind the counters allows access to more secure areas, including the vaults and secure deposits below, while a publicly accessible stairwell and elevator connect the hall to the upper floors.

At the start of the adventure, most civilians in the bank are gathered here, along with a single clerk and two members of the security team. Once the takeover begins, the attackers seize the hall quickly, kill the security guards, and use it as the primary hostage area for the duration of the siege.

#### Civilians Present
The following civilians are waiting in the banking hall at the start of the adventure:

- Drav Marrish, the archaeologist
- Clara Henwick, the mother
- Julia Henwick, the daughter
- Gareth Simmons, the veteran
- Mara Holt, the teller

#### Safe Deposit Room
A set of stairs behind the teller counters in the banking hall lead down to the safe deposit room and vault below. A heavy, metal door separates the deposit room from the stairway, which is currently sealed by an arcane lock as part of the bank's lockdown procedure.
Inside, rows upon rows of small safe boxes line both walls. To open one, one must place their hand on the correct box while speaking the password.

**Target Box.** The attackers are here for the contents of box 24601, which can be opened with the password Insurance 1423.
**Inside the Box.** A single piece of paper can be found inside the box. It reads as follows:

> I, Cardinal Hargrave, hereby confess that I orchestrated the bombing of Destroyer Square in 1423, at the behest and by the direction of Llewelyn Rowe.

### 2nd Floor: Specialist Offices
The second floor contains offices and meeting rooms and is accessible to members of the public when escorted by a bank employee.

#### Bastian’s Office
Bastian’s office is a tidy, meticulously organized room overlooking the banking hall through a wide interior window. 
Tall filing cabinets line one wall, and a heavy wooden desk occupies the center of the room, its surface stacked with neatly labeled paperwork and forms. 

**Starting Area.** The characters are escorted here at the start of the adventure for a routine administrative meeting. From the office window, the banking hall below is clearly visible, giving the party a clear view of the arrival of the attackers and the initial explosion. 

**Ventilation Grate.** A ventilation grate is set high on one wall, partially concealed behind some shelving. The office door opens onto the second-floor corridor.

**Doors in Lockdown.** When the bank enters lockdown, the bank's network of Glyphs of Warding triggers automatically, sealing the office door with an Arcane Lock.

#### Second Floor Corridor
The second floor corridors are a maze of offices and meeting rooms, all of which are locked and contain nothing of interest to the adventure.

### 3rd Floor: Secure Facilities
The third floor is strictly off-limits, holding secure facilities such as the records room and the armory.

#### The Armory
The armory is plain and utilitarian, designed for secure storage rather than regular use. Doors leading out to the third-floor corridor are sealed by an Arcane Lock when the bank is in lockdown.

> This small room could be mistaken for a large filing room, or a cricket team's locker room, if it weren't for the multiple warning and "OFF LIMITS" signs plastered on each of the twelve lockers here.

**Ventilation Grate.** A ventilation grate is set high on one wall, tucked between two lockers.

**Weapons Storage.** The armory is a reinforced storage room containing a series of lockers sealed with an Arcane Lock, and secured cases used to hold confiscated weapons and mage hunter gadgets in case of emergencies such as this one.

All lockers are protected by arcane locks that use the same rotating password system as the lockdown procedure. 
Inside the Lockers. Looting the lockers, the party finds a small cache of gadgets: 

- 3 Crackleballs
- 2 Gust Canisters
- 1 Gnomeglass Goggles
- 1 Moonflare
- 2 Smotherballs
- 2 Sparkleballs
- 1 Quick-Release Leadwave

Bastian, if he's present, notes that this equipment always makes him uneasy, much as the mage hunters themselves do.

> "Gives you the heebie-jeebies, doesn't it?" Bastian shudders. "These gadgets are designed to hurt people like me specifically, you understand?"

### Records Room
The Records Room is a dense archive of shelves and ledgers arranged according to an arcane filing system that prioritizes internal logic over accessibility. Narrow aisles run between tall shelving units packed with bound volumes and deposit records, leaving little room to maneuver. 

**Poor Visibility.** The lighting is dim and uneven, and the cramped mazelike corridor here grants creatures advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

**Tight Shelves.** As an action, a creature can push over a shelf into an adjacent aisle. Creatures in that aisle must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or fall prone. Whether they succeed or fail, that aisle becomes difficult terrain.

> You can see this door, unlike the others in the facility, is open. It's propped open by what appears to be a particularly nice boot.

**Door.** The door to the corridor is currently propped open with a particularly nice boot. It was left here by Julian Mercer, the Director of Security.

**Ventilation Grate.** A ventilation grate is fitted in the wall next to the door.

> "Unless we know what aisle we're looking for, we could be in here for days." Bastian sighs. "Passwords could be under K for 'codes', don't ask, or A for 'administrative function'. Or is that F?" 

**Employee Handbook.** A handbook in aisle A, section 3, shelf 12 (A-3.12), titled Employee Handbook Vol. 14: Access Management, Tertiary Systems, and Auxiliary Arcane Processes, explains how the bank’s rotating arcane passwords are generated.

They combine the surname of the most recently commissioned parliamentary chancellor (Martha Kidds), the first word of their constituency (Smoke Avenue), and the first word of the commission they received (Lots and Zoning). 

A character that succeeds a DC 10 Intelligence (History) check remembers reading about Kidds' commission a few weeks ago.

**Deposit Records.** A second set of records in aisle R, section 2, shelf 4 (R-2.4) lists recent safe deposit box activity, identifying Safe Deposit Box 24601, registered to Cardinal Hargrave, with its most recent deposit made shortly after the Siege of Blackguard in 1423.

### 4th Floor: Executive Offices
The fourth floor contains executive and C-suite offices, including those of the Director of Security.

#### Director of Security’s Office
The Director of Security’s office is a well-appointed office overlooking the banking hall below. A heavy desk dominates the center of the room, flanked by filing cabinets and a set of wall-mounted schematics of the bank's floors. 

**Door.** The door leads out to the fourth floor corridor, directly in front of the stairwell and elevator. It is currently propped open with a particularly nice boot.

**Ventilation Grate.** A ventilation grate is fitted in the wall in the middle of the room.

**Balcony.** A narrow balcony extends from the back of the office, overlooking the open space of the banking hall. A creature pushed from the balcony falls 60 feet to the stone floor below. 

#### The Desk
In the middle of the room is a nice desk, with a drawer on one side, a small picture frame, and a note in the middle.

## Robber Encounters
The encounters below represent escalating pressure as the attackers work to advance their plan and become aware of the characters’ interference. 

Use them flexibly, advancing to the next stage as the crew adapts to the party’s actions.

### Encounter 1: Search Team
Two gangsters are sent as a pair to retrieve information from the Records Room and the Director of Security's Office. 

They are focused on locating the deposit record and the note with the password specifically and are not expecting any resistance.

**Approach the Party.** They will begin their search in whichever room the party is currently closer to, arriving there around 20 minutes after the siege begins. As they approach, the characters will hear snippets of a conversation.

> "Does she seriously expect us to use parachutes?"
> "That's what I hear. Apparently Kristoff's been smuggling them in here for days."

**Ambushing Them.** Unless the party makes themselves known beforehand, the gangsters will be surprised at the start of combat. They will attempt to escape if possible to alert the other attackers of the characters' presence.
One of the gangsters carries:

- A folded note reading “R-2.4 – HARGRAVE”
- A sending stone, used to relay findings back to Kristoff.

**Alerted!** If the gangsters manage to alert the other attackers, even with gunfire, proceed immediately to Encounter 2. 
**Not Alerted.** Twenty minutes pass before the character that last held the sending stone receives a message from Kristoff.

> "What's taking you so long? The police are already surrounding the place."

Whatever response he receives, Kristoff will become aware that something is wrong. 

Proceed to Encounter 2.

### Encounter 2: The Hunt
Once the attackers realize something is wrong, Kristoff takes control of the situation. He dispatches two teams to search different sections of the bank.

**Team Number 1.** One team consists of himself (gangster boss) and a single gangster. They will immediately start hunting the player characters down, starting at the Records Room, and following any sound or clues they discover, including into the vents.

**Team Number 2.** The second team consists of a single gangster, plus any gangsters that survived the first encounter. 
They will head for the Director of Security's Office to activate the golem. If they succeed, or if the golem is already activated when they get there, they will start hunting the party in the same manner as the first team.

**Finding the Party.** If either team finds the party, they will attempt to pin them in place while calling for backup from the other team.

### Encounter 3: The Ultimatum
With time running out, and only four attackers remaining (Maribel, Eliza and two gangsters), Maribel abandons subtlety.  Maribel's Ultimatum. She shouts up the stairs to attract the party's attention and issues a clear ultimatum. The characters have thirty minutes to bring her the number and password for the deposit box she's after. 

If they refuse or delay, the hostages will be executed one by one. If they comply, she will let everyone go; a bargain she has no intention of honoring.

**Eliza's Conflicted.** Eliza is visibly conflicted throughout this encounter. She argues against killing civilians, particularly Julia Henwick, and can be pressured, persuaded, or pushed toward open defiance, even attacking Maribel, all depending on how the characters engage her.

### Resolving the Adventure
Depending on how the player characters engage with the adventure they could find themselves in over their heads facing off against increasingly terrifying odds, or they might make short work of the bank robbers.

Robbers Win. If the bank robbers obtain the confession letter, they pivot hard to their escape plan. If the party has not stopped them by then, the final act becomes a chase through the upper floors, the elevator maintenance loft, and the roof.

**Party Wins.** If the party secures the letter first, the attackers will attempt to force a trade using hostages, or escalate violence to regain control of the situation.

### What's Next?
When the adventure is over, make sure to give each of the NPCs a small moment to bring their story to a close if they're still alive.
Bastian. The desk wizard thanks the party for their instrumental work in saving the hostages. He promises to clear up any issues they may have with the bureaucracy.

**Drav.** The archaeologist is promised his loan and excitedly plans his next adventure.

**Clara and Julia.** The mother-daughter pair open their account and are given a head start on their savings by a donation from Bastian.

**Gareth.** Demands compensation for his trauma, and is promised as much by the bank.

**Mara.** The teller resigns and decides to get a job doing something less stressful. At the moment an apprenticeship as a cobbler sounds appealing to her.

**Eliza.** The sorcerer thanks the party for everything. If she has the confession, she dedicates herself to bringing the truth to the public's attention, otherwise she leaves the city for good.

Bankstreet is one of 31 boroughs that make up Blackguard, and home to the financial institutions that make Blackguard an international economic monolith. 

As a private borough, it’s owned by the Fellhorn Estate, a private corporation that was set-up to handle the assets of Arthus Fellhorn after his mysterious disappearance. They're a caste of wizards that control the banks and stock market.

### The Lost Hegemony
For a brief period in 1423 and 1424, the Fellhorn Estate controlled the economy, education, the spellcasters, the armed forces, and even the Emperor. Their rule over Atroxia was a hegemony that extended into every part of Atroxian life.

This hegemony was orchestrated by the Erudite Archmage of the Venerable Order, Horace Aldworth

#### Siege of Blackguard
During the Last War, the secessionist forces closed in on the capital in the spring of 1423. The battle lasted eight days as the city became a battleground covered in trenches. The battle tested experimental weapons, chemical weapons, and saw the deaths of thousands.
The Emperor enlisted the aid of the corporations running the city to help defend it. Aldworth proved his loyalty to the Emperor and became instrumental in defending the city.

#### Economic Recovery
In 1424, as the Emperor's autocratic power had lost all public support, it was Aldworth who organized an economic reconstruction that was likened to miracle work. This hegemony eventually ended with the Arcane Crisis.

### Arthus Fellhorn
The Oldfield Empire rose from the ashes of the Interregnum. The first emperor, Barnabas Augustus Oldfield was a violent tyrant who inspired enough fear to end the war after he detonated huge parts of the Obsidian Palace. 

By his side was Arthus Fellhorn, a powerful wizard with extraordinary intellect. Fellhorn led the establishment of two this new empire's greatest pillars; education and the economy.

**Arthusian Economics.** One of Atroxia's most powerful innovations was fractional reserve banking, whereby banks would hold a fraction of their total value in the vaults and invest the rest in to grow the economy as a whole. This created a huge economic boom that funded the nation's massive expansion in the 1300s. 

By the 1400s  the instability started to show. It led to a bank run and depression during the Last War in 1422 and the recession in 1430 caused by the Sindruki trade war and a government deficit caused by increase in government bond payments and interest rates.

**Arthusian Education.** Later in life, Arthus turned his attention to the seven colleges in the city, determined to create a system whereby a new ruling class could be educated to understand and manipulate spellcasting, statesmanship, and economics. All things Arthus deemed to be the essential to the creation of a stable great power on the world stage. He funded an expansion of the colleges, adding four more in 1330, and this expansion was continued by his estate after his disappearance.

#### The Arch Lich  ·  Campaign Idea
Arthus Fellhorn vanished without a trace in 1339, his tower, the Spire Una (pg. 29) was found to be in a state of total disarray after a ritual that went awry. His apprentices sealed the tower and left it undisturbed to this day, in case he returns.

The failed ritual was Arthus's attempt to rebuild a demiplane as his phylactery when he ascended to lichdom. This failure shattered him across time and space, scattered into the weave itself.

Now, made mad by time itself, Arthus Fellhorn is a grand archlich and possibly the smartest creature in the entire multiverse.
He's collected hidden lore and artifacts from across time to aid him in his ultimate goal: to restructure the weave in his own image, with him as a god of all magic; the Sovereign of the Arcane.

And it's only matter of time when he'll begin...

### The Arcane Crisis of 1424
Nearly twenty years ago, in Monsoon of 1424, a series of violent events changed the empire forever.

#### Death of Gerald Russel
Atroxian Trades Guild CEO Gerald Russel was murdered in his prison cell by Horace Aldworth, who used magic to make this death appear as a suicide.

#### Celia Powell's Revolt
Parliament, with tremendous public support, had attempted to pass a fairly toothless Government Oversight bill which the Emperor vetoed. At the time, no method of passing law despite a veto existed, despite demands from parliament. 

Unionist Party Leader, Celia Powell, the First Chancellor and head of Parliament, threatened protests and on the 24th of Monsoon, 1424, huge crowds gathered outside the palace to protest. 

Proof of Murder. Private investigators found Powell and showed her proof of Russel's murder at the hands of Aldworth.
Emperor Flees. News spread about the Emperor having fled the city to Grand Chateau, a villa to the north of the city.

#### Charge on the Palace
Informed of Aldworth's murder, Celia Powell whipped the crowd into a fervor, and on her order the crowd charged the Obsidian Palace.
Being a well-defended fortress, the Palace immediately began shooting at the protestors. Both Aldworth and a wizard named Anthony Dupree, the Evoker Supreme, took to the skies and attacked the protestors with spells like Meteor Shower.

Despite overwhelming odds, the protestors managed to take the palace thanks to a sacrifice of military veteran Katila Helveg who was killed opening the gates for the crowd.

#### Manhunts and Reformation
Powell waged a war of pressure and blackmail against the Emperor, eventually passing the Monarchy Abolition Act, signed by the Emperor
With the power of the executive government in her hands, Powell ordered the manhunt of Aldworth and Dupree, who were imprisoned in the Blacksteel Raft for their crimes.

By 1430, the Reformation Act seperated Parliament from executive powers by creating the office of the President.

### Arthusian University
A majority of Parliament, the nation's CEOs, the executive branch, the Supreme Court, innovators, authors, explorers, and professors are all graduates from the Arthusian University. It's a prestigious institution, with some of its colleges nearing their one-thousandth birthday.

**History.** According to the Fellhorn Estate, the University was founded by Arthus Fellhorn in the 1330s to further Atroxian education.
But there are collages in the University that were operating independantly earlier, some even 900 years before before the 1330s.

**Institutions.** There's a number of instiutions shared between all the colleges, such as lecture halls, libraries, study halls, cafeterias, and the commons green. 

#### Colleges
Each of the eighteen colleges has bedrooms, dining areas and common rooms, to serve as student housing. There are also study halls for tutoring, workshops, and study groups.

**Original Set.** Before Arthus Fellhorn united the colleges into one institution, there were seven seperate colleges in this part of the city. Two of which, Baille and Merwin, claim to be the oldest.

**1330s.** In order to build this university and further legitimize the new empire, Arthus oversaw the construction of 4 new colleges named after the heroes of the Interregnum and the founders of the Oldfield Empire, including himself.

**Newer Colleges.** Since the 1330s, the university has continued to grow. A new college is built every few years. Today, there are eighteen in total.

**Provost.** Each college is lead by a Provost who oversees the college's curriculum, settles debates between students, and runs the administrative staff that tends to the college building.

#### Essential Studies
While there are countless philopsophies taught at the university, all first year students study the same essentials.
**History.** An overview of the world's major historical events, and how each leads into the next.
**Oratory.** The principles of public speaking and making convincing arguments.
**Geography.** Studying the seven realms and seas, and how those lands effected their people.
**Arcanlogy.**  An introduction to the arcane, why it works, and how one might begin to study it.

#### Thesis
In their fourth and final year, students write a thesis on a specific field within their philosophy. Usually exploring a highly specialized subject that could prove useful on the job market.

#### Tuition
Attending the university is ruinously expensive, except for the very wealthy. Depending on the college, students are paying between N800,000 and N20,000,000 for their tution.

#### Duels
University students have a reputation for night time duels to settle their disputes. This is done on a hill in the Scrapyard, outside the city walls. 
While death is an unlikely outcome in these duels, it happens more than anyone would like to admit.

#### Philosophies
A degree from the Arthusian University is called a Philosophy, and alumni are  referred to as Philosophers. These Philosophies are taught here:

- **Anthrobiology.** How the behavior of humanoids is explained by their biology as well as their cultural environments.
- **Applied Archeology.** The science of finding and exploring ancient ruins to acquire lost knowledge.
- **Arcane Philology.** Understanding the power of words, through spellcasting and speechwriting.
- **Astrocosmology.** The study of heavens and hells, their tangible and intangible effects on the mortal world.
- **Botanical Alchemy.** The innate properties of flora; and how it can be harvested for herbalism.
- **Civic Sociology.** The study of societies, how they're organized, controlled, and inspired.
- **Classical Antiquities.** Exploring the ancient world through text, relics, and stories.
- **Comparative Runography.** How the power of shapes and symbols affects the arcane weave.
- **Cultural Thaumatology.** Understand ancient societies by studying their systems of magic and spellcasting from an anthropological perspective.
- **Democratic Diplomacy.** How leaders project legitimacy through systems that reward fairness.
- **Divine Theology.** How real divinity is shaped by both worship and the worshippers.
- **Ethnolinguistics.** How languages and cultures shape and form each other.
- **Financial Economics.** The study of created ecosystems, such as finances. How they operate and how they're manipulated. 
- **Historical Geography.** World history meets international geography, and how the two affect one another. 
- **Jurisprudence.** The study of Atroxian Law, and the principles with which it should be applied.
- **Mathematical Engineering.** How one creates the machines that build a better tomorrow.
- **Metaphysics.** Space, time, change, matter, and the nature of existence. Where they come from, and if they can be altered.
- **Moral Psychology.** The inner workings of the sapient mind, how it works, and why it can break.
- **Occult Mythography.** The study of heretical myth, its origins and the very real dangers it can pose to the world.
- **Orethonian Literature.** Stories, and their effects on the world at large.
- **Physical Sciences.** The study of all natural philosophies, such as the sciences.
- **Quintessant Geology.** How the quintessent elemental planes create and shape of our world.
- **Statesmanship.** Leading nations in both peace and war times. Strategy, diplomacy, and speech.
- **Rhetorical Oratory.** The study of how debates change minds; or change worlds.
- **Theoretical Arcanology.** Exploring the far boundaries of the arcane through theory.
- **Xenobiology.** The study of non-humanoid biology, such as that of outsiders.

**Ticket Price.** A ticket can range from N10 for common seating, N100 for prime seats, and N1000 for the box seats.

### Boyle Estate
This manor belongs to Benedict Boyle, former CEO of the Fellhorn Estate, who was arrested for embezzlement following the Arcane Crisis of 1424. He spent 10 years imprisoned in the Raft.

When he returned home he founded the Marigold Casino in Downcastle, a front for a crime syndicate that deals in smuggled blackmarket arcane goods.

#### Artefact Request  ·  Adventure Hook
Benedict Boyle is trying to source a magic item, a Tome of Clear Thought that was used just under a century ago. He heard of a rumor that a woman named Azeeza Labibi, the owner of another mansion in this borough, has the item on display in her ballroom.
**Reward.** In return for stealing the item and giving it to Boyle, he offers the party their choice of an uncommon item per player character and N50,000.

### Bureau of Mage Enforcement
The Secretary for the Arcane's law enforcement department is called the Bureau of Mage Enforcement. They are meant to arrest unregulated spellcasters. 

**Staff.** Agents and Special Agents equipped with gadgets that counter magic and spellcasters. They're lead by a small core of Commanders.

**History.** The Bureau was founded as the "Mage Hunter Guild" during the 1300s by the Fellhorn Estate to help regulate magic. Following the Arcane Crisis, they were folded into the government for increased oversight.

**Leadwaves.** Mage Hunters are famous for their various Leadwave Devices (pg. 63), a horseshoe-shaped gadget that counters spellcasting.

#### Rogue Spellcaster  ·  Combat Encounter
When arriving at the Bureau's headquarters, the party discovers that an arrested Rogue Spellcaster has just broken free of their bonds and killed four Mage Hunter Agents. A Mage Hunter Special Agent on the scene calls for help.
**Reward.** Special Agent Sampson Collins thanks the characters for their help and hands them a Leadwave Device and Moonflare as thanks for their help.

### Cerebral Ease
Cerebral Ease is a world-class hotel. The high ceilings, mahogany furniture, and a seemingly endless supply of tapestries make it feel like staying in a palace.

Each bedroom is outfitted with bookcases stocked full of old tomes and the newest paperbacks in equal measure.

You could book a room full of arcane tomes, historical literature, or modern fiction. Any subject, no matter how niche, is catered to.

**Prices.** Booking a room costs N500 a night, food and drinks are included in the price.

### Cricket Market
While this market is open every day of the week, it triples in size on game day at Badger Stadium as new stalls open selling candy, deli meat, and ale. You can buy most typical adventuring gear here if you know where to look.

### Empire Park
Arthus Fellhorn built this park to legitimize the new empire back in the 1300s, complete with an arch in the middle to depict the empires as equals.

**Arch of Duality.** On one end of the arch, elven nobility fights with swords, bows, and arrows. On the other, human fusiliers fire guns, backed by cannon artillery. The sides are depicted as fighting back-to-back against an outside force.
**Park Picnics.** It's not uncommon for residents of nearby houses to come here on their days off to have a picnic, if the weather permits.

### Fellhorn Estate
In the center of the district is a large castle, built on the orders of Arthus Fellhorn to oversee the empire he was building alongside the new emperor. Upon his sudden and mysterious disappearance, his apprentices and colleagues inherited the castle along with all of his property. They formed a legal estate, named after their old master and swore to uphold his ideals.

#### Corporate Headquarters 
This large four-story structure hosts offices for an army of administrators and their pools of assistants and typists.

**Town Hall.** Local matters that would ordinarily be taken to a city council are instead redirected here, where Fellhorn Estate associates attempt to resolve matters in a manner suitable for the corporation.

#### The Vault 
Both gold and artifacts acquired by the Fellhorn Estate are stored in this lead-lined structure. Inside,  a dozen cast-iron vaults house the emergency wealth of the Estate.

**Preparing for the Worst.** Since Arthusian Economics are inherently less stable than gold reserves, the Estate is always concerned with market stability. These gold-storing vaults are meant to secure the corporation if the economy collapses.

#### Ecclesia Arcana 
This tall library and study hall is the private sanctum of the Fellhorn Estate's itinerant corps of wizards and economists. Members of the Spire Decima and Venerable Order frequently come here to discuss matters with the estate. If the rumors are to be believed, these matters include corporate espionage, manipulating government officials, and complex schemes abroad.

#### Executive Residence 
This building is the official home and residence of the Fellhorn Estate's current CEO, Caspian Pemberton-Smythe.

#### The Spire Una  ·  Adventure Idea
This spire is the personal study of Arthus Fellhorn, now sealed shut by the Estate. Rumors persist that the inside of the tower is a maze of demiplanes, hidden research, and foul rituals.

### Ferryman Union
These buildings are part of the Ferryman Union, an organization of riverboat drivers that manage pedestrian travel up and down the river.
Booking a Boatride. A riverboat can be hired to take you to any borough on the river for N12 per borough crossed, including the starting borough.

### Financial Report
The Financial Report is the nation's foremost financial newspaper. It's read by anyone who intends to invest in the stock market to keep them up to date with the following segments:

**Investigative Journalism.** The very top of the broadsheet is reserved for the work of journalists uncovering possible upcoming scandals. 
**Corporate Announcements.** A bulk of the paper is dedicated to corporate announcements intended to work as advertisements for the stock.
**Market Movements.** At the back of the paper is a big spreadsheet listing how every individual stock moved yesterday.

#### Dangerous Journalism  ·  Combat Encounter
While investigating a case about Llewellyn Rowe's supposed occultism (See Issue #1), a journalist named Isabel Luker is accosted by jackboots (See Issue #1) trying to intimidate her to give up on the case. Only today she drew a gun on them, so they're attacking to kill. 
Reward. In return for saving her life, Isabel Luker offers to tell the characters about the case she's working on. She'll reveal that there are peristent rumors surrounding Rowe, but no proof.

### First Bank of Blackguard
The First Bank of Blackguard is the premier institute for banking in the Atroxian Republic. It offers large scale loans, secure vaults and safe deposit boxes, and bank cheques.

**Cheques.** Any cheque issued by an account holder at a Fellhorn Estate bank can be redeemed by any other Fellhorn Estate bank, allowing for unrestricted movement of capital.

#### Who Is Hargrave?  ·  Adventure Hook
If the party read the letter (pg. 13) from Cardinal Hargrave about Llewellyn Rowe in Bank Holiday, they might be interested in tracking him down to ask him further questions about Rowe.

Upon finding his address, the party finds it smelling of rot and decay. Inside they find Hargrave's dead body, killed by jackboots a few weeks ago as part of the same cover-up.

### Five Stars
This world-class restaurant is where the corporate elite celebrate major victories.

**Prices.** A table here costs N5,000 per person, which includes a gourmet meal and rare aged wine.

### Fort Merwin
This fortress was built on this site a thousand years ago, long before the modern Blackguard came to exist, in the year 412. 

Fort Merwin was a stronghold of an elven sorcerer that used the site as his home, study, and residence. He advice the Nathaniel Dynasty on various matters of state.

Today, it's property of the Fellhorn Estate and not open to visitors.

#### Castle Delve  ·  Adventure Idea
A well-connected member of the criminal underworld named Alden Barnett hires the party to keep him safe as he explores the keep searching for a Potion of Longevity for a wealthy client.

**Reward.** Alden offers the party to keep any loot they find, as long as he gets the Potion.

### Interregnum Memorial Park
This park was built in 1370s to commemorate the sides that fought in the Interregnum. A statue represents the leader of each faction when the conflict started: two elves, three humans, and a dwarf.

### Oswald Paine Library
This library is named after Arthus Fellhorn's greatest disciple and the founder of the Fellhorn Estate, Oswald Paine. Something that caught heat in the aftermath of the Last War, when the nation learned that Oswald Paine had become a lich working for the enemy. As with everything else, the Fellhorn Estate refused to change it.

This six story library, with its three wings, is a colossal maze of books from every nation in the mundane world, and nations beyond the veil.

#### Book Delve  ·  Adventure Hook
Catacombs underneath the Argent Sector's Church of St. Arthur are said to hold a book named The Complete Journal of Saevil Nathaniel which promises to divulge the former Empress's masterful games of espionage.

**Reward.** In return for the book, the library is willing to pay a hefty sum of N50,000.

### Rooftop Suite
This lovely bar is a favorite of bankers and university students alike. Wealthy people from every business sector come here to discuss economics, politics, and, most controversially, cricket.

The entire bar is identifiable by how upper-class it is. There's no house ale or mead, only branded and imported drinks at inflated prices. The entertainment is always a live performance, and the doormen won't let anyone inside unless they're dressed to mingle with the corporate aristocracy.

### Scrollery
This large store owned by the Fellhorn Estate has all the ingredients one would require to scribe scrolls, cast any spell, or create magic items.
The proprietor is an elf named Mytheye who claims to have lived for a thousand years, despite a youthful complexion.

**Information Collection.** When Mytheye recieves suspicious orders, finds strange characters practicing the occult, or notices anything out of the ordinary, he reports it to the Fellhorn Estate.

The Scrollery's large selection and reasonable prices are designed to appeal to as wide a customer base as possible, to make this information collection as efficient as it can be.

### Spellcaster Registry 
All spellcasters within Atroxia are required to be registered with the Spellcaster Registry Service, a branch of the executive branch's Ministry of the Arcane, as overseen by Secretary Hawley.

**Registering as a Spellcaster.** Since spellcasters of all skill levels immigrate to Atroxia, and Atroxian Residents have been known to suddenly and quickly become very adept at the arcane, the registration process is very open.

Officials are trained not to judge anyone's magical origins, or possible tardiness with registration.

Registrated spellcasters are asked to report in regularly, and update the SRS on their skill levels, such as what spells they know, what school of magic they align themselves with if any, and especially what circle (spell level) of spellcasting they're capable of casting.

The residents are asked about their name address or current residence, as well as being weighed, measured, and, most invasively, a lock of their hair is taken to be used as a component for spells to spy on or teleport to the caster.

**Vaults of Documents.** Registrations are stored in dossiers sorted alphabetically in this building's massive complex of records rooms.

Access to those lead-lined vaults is sealed away with powerful warding magic, like Arcane Lock, Glyphs of Warding, and various other wards forbidding planar travel and teleportation.

**Information Requests.** Information on spellcasters is frequently requested by various branches of the Arcane Ministry's many branches, such as the Bureau of Mage Enforcement, the Office of Arcane Affairs, or even by local law enforcement.

The SRS can deny information requests if they deem them to be made in bad faith, but in actuality that rarely, if ever, happens.

### Spire Decima
Ten towers line the riverbank here on Bank Street proper. These tall spires belong to the ten greatest wizards in the Venerable Order, positions that usually pass from master to apprentice.

Each tower is both the private residence of a master spellcaster, and a research hall where apprentices capable of powerful spellcasting work on creating new spells, researching the unknown, and furthering the Spire's goals.

The Spire and the Venerable Order both intend to further the standing of spellcasters in a nation that has turned its back on them.

**Erudite Archmage's Tower.** Upon the death or disappearance of the Erudite Archmage, a majority of the 9 supremes must elect a new Archmage. The Archmage represents the Spire's business to the public, by liasing with instutitions. And in times of war: protecting the nation.
As an act of protest, the Spire hasn't appointed a new Archmage since Aldworth was arrested.

- **Tower of Abjuration.** Gregory Hick is the Abjurer Supreme, he's incapable of 9th circle spells.
- **Tower of Conjuration.** Melanie Hartfield is the Conjurer Supreme, capable of summoning armies.
- **Tower of Divination.** The dwarf oracle Durzum Bick is the Diviner Supreme.
- **Tower of Enchantment.** The Enchanter Supreme is Derek Hawley, who is also the Secretary for the Arcane in the President's Cabinet.
- Hawley is known for being paranoid, perpetually casting spells like Detect Magic and Detect Thoughts to read the minds of everyone he speaks to, fearing they may be double-agents or doppelgangers.
- **Tower of Evocation.** The Evoker Supreme is Anthony Dupree, who's imprisoned in the Raft.
- **Tower of Illusion.** Famous wanderer Priscilla Vansant is the Transmuter Supreme.
- **Tower of Necromancy.** The Necromancer Supreme is Oswald Paine's son, Asahel Paine.
- **Tower of Transmutation.** Cathaniael Widowheart is the Transmuter Supreme.
- **Tower of Universalism.** Dunnahan Coldheart is the Universalist Supreme, a position that Arthus Fellhorn held when he was still alive.

### Stock Market
Entering this large building is a dizzying experience of shouting and loud conversations. Brokers attempt to game the market by buying and selling stock based on a rapidly evolving market.

### Venerable Order
The Venerable Order is an invite-only, exclusive members club for spellcasters. Their experience levels range from beginner to expert, but what they share in common is a thirst for knowledge.

**Membership.** An annual membership costs N100,000, which is used to pay for upkeep on all of the Order's facilities.
**Facilities.** On the grounds are libraries, study halls, bars, restaurants, and plenty of private studies that members can use as they please.
**Visitors.** A member is permitted to bring visitors to the club whenever they want.
**Conscription.** In times of war, the Order has been conscripted to help defend the city.

#### Occultism in Atroxia  ·  Campaign Idea
A member of the Order, named Charles Seraphim asks for help with retrieving items related to a field he's studying: the Forgotten Gods.
Use this campaign structure as a vehicle to introduce the party to the occult that hides just beneath the surface in Blackguard.

**Book 1: Strange Cults.** A strange rumor sends the party to a forgotten catacomb under the city, where the grave of a cultist is hidden.

**Book 2: Oligarchical Occultism.** While working alongside Seraphim, the party finds themselves facing off against cults enacting human sacrifice to the Forgotten Gods.

**Book 3: Shapechangers Among Us.** The presence of skinwalkers and doppelgangers in the nation's most influencial positions reveals a dark truth. The cult is much more influential than anyone had dared to believe.

**Book 4: Against the Gods.** A Forgotten God manifests in the city, leveling entire neighborhoods. It's up to the party to stop the massacre.
