# **MID-CAMPAIGN ARC (Levels 4–9)**

### **Theme:**

*The world under their feet is moving. Goblinoids are abducting spellcasters, obsidian is fueling strange rituals, and old horrors remember things better left forgotten.*

---

# **ARC 1 — The Abductions Begin (Lv 4–6)**

### *Hook:*

Arcane users in and around Phandalin vanish. It’s subtle at first—an apprentice, then a hedge witch, then a well-known town wizard.

**Clue Trail:**

* Witnesses report *goblinoids with green-painted hands* escorting hooded figures into the mountains at night.
* Strange, glassy shards (obsidian) are found at the abduction sites—charged with psionic “echoes.”
* Goblinoid shamans show early signs of being **psionically warped** by contact with the shards.

This is your connective tissue: the goblins are not masterminds. They’re errand boys corrupted by the lieutenant mind flayer holding the Hand of Vecna.

### **Key Adventure Beats:**

1. **Interrogating the Green Hand Clan**
   They’ve been ordered (psychically compelled, even if they don’t understand it) to deliver “magic-minds” to tunnels beneath a sealed mine.

2. **Dungeon: The Black Maw Crags**
   A surface-level mini-dungeon where goblinoids are stockpiling obsidian shards.

   * Players encounter psionically warped goblin casters.
   * They find a crude map leading downward—clearly not made by goblins.
   * There’s an obsidian “relay” stone the Aboleth can *whisper* through.

3. **The First Hint of the Rogue Illithid**
   A psychic “ping” interrupts the whispering:

   * Players get a short, confusing transmission: *“Stop. Not them. Wrong path. Obsidian lies.”*
   * It’s the rogue illithid trying (badly) to help.

### **Payoff:**

* Players understand something *intelligent* is guiding the goblins.
* They know the missing mages are alive and “below.”
* They gain the first real hint of the obsidian–Aboleth connection.

---

# **ARC 2 — The Underdark Opens (Lv 6–8)**

### *Hook:*

The goblin tunnels lead to true Underdark passages—NOT a giant D&D Underdark, but a local, grounded network.

### **Factions in Play:**

1. **The Rogue Illithid**

   * Hiding in a forgotten pocket-space.
   * Paranoid, half-starved, desperate.
   * Knows about the shards, their purpose, and the danger of the lieutenant with the Hand of Vecna.
   * Won’t trust easily; may test the players mentally or ethically.

2. **The Deep Dwarves (Duergar or your variant)**

   * Refugees—displaced from their ancestral hold by **an emerald wyrm** (tied to the obsidian? or simply opportunistic).
   * One of them is being *slowly enthralled* by a sinister illithid lieutenant.
   * They mistake the players for agents of the goblins at first.

3. **Illithid Enslavers (the two “bad ones”)**

   * One controls the goblins.
   * The other is experimenting on dwarves and captured spellcasters.
   * Both are building prototypes of the “obsidian machine” your endgame requires.

### **Adventure Beats:**

1. **Dwarven Refuge Cavern**
   A desperately fortified, half-collapsed hold.

   * Players must negotiate trust.
   * They learn the deep dwarves saw goblins delivering captives deeper into the tunnels.
   * They reveal *something older* sealed behind obsidian doors.

2. **Dungeon: The Psion-Loom Laboratory**
   This is your mid-campaign setpiece.
   A semi-organic mix of ancient machinery and mind flayer bioconstructs.

   * Captured mages are suspended in obsidian cocoons that amplify psionic energy to build a “machine primer.”
   * Players can rescue some (not all).
   * Encounters show early prototypes of obsidian conduits, including one that the Aboleth attempts to “reach through.”

3. **First Face-to-Tentacle with the Rogue Illithid**
   The rogue watches from afar, then intervenes by disabling one of the machinery nodes that harms the party.
   When confronted:

   * It explains the shards are *dangerous conduits* and must not be used.
   * It reveals the existence of its brethren and the rivalry among them.
   * It guides the players toward the sealed ruin if trust is earned.

### **Optional Side Plot:**

The wyrm that displaced the dwarves is either:

* guarding another obsidian shard,
* or unwittingly corrupted by its influence.
  This makes a great level 7 or 8 mini-arc.

### **Payoff:**

* The players gain a reluctant, morally gray ally.
* They understand the stakes: the illithids are preparing something massive.
* They now know where to go next: **the sealed ancient ruin.**

# **ARC 3 — The Forgotten Aboleth Temple (Lv 8–9)**

### *Hook:*

The deep dwarves reveal the sealed ruin they fled from—a forgotten temple built during the first war between Aboleths and the earliest illithids.

### **Dungeon: The Drowned Memory Sanctum**

Tone: ancient, oppressive, full of psionic echoes and stone that feels “alive.”

Rooms should include:

* **Memory Pools**
  Liquid surfaces storing visions:

  * Show glimpses of the ancient time loop.
  * Reveal the creation of the Aboleth by future illithids.
  * Show early obsidian conduit technology.

* **Obsidian Conduit Spines**
  Ancient machinery partly turned off, partly corrupted.

  * Players can disable one (weakening the endgame villains).
  * Or leave it intact and suffer consequences later.

* **Lore Tablets**
  Reveal:

  * why the Aboleth hates them,
  * how psionic loops work,
  * and why obsidian serves as a bridge between minds.

* **The Rogue Illithid’s Secret**
  This ruin is where it severed itself from the hive.
  There’s a psionic imprint chamber letting the players see that event.

### **Final Encounter of Mid-Act**

A guardian—either:

* A psionically mutated beast made from early illithid experiments
  **or**
* A shard-touched aberration created by the Aboleth through time echoes.

When defeated:

### **Major Payoff:**

* Players unlock the truth about the cycle.
* They gain clear, actionable knowledge for the final arc:
  **the villains are gathering obsidian shards to rebuild the ancient memory-engine that once birthed the Aboleth.**
* They gain access to a dwarven forge pattern for working **mithril as psychic armor** (huge payoff).
* They discover the “map” showing locations of the remaining shards—setting up the final race.
