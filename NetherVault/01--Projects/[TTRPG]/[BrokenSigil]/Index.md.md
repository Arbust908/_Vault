## Conocimiento Prohibido / Forbidden Knowledge

## Why it works

Every other skill has a starting value derived from attributes, or at least a base percentage. This one doesn't. That asymmetry is the hook — players _will_ ask, and the ones who ask loudest are exactly the ones who should be rewarded first.

The answer the GM gives in-world should be unsatisfying on purpose: _"Nobody knows that. Move on."_

---

## How to Earn It (GM Eyes Only)

Points in Forbidden Knowledge should never come from study or training. They come from **witnessing things that shouldn't exist:**

- Surviving direct contact with one of the old beings
- Reading a text that predates human civilization
- Successfully interpreting a dream sent by something vast
- Watching someone else's mind break from the truth and _not breaking yourself_

Each point earned should cost something — a failed Sanity check, a permanent memory, a detail the character can never un-know.

---

This is where you can go two directions:

**Option A — Pure Revelation:** Forbidden Knowledge is passive. Higher values mean the GM feeds that player more true information about what's really happening. The hybrid is strongest — passive revelation up to a threshold, then occasional secret rolls past it.
