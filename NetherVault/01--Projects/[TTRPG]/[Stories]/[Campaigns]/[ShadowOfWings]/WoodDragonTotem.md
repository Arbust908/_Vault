
**The core concept — Dragon Stances:**

The carved dragon physically shifts between positions, each radiating a different aura that affects the whole party. Switching stances could be a bonus action for the carrier, with maybe one or two activations per rest so choices matter.

---

**Possible Stances:**

**Wings Folded — Resting/Dormant** No aura. The totem is just wood. This is how it looks to outsiders, which is why it appears mundane.

**Wings Raised to Shield — Aegis** Protective aura. Could grant resistance, reduce incoming damage, give saving throw bonuses. _"The dragon shelters you under its wings."_

**Wings Spread Wide — Presence** Inspiring or intimidating aura. Advantage on charisma checks, enemies are unsettled, allies feel emboldened. The dragon _declares_ itself.

**Wings Back, Neck Extended — Warding** Detection or awareness. Party can't be surprised, sense nearby dragons or cult activity, see through illusions. The dragon _watches_.

**Wings Swept Forward, Claws Out — Fury** Offensive aura. Bonus damage, advantage on attacks, reckless power. The dragon _strikes_.
