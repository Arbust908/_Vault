# **Scales of Dominion**

> Based on Tyranny of Dragons
Tyranny of Dragons is a two-part adventure (originally published as _Hoard of the Dragon Queen_ and _The Rise of Tiamat_) that was released alongside D&D 5e's launch in 2014. A revised single-volume edition came out in 2023.
**The premise** is classic high stakes: the Cult of the Dragon is working to free Tiamat, the five-headed chromatic dragon goddess, from the Nine Hells. Players spend the first half disrupting the Cult's operations and the second half trying to build a political coalition to stop the ritual before it completes.

---

## **ACT ONE — SHADOW OVERHEAD

_Establishes the threat, the characters, and the stakes._

- **Chapter 1: The Wrong Place** — The characters are traveling or pursuing their own dragon-related purpose when the Cult attacks a town/keep. Baptism by fire. They survive, they help, and their relic draws attention.
- **Chapter 2: Ashes and Questions** — Investigation of the aftermath. Who were the Cult operatives? What were they looking for? First hints of a larger operation and the ritual.
- **Chapter 3: Into the Den** — Infiltrate or assault a Cult stronghold to retrieve a person or item of importance. First real taste of Cult organization and a captive or young dragon encounter.
- **Chapter 4: The Council of Lords** — The three lords convene, quietly organized by an unseen hand. Players present what they know. The ten ritual items are identified. The campaign's shape is revealed.

---

## **ACT TWO — THE HUNT OF RELICS**

_The core gameplay loop. Each chapter is a relic mission that doubles as a faction story._

- **Chapter 5: The First Hunt** — Players choose their first target. Establishes the pattern: find the relic, navigate the faction tied to it, decide its fate.
- **Chapter 6: The Cult Responds** — After the first success the Cult escalates. A relic the players ignored is now secured. A location is lost. The clock is real.
- **Chapter 7–9: The Middle Hunts** _(three chapters, each a distinct region, dragon type, and faction)_ — The world expands. Alliances form or fracture. The good dragon begins appearing more actively, offering guidance and requesting items for safekeeping.
- **Chapter 10: The Patron Revealed** — The good dragon's identity and role as council organizer is uncovered. A moment of trust and gratitude — and the last time players feel safe giving them anything.
- **Chapter 11: The Final Hunts** — The remaining accessible relics. Harder, more costly. Players now know not every item can be saved. Choices feel final.

---

## **ACT THREE — THE LAST ALLIANCE**

_Political payoff, the betrayal, and the final battle._

- **Chapter 12: The Dragon's Hunger** — If players have been giving items to the patron, the corruption manifests. The dragon becomes an aspect of Tiamat. A powerful ally becomes the most personal threat of the campaign.
- **Chapter 13: The War Council** — Alliances are tallied. Factions who received relics commit forces. Factions who were ignored are neutral or hostile. Players see the direct consequence of every Act Two decision.
- **Chapter 14: The March on the Ritual** — The coalition moves. A large-scale battle where players are not just fighters but commanders. Cult forces, corrupted dragons, and chaos.
- **Chapter 15: The Embodiment** — The ritual is incomplete but Tiamat partially manifests. Her power reflects exactly which relics were lost or destroyed. The final confrontation is shaped entirely by what the players did — or didn't do.
