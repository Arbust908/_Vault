## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

### Session Description
A series of coincidences, let's call it fate, has pulled half-a-dozen people of all walks of life into a strange mission to find their way into Roachport, Logger's County. #### The Crossed Swords Inn
The Crossed Swords Inn is a small inn, and the only one here in Crosskeys, built on Regentsway's side of the Brooksway, though there's a small pub called Lowe's on the other side of the river; which is populated by more locals.

Stepping inside the tavern is like stepping into the past; its equal parts a saloon and an old world tavern, and it's left unattended much of the year. Though occasionally, on ferry-days, the inn becomes a hot spot for people looking to cross the river or take the ferry to Roachport.

The barman, Matthew Benitez, will gladly sell them a bedding for N3 in the cots on the second story; and he'll happily tell patrons about life in Crosskeys.

* ...like how they have a ferry, but its only operated once a week or so.
* ...like how last month, an attack out of the forest left several houses burned.
* ...like how the [[Anarchist Hillfolk]] were responsible for the attack, and they've carried out many attacks like this.
* ...like how, as a result, the local people are armed and prepared to defend their property if it comes down to it.

> "Ain't nobody out here who ain't choosing to be out here."

Benitez says there are only four kinds of settlers in Loggers County, and that they make up the cultural identity of the region, they are:

* Dreamers looking to make a better life for themselves or others.
* Fugitives from the law or other organization, hiding away from the civilized world.
* Explorers hoping to unearth new wonders within Areston itself.

#### The Eight Groups of Settlers
Maxley, above anything else, wants to ensure a large entourage, and eventually build up a  mission to find the eight groups of settlers that have left since the [[1366 Manifest of Industrialization]]. He considers those groups brave explorers, frontiersmen and women who put their life on the line to explore the world.

Since the 1380s, as a part of County Commissioner Angus Roach's plan to incorporate the county, eight groups of settlers left their hamlets to find new land to settle. These groups have not been heard from since.

He doesn't know where they are, if they're still alive, if something found them, or if they've merely failed to make contact again. He want to find them, and incorporate them. Give them a chance to return home, find Atroxia again, and maybe make their lives a little easier.

#### Other Maxley Knowledge
Maxley knows about a ship called **the Hermes** that crashed in the bay. He knows about the Hillfolk, the Skullswords, Bloodstone, and Palefist tribes; and the plague of lizardfolk in the forests. He knows many of these tribes are violent, some act like gangs, most of them worship foul creatures. Though he admits not having had many run-ins.

He might also be able to relay a few things about the [[History of Loggers County]].
