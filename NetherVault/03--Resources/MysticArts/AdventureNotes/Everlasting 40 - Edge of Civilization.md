## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

With the Battle of the Crossed Swords Inn shaking the people of Crosskeys to their core, one thing's evident, this town won't be safe until they start punching back. ## The Aftermath of the Battle
The Battle of the Crossed Swords Inn was a fierce, if small, skirmish that took place on the 11th of Springsdawn, 1450. Where a Tanarukk summoned a Hell Hound, the hound was killed by Dee's Wand of Magic Missles, whereas the Tanarukk was killed by Maeve on Church's orders.

#### Aftermath Scenes
It's important to allow the aftermath of last session's combat to land appropriately, it'll be my job to initiate those scenes, so here are a few of them, the rest will be up to the characters to do.

* Rapids asking CC if she saw anything inside the house, if she saw where the katana wielding man was going.

#### Maxley's Suggestion
Maxley thinks they've got a once-in-a-decade opportunity to deal with these Anarchist attacks, if they split their large party in two, a group could stay behind to protect the town while another group goes off to attack the Horned One and his particular cell's camp.

They don't know where he is, but they might be able to track him down to his camp and break this cell before another attack is mounted, one that might lay the town low.

#### A Night at the Inn
It's highly doubtful that this night ends without several conversations playing out as the campaigners sort out their questions for one another in various small scenes. It might also continue in the Inn as the group is in there, or as they split off for duo scenes.

Let's just let all that happen, it's okay as long as there's a goal looming. That's what tonight is about.

#### The Splits
So, why is Maxley worried about an attack here in the interim? Because without that concern, the players will take their party of sidekicks with them! So, let's make sure they split up the party. Ideally; NPCs stay behind, PCs go on the adventure.

Here's some ways that might need to manifest:
* The Bridgemen think it's important they stay behind to lead the defense.
* Talia and Brooke have been talking how to better prepare for an assault. They're thinking they could help create a highly defensible point from which they can shoot.
* Dee should want to stay behind in-case there's legal trouble. There's no guarantee that members of some Bureau won't come here and cause trouble while they're away.
* Rapid is toughest to justify, ideally Emily asks her to stay behind. But failing that, she could say she's worried about them and conflicted.

**Muffin** could be taken along, but her Euphoric Breath is now once per long rest. However, **Sir Rotheric Maxley** should want to come along, just so that there's one NPC with the party.
