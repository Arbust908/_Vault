## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

### Session Description
After their first night in the Elmwood, the campaigners find themselves in a foreign land making their way through the forest one league at a time. Herein, they're likely to find tempting landmarks, clues to other areas of the forest, and treasures hidden within... or they'll decide to keep their wits about them and head straight towards the anarchist's camp, with no time to waste. That's up to them.

## The Old Windmill
There's an old windmill, it's occupied by a small family of goblins who guard a troll in return for food. There's also an otyugh who guards their sewage. There's treasure here. I don't know more about it.

Sir Rotheric remembers something about [[Angus Roach]] talking about a windmill that was settled out here, but when it was attacked by monsters for a week straight, the farmers (Braunstone) ran for their lives before settling down in Crosskeys.

- The outside of the Windmill is occupied by **five goblins**, and one **bugbear**.
- There's a headless body of a goblin that was taken by the **Bredbettle**.
- There's a huge pile of sewage out back, which is being watched over by an **Otyugh**
- In the windmill, there's a **big troll**, and **limbjumbles**!
- There's a small chest with some jewelry, gold, and an old brown bess (jammer)

## Horned One's Camp
There's a camp here, it's not a dungeon, but an open camp that can be separated into a handful of encounters if done cleverly, or a massive battle.

* There's two separate **patrols** of two anarchists that circle the camp.
* There's a pair of watchtowers, each occupied by a pair of **anarchists**.
* There's a large structure where the **Horned One** makes camp with his two lieutenants.
	* There's a musket wielding lieutenant.
	* There's a big fat thug lieutenant.
	* The Horned One is an unarmored **knight** can topple with his sword, and 40ft speed. 
* There's a map to the Utopian Hollow, and the Duergar's Outpost.
