## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

#### Four Watches
As is tradition, they'll have to set up watches and in all likelihood, they'll want to talk during their watches. Let them.

* On 1st watch, they might spot an anarchist scout who ducks back into the trees.
* On 2nd watch, they might notice tremors, an Ankheg burying underneath.
* On 3rd watch, they'll hear rustling by the tree line from an Ankheg devouring the bodies.
* On 4th watch, nothing of note happens.

On CC's watch, she might receive [[Reward Cards for Everlasting#*(CC)* Level 3 Apprentice Spellcaster|her reward card]] if she borrows Altan's spellbook. Seraph will upon getting his gun back, get his [[Reward Cards for Everlasting#*(Seraph)* Level 3 Southern Gunslinger|his reward card]].

#### Come Morning
In the morning, they'll spot the pile of bodies having been taken and the earth around it all dug up and several tunnels leading away into a honeycomb-like maze of ankheg tunnels, leading back to its lair some leagues of underground travel away.

It may occur to Altan that an Ankheg's acidic glands can be used to create a foundation for a self-regenerating vial of acid creation. If he mentions this to Maeve, she could receive [[Reward Cards for Everlasting#*(Maeve)* Level 3 Serendipity's Call|her reward card.]]

Crucially, it's already long gone, but bodies seem to attract it.

#### Dead Elf (Actually Dwarf)
Dotted around this hex, spread about in a pattern that evokes an image of a skirmish or ambush of some kind, are bodies. It looks like a collection of elven skeletons, and dotted about are wooden arrows made of elm, barbed and with golden feathers. Easily recognizable as elven craftsmanship. They only see elven skeletal bodies, not what they were fighting, but the elven skeletons are often broken, cracked, or splintered.

Spending four hours searching this hex will yield the following discovery:

> Then you spot it, a different kind of arrow imbedded in a tree, or a bolt rather; a crossbow bolt made of steel through and through, with stark black feathers. 
> 
> Tracing the bolts line of fire to its origin point, you see another skeleton, but this one is short and stocky, with a denser bone-structure. Killed by what appear to be an abundance of elven arrows.
> 
> You see he's wearing a helmet that doesn't look deteriorated even slightly, and he's got a bolt loaded in his rotting crossbow; a bolt in pristine condition.

It's wearing *Helm of the Ambusher* and has knocked a single *Bolt of Elf Slaying*, and a *Scroll of Tongues*. The dwarf's heraldry indicates a white dwarven helmet on blue.

#### The Old Windmill
This Old Windmill used to belong to Horatio Rowan and Aaron Braunstone, but after suffering monster attacks every night for a week straight, they moved to Crosskeys. Sir Rotheric remembers that story.

Now, it's been taken over by an eclectic mix of monsters who form a delicate ecosystem together.

###### Outside the Windmill
The exterior of the windmill is home to five **goblins** and a **bugbear** named *Stodak*. (Deep, growly, thick and southern) They guard the exterior of the windmill, and feed the Windmill King to keep it sated. They will not let the campaigners inside the tower because they fear that the Windmill King will kill them if its forced to meet these characters.

Last night, one goblin was killed and beheaded by a monster called the Bredbettle. The headless body was burned on a pyre, whereas the head is still missing. The pyre collapsed and they didn't have enough firewood to fix it, so the body wasn't entirely burned but a headless charred corpse remains.

In addition to their weapons, these goblins have collected a number of old coins that were scattered across this area in ages past:

* 28 gold crowns
* 191 silver shillings
* 612 copper pennies

They have various kings and queens minted on them, some from the Interregnum, or the Rebellions, a few have King Arthur minted on them. One gold crown is minted with Queen Rathia Nathaniel; and is worth N3000 to a collector.

###### The Sewage
There's a pile of sewage a bit away from the tower, this pile is 30 feet in diameter, and inside of it there hides an **Otyugh** reveling in the trash. It's a simple beast and its territorial. In the pile of sewage however, there are a few goodies:

* A rotting elven body, with studded leather, a +1 longbow and a quiver of arrows.
* A skeletal body of a lizardman, wearing an elaborate golden headpiece and necklace worth N20.000.
* A hobgoblin carrying 3 scrolls (witch bolt, false life, ray of enfeeblement, fireball) and 3 healing potions.

###### Level 1: Windmill Interior - Living Room
This is a living interior that used to belong to godfather and godson Horatio Rowan and Aaron Braunstone. It looks like it was left in a hurry. There's a number of things of interest here:

* A child's charcoal drawing nailed to a wall of a kid (me) and an adult ("Uncle H.")
* A pair of repair kits (healing potions for warforged)
* A box of ammunition (12 balls and powder)
* A raided food cupboard (3 rations)

###### Level 2: Windmill Interior - Troll Lair
Here's a throne of bones and a nest of hey in a room of snot and gunk. Inside there is a big **troll** who calls itself the Windmill King. He speaks broken common and will offer the characters to give him an offering of bones, gold, or food, to remain alive.

If angred, the troll attacks and so does its **limbjumbles** scattered across the room.

###### Level 3: Windmill Interior - Top of the Tower
Up here, most of the floor has caved in, but there's an old Blunderbuss (busted) and a small lockbox containing the following:

* A pair of old wedding bands (N10.000 each)
* Sixteen Waterdhavian gold dragons, and eighteen Mighthall gold crowns.
* A silver locket broken by a bullet inside of which there's carved the letters; "Return to me." (N35.000 valuation)

From here, you can also barely make out an old stone watchtower three leagues southeast.


## Return to Crosskeys
*16th of Harvestfall when they return at 2 in the afternoon.*

After they've completed the quest and returned to town the campaigners have an opportunity to familiarize themselves with [[Crosskeys]], and set in motion their own personal quests. The goal of this section is to achieve the following:

* Provide introduction to the second part of [[Excursion Resting]]. We've done an excursion, now let's figure out how the resting downtime feels.
* Tutorialize how progress in town is made by introducing NPCs with ideas of reform and improvement and tasking the characters with providing resources.
* Introduce new NPCs to Crosskeys to render the town in more detail.

#### *(Altan)* On Diabolism
Dee says she's been talking with the Sheriff, and he knows a guy who's a researcher. He may have books on diabolism that they never got to read. Dee shares the address of Braxton Knutsford, an old gentleman who's a book collector and heading to Roachport. 

He's doing research here into the possibility of extraplanar connections in Loggers County. He has a copy of Cole's Diabolism Essentials, by Miriam Cole. And gladly hands it over. In return for a promise of return alongside interesting notes from the county.

Knutsford is an old timey style Independent and doesn't want statehood or government interference, so he's unlikely to budge on anything relating authority, but chatting is another matter.

Upon getting this tome, Altan will receive [[Reward Cards for Everlasting#*(Altan)* Level 3 Artifice Anew|his reward card.]]

#### *(Barton)* On Logging with Jared Ansel
Barton might spot an older man chopping trees in the wood, teaching his granddaughter how to do it. The little girl is only eight, but she wields the axe with expertise. He's giving her a few pointers before letting her loose on a bit of tree.

> "And that's it, Abbey, now go on. Give it your all."

This is Jared Ansel, the owner of two of the major logging houses in [[Crosskeys]]. He could fill Barton in on what happened with his competitor and compatriot, Jack Sampson. As well as talk to him about his world view. Here are some topics:

* Raising kids, and grand kids. Jared is a proud father and grandfather.
* Standard Pulp is a leech on society, the beauty of Crosskeys was in the community of people helping one another. Corporations and Unions ruin that community of trust.
* His daughter, Lydia, wants statehood, she's got a petition (40 signatures) that she's handing to **Tom Peterson** on the next ferry. He's skeptical, worries about big government.

#### *(CC)* The Crosskeys Three
CC might witness a creature halfway up a house, with the window open, pulling out a small golden necklace through the tiny openable slit. This slender little Kenku goes by Echo of Feathers. It begs CC not to mention anything, it was stealing because it likes shiny things, but promises to return it later.

Echo of Feathers says it can offer CC a favor in return and introduce her to Rene Quickpaw; an infobroker who's in town at the moment.

Quickpaw sits in the tavern most days, and knows a few things about what's going on, but won't share anything for free. He wants N50,000 per bit of information.

* He knows Rhul, a member of the Cloven, and can point out where he is. (House in Roachport, he goes by another name; Mel Kelly)
* He knows who the Nightfont and Starkhelm are.
* He knows about a member of the Prescient Few in [[Roachport]].
* He knows about Fulburne Consulting, and that there's a member of that corporation in Roachport goes by Callum Rose.

#### *(Church)* Tilting at Windmills
Passing the windmill, Church might get called out by an old man. This 70-year-old has lived in Crosskeys since after the [[Last War]] where he served as a Sergeant-Major in the [[Atroxian Army]]. He's sitting dejectedly outside his big windmill when he calls out.

> "Oi! You there! Big 'un! Yer one of thems warforged ain't ya?"

This man, Horatio Rowan, isn't actually all that mad at Church, he just wants someone to shout at and be mad at. Horatio served under Brigadier Keltoris Silentheart and Major-General Everett Burnside, but he remembers thinking they were especially harsh on the warforged.

> "Many o' my boys died that day, ya hear? Mi'lads... my chums..."
> 
> He breaks down crying.

Horatio can't be mad about it, he's been mad his whole life. It isn't working. He remembers Fusilier (Skylar) Braunstone dying in that battle, the father of the (Aaron) patriarch across the river. He remembers raising the Braunstone boy, little Aaron, remembers his wedding with his love Alfie.

He remembers settling here, building this damn windmill, and now he's nowhere.

> "I ain't got nuffin. No boys, no job, no people o' my own. And I weren't even there to see it for my own self. Now I'm just an old codger with nothing."

His loss of purpose was a one-two punch, now there's nothing for the windmill to mill, but worse, of course, is seeing his godson die.

Other scenes for Church:
* The Bridgemen are about half-way done with the tavern, it's a ten-day job. Benitez won't have to spend more money than what Barton gave him, since the Bridgemen are cheap.

#### *(Emily)* Rapid's New Story
Rapid finds Emily to tell her about a Casterian immigrant named Hordur Gunnarsson who moved here following the whole crisis in Hofudstad recently. He went here mostly to avoid the government, and other issues, but even he's regretting it. Everything's crumbling apart here.

They were supposed to dig a well for clean water here, but never did. Apparently Parliament passed some bill for it, but it didn't happen. He wonders if the government has forgotten about them. Maybe he'll just give up and pack it in.

Cedric, if consulted, may think there's cause for class action lawsuit here. None of these people have had their well dug as mandated by the Clean Water provision in the County Cultivation Act. Sue the government.

#### *(Maeve)* Talia and Sister Morabito
Maeve finds Talia Sinclair talking with a nun named **Sister Laura Morabito**, Rector Ruberti's right hand in Regent's Peak, she says she saw Weiss kill a person by tearing their arms off, cackling while she did it. When she reported it to the police they called her "hysterical" and she was followed by the police every day thereafter.

Talia will share with Maeve this story, and together they'll talk about the church who's avoiding Regent's Peak.

Talia is also very happy to see Maeve back, apologizing for being distant when she left.

#### *(Seraph)* Sheriff Amari Morton
The Sheriff of Crosskeys is a local government position appointed to the settlement by the County Commissioner, Tom Peterson, back in 1447 to replace the previous sheriff. Amari is a dejected about the whole local governance situation, unsure of how they ever manage to move onwards.

He knows this town would thrive if they somehow managed a population uptick and had new funding, they could reinvest in fishery and agriculture and it would start to turn things around. But...

> "Nobody's willingly moved to Crosskeys for a few years now."
