## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

## Dinner Time
Tonight's session is all about what comes next, and more so, about how the characters decide what comes next. It's all well and good that they have different ideas about what's going to happen, but somehow they must reach a consensus on what's important.

#### Sir Rotheric
Upon hearing that the team is planning what's next and heading to the Inn, Sir Rotheric excitedly decides to come along. He'll want to introduce everyone to Edith, his lady friend.

Edith Brady is an elderly woman who was born in Maplewood, and moved to Crosskeys following a tax incentive plan around the turn of the century. She knows lots of people who moved because of it. Now, she's unemployed, cutting into her savings, and thinking about moving somewhere cheaper. Sir Rotheric is trying to convince her not to.

Edith and Sir Rotheric met back in 33, he had just turned to Loggers County after reading about their debt in the news and looking into the matter. He mounted an expedition, and met Edith while drinking at Frank's, he fell head over heels.

> "Not everyone is so lucky as to get to experience love. I was lucky enough to experience it twice."

He's kept returning ever since. Mostly for her, but she maintains if that were true he wouldn't continue venturing out into the forest and just stay behind. He *is* getting on a bit.

#### Elias Altman
The Brooksway Ferry is a single-masted brig and the only ship that makes frequent trips up and down the river and aids in crossings in Loggers County, both in Crosskeys and in Roachport.

The Ferryman, **Elias Altman**, reminds the campaigners that he's planning on heading out to Roachport tomorrow morning, but he doesn't fancy delaying the journey much longer. 

He's planning to make a trip there and back, to return here in somewhere between 4 and 12 days, circa. (2d4+2) Or, about a week.

He knows there's a few others that are wanting to get a ride, namely the Bishop, **Ennio Federici**, and Sister **Dorotea Tasanella**. As well as **Lydia Ansel**, an activist for statehood.

He's confident about sailing the river now that the Horned One is taken care of, he's sailed it loads of times, and is feeling more confident now.

He might be able to delay going another day, if they really want him to, maybe two, but anything more and he'd rather just go there and back and take the players on another trip. Ultimately, he's prepared to plan his days around the players, for a down payment of N1.000.

#### The Tavern Rebuilding
The **Bridgemen** are about half-way done with the tavern, it's a ten-day job fix up job. With the Bridgemen working on it, Benitez won't have to spend more money than what Barton gave him. Upon hearing about other projects they should be ecstatic.

**Brooke Shab** has been helping Benitez with his finances and other organization, he's agreed to invest a little in a larger ale shipment, thinking it's going to save him money in the long run.

**Charles Kenton** has sourced a bottle of Swinewood Wine for Barton. He also heard of a bottle of Blue Crescent, a blueberry wine sourced from Moonlit Bend in Silvercreek, Eastern Areston for around N2.000 from the scarred woman, Martina.

**Claire Anders** has gotten a detailed briefing from Sheriff Amari on the state of the town and can, at this state speak to the situation the town is in. She thinks it's pretty dire. She wants to pencil in a meeting between her, Brook, Seraph, and Barton at a given opportunity to see what they can figure out.

#### The Big Benitez Dinner
Invited into the Crossed Swords Inn, the campaigners find a massive impromptu dinner table arrangement, complete with meat, bread, beans, corn, and whatever else. There's ale and wine, and Marla Benitez, Matthew's wife, has prepared a meal to thank the heroes for saving her husband's life.

She invites them to dig in and says they're welcome guests at this Inn whenever they like.

#### What's Next?
At this point, I assume the players are going to want to talk about what comes next. Though a long rest is in order and will require being stationary for 3 days. Let's lay out a few options!

* **Plan A: Advancing** - They may want to stay here the minimum amount of time, ask the ferryman to delay by 2 days, and then head out to Roachport.
* **Plan B: Building** - They might want to remain in town until they can start digging a well, maybe even until they can start the next project after the well, and then head out. 
* **Plan C: Constant Activity** - They may want to wait a little, immediately go on another expedition, and then go to Roachport after that.
* **Plan D: Dilly Dally** - They might be most tempted to stick around here for some number of weeks, working on their projects.

Another important question here is where are they going to rest. They probably can't rest in the Crossed Swords if they're wanting to do stuff on the other side.

#### Sidekick's Opinion
Ultimately, my job in this scene is to portray the sidekicks, who are primarily concerned with what they're doing next.

**Sir Rotheric Maxley** (bombastically posh) was excited about going to Roachport to prepare the next leg of his expedition. But he understands that this party is developing its own mind, a young person's mind. He muses that maybe he'd be better off as a guide later on.

**Rapids** (deaf) wants to help Emily set up some sort of news organization, something the pair can run. And Rapid can prepare and manage in Emily's absence. What do they call the paper?

**Bolt**, (church) **Forge**, (cockeny) and **Wheel**, (posh english) are all excited about staying here and building something for once. They could be torn away with the promise of more building in Roachport, but that's the bottom line: build and help.

**Cedric Thorne** (royal english) is excited about this company they were talking about forming, and especially preparing a legal charter and handling their paperwork.

**Talia Sinclair** (posh english) is excited about managing an estate if that's brought up. She loves all that stuff! Dinner parties, horses, farms. **Muffin** is perfectly happy doing whatever.

**Claire Anders** (american) and **Brooke** (southern) are both laser focused on helping this town and Loggers County in general. They think there's reform to be done here, but importantly, nothing major can happen without Tom Peterson's approval.

**Jane Tranter** (american) wants Barton to win the election. Retail politics is important, get to know the locals. Have him talk to them, find a way to make their concerns his policies. However, she also thinks that stuff like getting shit done might move the needle too. It'll need a bit of both, and getting the County Commissioner to help would be a huge boon.

**Kenton** (hackett's driver) is here to serve, and he's really been twiddling his thumbs this past week. Ideally he gets a task he can manage soon.

**Dee** (american dead-pan) is focused on the job. She wants to start building her case. Roachport might be the place to go. Where's Rhul? Who is he? Does someone know him? Then there's the question of magic items, she's not the type to make them, but isn't opposed to writing scrolls if funded. Though at the moment, her desire is largely to go get their equipment from Blackguard. Send for it, get it here.
