## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

A great way to land a session in town is to focus on the npcs the party brings with them, here are some scenes.

#### Callum Rose and Fulburne
**Callum Rose**, is a nicely dressed stick-insect looking man steps up. He's actually an employee of [[Fulburne Consulting]] and is here in his capacity as CAS' man in Loggers County, arriving back in Wintersdusk.

Callum came here intending to investigate the [[Quexil Caste]]. He's personally investigated Gaxek Point and made contact with the violent members of the tribe's inner circle. He intends to parlay with the [[Nightfont Nomads]] to find out more.

While Callum Rose didn't murder Tom Peterson, his written intelligence report sent via Waterdhavian Paper Bird on the region was intercepted two weeks ago by the Whisper and resulted in the assassination of Tom Peterson last week.
