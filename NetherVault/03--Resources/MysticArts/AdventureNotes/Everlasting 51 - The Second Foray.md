## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

## The Ferry Trip
We ended last time with them on the ferry, it's a short trip. Only long enough for a brief conversation between CC and Callum. See below. Other than that, it's goal is to just land us in this episode: the one where they are explorers again.

#### Avoiding Me?
When he stepped on the boat, Fulburne Consulting operative, Callum Rose, noticed that he'd entirely missed one of these Expeditioners up until now. He wants to know who she is and why she seems to be avoiding him.

He doesn't know who she is, and wouldn't think much of it. He doesn't work for Boyle and doesn't care much about what happened in Blackguard. He does know Virginia Hill, Sandbagger 5, but he isn't a Sandbagger. He works for Fulburne. They're separate.

The purpose of this scene is to muddy the water with CC enough so that Callum's presence is interesting enough to provoke a risk vs reward discussion.

###### What is Fulburne Consulting
**Fulburne Consulting** was a shell company founded by CAS back when he was the [[Undersecretary for Intelligence]]. But after he vanished, he's taken the company as his own and used its untracked funds to conduct itself in accordance with CAS's desires.

Virginia Hill is IIC, and not a part of Fulburne. But the two often work in tandem when the situation demands it for one reason or another.

## Hexploration!
Now, it's an expedition session, let's lean into it. This time roll for random encounters, like every other night or something! We'll do survival, weather, the works!

**Random Encounter**, don't be afraid to let random encounters happen, roll and see what happens! They can be roleplay or action, it's all good by me.

**Ankheg Attack!**, with all the set-up, you can't go wrong with an Ankheg!

**Roach's Tombstone**, A forgotten gravestone in the forest, marking the resting place of Angus Roach. His epitaph reads: "Brave new horizons can be found at home." Locals say the wind always picks up near the stone. There's a bed of wildflowers at the base of it.

**Koalinth at the Mouth**, around the mouth they might spot a small warband of Koalinth lead by Sergeant Holg. They think it's ridiculous they'd assassinate Peterson, they're not interested in dumb human settlers. They're likely to pincer move the players if the opportunity presents himself. He works for Warchief Mulk.

**Old Docks**, the remains of a wooden dock, once used by fishermen during the early days of the county. The wood is rotting, and most of the structure has collapsed into the water, but the posts still rise out of the lake like skeletal fingers.
