## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

## Hungry Mutts
It's initiative! The [[Hungry Mutts]] are scattered about, but so are the expeditioners. This fight may well be a lot more than Crossroads bargained for. But that is the nature of the beast. It may also be that this fight is surprisingly easy. I think we'll have to play it a bit by ear. Regardless, it should feel tough.

I've prepared paper statblocks with the characters on them, they're all 2024 **pirates**, **cult fanatics**, **mastiffs** or 2014 **thugs**, though all modified somewhat. Broadly speaking, the AC is 13, with the exception of Gregor who has 16.

Gregor is drunk (poisoned) but will sober up entierly once he's bloodied, which would be a good time for him to be healed by Big Bjorn.

CC starts at the top of the tower, 60 feet above the ground, next to Max and four mastiffs. Maeve starts outside the tower's front door, next to Pike, waiting for Muffin. While inside the tower was everybody else, including NPCs Rapid and Sir Rotheric.

Let the dice fall where they may, it's a scary fight, let it be scary.

#### The Watchtower
The tower is split into four levels. They are:

**Living Quarters.** This elaborate common room contains all the niceties of luxury and comfort. It's an elaborately decorated living space, nothing gaudy or gold, but elegant. The objects here originally belonged to the people of Soda Springs and are valued at around N120,000.

**Armory.** This floor contains seven cupboards with two additional copies of each Hungry Mutt's loadout. As in, a pair of extra copies of both their armor and their weapons.

* x12 Battleaxes
* x6 Heavy Crossbows
* x9 Handcrossbows
* x3 Shortswords
* x3 Spears
* x3 Daggers
* x3 Pikes
* x3 Light Crossbows
* x3 Leather Armor

**Storage.** This floor is the loot storage of the Hungry Mutts, not items taken from the town, but rather items stolen from other factions across the Elmwood that came here to attack the town. This includes:

* Magical paper and ink worth N50.000
* Six healing potions.
* Five repair kits. (healing potions for Church)
* A couple of bloodstones (worth N5000 each)
* Three elven made Nightfont longswords
* Three elven Nightfont bows.
* Three elven studded leather.
* A pouch containing crushed bones.
* A scroll of **Summon Construct**, carrying the Seal of the Emperor. 
	* (found off of an Anarchist who was an archivist.)

**Workshop.** This floor is used for making and repairing the weapons they own. It doesn't contain much of value.

#### In Case of Death
I think we're still technically in the tutorial. So, I think a death here isn't right just yet. But that doesn't mean I'm going to take it easy on the PCs. This is a tough fight, and it's going to be a tough fight. So, if someone dies tonight, they get a [[Permanent Injury]] instead. 

## Soda Springs
With the Hungry Mutts dead, the town of [[Soda Springs]] is liberated. The townsfolk would happily invite the heroes into their home, or gather around in the Boar's Head to meet the heroes and tell their stories together.

Ultimately, the town is theirs to command, and with it discovered it could be integrated into Loggers County. As long as it maintains no production and doesn't abide by Parliamentary Law or the County Cultivation Act, it neither consumes or provides anything. It's just a blip.

#### Security Is Lacking
A point that'll be raised quickly is that with the Hungry Mutts dead, this town is no longer protected. But there's two counterpoints to this that Sir Rotheric will happily bring up.

* Nobody knows that the town's security was just killed. There's no reason to believe the macabre fence of corpses won't continue to work as deterrent for a while longer.
* Sir Rotheric is prepared to settle here for a bit, and provide protection. At least until some form of communication can be set up, especially if Edith Brady will come along.

#### Glow in the Geyser
As Barton looks into Little Fizz (or Big Fizzie), he'll see:

> There's this beating, almost pulsating purple glow. It hums, flickers, changing almost randomly. Not unlike the Geode you saw in the Southern Isles.
> 
> It beckons.

Attempts to fish the geode out will invariably fail, because it's snagged in some sort of net that's been down here since the town was settled.

Diving into the geyser is firstly, and surprisingly brisk and cold, it's mineral rich and opening the eyes is terribly difficult. But he'll find a geode in there.

> Barton, your sight goes blurry as you collapse.
> 
> Another string of visions, a six armed woman, a giant made of fire, floating garden cities, civilizations in a quintessant existence. Hawks, men, hawk-men, Atlantis and Babylon.
> 
> Emily, you see the same thing, as you find yourself shaking and then... collapse.
> 
> A spiraling maze of rocks that burrow deep within the ground, a land where the ground is ground and the ground is sky, a nesting network of narrow caves that number in the cacophonous infinity.
> 
> Death, dark, deep below. There lies the Forge of the Earth: the Earth Heart Forge. Forever burning, both fire and earth, both rock and flame. A heart of lands.
> 
> And then, a spiraling vision that lands on the Elmwood. Mountain peaks you both recognize to be the Starkcrag Mountains.
> 
> When your sight returns, tremors rock your bodies along with a soup of cold sweat and heart palpitations.

#### Progression
**Party Milestone.** For disposing of the **Hungry Mutts**, an enemy faction, the party as a whole receive a Party Milestone.

**Safe Haven.** If they take the tower as their own, and post some security in it, like Sir Rotheric for example, they could gain a [[Unaligned Progression#Safe Haven|Safe Haven]] to be able to take a long rest in a single night with [[Excursion Resting]].

**Ancestral Connection.** Barton has an opportunity to discover the nature of his bloodline. He gets his reward card. His origins are in Loggers County.
