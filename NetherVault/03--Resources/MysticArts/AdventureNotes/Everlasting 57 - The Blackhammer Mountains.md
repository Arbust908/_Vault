## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

### Session Description
Barton's old friends make an expedition into a locked off lead mine where a Dwarven rune was found. Hopes of discovering a long dead civilization sends the archeologists on an expedition into the mine.

#### Who Are These Adventurers?
These are Barton's friends, all excited to go on an adventure after being apart for a few months, and there's a persistent lamentation that their adventures might be rare now that the World Tour is over.

###### Gavin Teague (Human/He)
Gav's a lovable little bastard. A rascal who loves getting into scrapes and poking around where he shouldn't. He's an archeologist and a historian and a lot like Barton (who he calls 'Barty'). The pair of them are thick as thieves and together they're going to keep egging one another on until they both die.

> "Come on! Anyone can yap about these things! Knowledge is found at the source!"

###### Professor. Henk Vilgar (Orc/He)
Henk is far smarter than he looks, he's an expert on ancient and dead languages and hieroglyphics. And he's scary in fights too. There's this duality about him, the calm scholar, and the fierce orc. Only one has the reigns at a time.

> "Odds are we'll discover something that was suspected but not known. Those discoveries are no less valuable than the other kind."

###### Arabella Leafwalker (Halfling/She)
Arabella is a [[Geology|geologist]] and metallurgist, and a spectacular cook in her free time. She's the primary excavator and rummager... and remarkably thick-headed. Unlikely to give up, ever. She thinks of [[Smallfolk]] as being too passive and cannot imagine a world where she doesn't follow her passion at every turn.

> "That far underground, there's no telling what happened with these dwarves, maybe they evacuated into the [[Underdark]] or the [[Bronze Realm]]."

###### Deneiath Treneer Aeneiros
This is the rare [[Atroxian]] theologian, an expert not just in religions and religious iconography, but also something of a sceptic. She reads a lot of [[Mordenkainen]] and believes the gods cannot share our morality by virtue of their divinity. That agents of their will have more control than anyone realizes.

> "How could Ord know what the dwarves are doing? How could he care?"

###### Dr. Rebecca Wynne
Rebecca has a morbid fascination with death and how societies die. Her degree was in epidemiology and she especially loved studiying the [[Aeriidian]] histories of the [[Seven Plagues]]. In particular the locusts that nearly annihilated the Aeriid. From a historical perspective, Rebecca believes it's entirely possible the [[Modern Period]] is a blip of a relatively plague free time that will end eventually with what she calls "The Final Swarm." 

> "A thousand dead societies litter this world, lost to time. We could uncover one!"

#### Credge Statue
Passing through town, the group will pass by a statue of Wallace Credge, and a plaque that reads:

> "Your nation owes you an eternal debt."

It was given to the town by the War Committee in the summer of 1421, at the suggestion of the [[Office of Weapons and Munitions]], along with a huge investment in factories that are now owned by private corporation [[Blackpowder Ltd]].

Necessary during the war, privatized by the Powell-Freemantle administration of 1428. Due to an agreement [[Celia Powell]] had to come to in order to pass several pieces of legislation, including her [[Colonial Representative Act of 1429]].

Might be a good time for Gavin to rib on Barton for his political opinions. "You said you liked her didn't you?" - Gav personally likes [[Charles Freemantle]] quite a bit, adding: "The Freemantles have a good record on the Last War, the economy, and the royalty." - he'll balk at the fact that Freemantle did this.

## Journey Uphill
The middle of the adventure consists of the journey to the location of the mine. We'll break it into the following challenges:

* The climb uphill to the mine is actually quite the treck. Requiring a DC 10 Constitution save to avoid gaining a level of Exhaustion.
* Once at the mine, a foreman named Mr Gunnar Gunnarsson forbids the heroes to enter unless convinced otherwise with a DC 15 Persuasion check, a N1500 bribe provides advantage.
	* Failing that, they'll have to sneak in. Or bribe.

#### The Fume Tunnels
Find the Fume Tunnels and then find the source of the dwarven stuff! Any skill failure here means an hour is spent dealing with it. Which results in the following check:

* Each hour, a DC 13 Con save is necessary to avoid 1d4 poison damage.

The Fume Tunnels is basically a series of skill checks:

* To enter, they'll have to tear down the boards, and break in: DC 15 athletics.
* To find the dwarven area, they'll have to understand the layout: DC 15 history.
* And then spot the runes: DC 15 Perception.

#### The Bats and Piercer
While in the Fume Tunnels, there's all kinds of dwarven runes, and a small segment of a staircase leading further below. It's a breakthrough! There was a civilization here! But in that moment, they're attacked by a **swarm of bats** and a single **piercer** drops on Henk (or whoever has the most hp / not Rebecca.)


## Cave In!
At this point, the tunnels cave in and both Barton and Rebecca stand on a bit of floor that collapses into a small ravine that leads further below, they'll take 7 (3d6) falling damage. 

The rest of the team shouts down they'll have to hire a work crew to dig them out, urging them to stay still, it's dangerous in there! Rebecca and Barton just have to remain there. Not move.

But there's a big door that leads out, it looks royal, like a throne room door.

#### The Time Abyss
Rebecca tells a story about how deep time is, how any countless civilizations could've been found. Not just dwarves. Even with their clues, they were as likely to find anything!

* Ruins of ancient species, from the alliance.
* Razul ruins, built by dragons.
* Drashkut ruins, built by dwarves.
* Nathaniel ruins, built by elves.
* Casterian ruins, built by counter-settlers.
* Giant ruins, built before the Casterians came.
* Hell... it could've been a planar temple if Arabella is to be believed!

#### The Scripted Event
Barton's backstory says there was a door and a preserved chest (stone). It's up to him what exactly happens, but basically when he interacts with anything, there's a small explosion as rocks are thrown around and poisonous gasses fill the room!

As they choke, a spirit appears, touches Barton and he can breathe the poison as if it were air. But Rebecca tries to open the door and fails... and she chokes to death.

Her corpse is pale with bulging eyes and bleeding from the nose and mouth.

#### The Funeral
We cut a week or two into the future!

It's a rainy day in Blackguard, Wynne is being buried underneath the Abbey in the catacombs, her family is present, so is everyone except Henk. A theme that would continue from now on, they'd never all be together again.

As the funeral comes to a close, and an honor guard by the Abbey steps watches as the pole bearers bring her into the catacombs, Gav approaches Barton and asks:

> "Barty..." he says: "It wasn't your fault."

And to end, we'll say:

> "Barton?" you hear Sir Rotheric Maxley ask, and that's where we'll pick it up next time.
