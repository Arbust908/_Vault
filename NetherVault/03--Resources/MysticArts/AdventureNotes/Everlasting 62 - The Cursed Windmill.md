## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

## Cursed Mill
This old windmill said to be cursed. The blades never stop turning, even when there’s no wind. Strange howls can be heard from within on stormy nights. 

* The **Ground Floor** is dotted with 6 **Yellow Musk Zombies**. (with 12 ac)
* The **Second Story** is a tripping hazard, careful navigation is required over its ledges to reach the third floor.
* The **Top of the Mill** consists of 15 **Yellow Musk Zombies** and a single **Creeper**.
* The **Basement** is locked and untouched by the haunt! It's got a whole apartment in it. Untouched, and just a lot of dust swirling around.

#### Who Were These People?
Each of the zombie was an explorer of some kind that adventured here, this is the remains of 4 whole adventuring companies: The Goblin Stompers, The Dawnseekers, Platinum Pigeons, and the Thorncloaks. One of these guys is a member of the Grave Order, see below.

Each zombie looted yields:
* 3 simple or martial weapons.
* 3d12 gold pieces in notes.
* 4d20 actual coins, each d20 roll is one type: copper, silver, gold, platinum.
* simple leather armor (except two are in chain mail)
* ...and there's a 5% chance of a single art object worth N50.000. Jewels worth:
	* 20.000
	* 10.000
	* 40.000
	* 30.000

Among all the bodies the following can be found:

* 3d4 healing potions (3) or adrenaline (4)
* 4 repair runes on a dwarf member of the Dawnseekers.
* A couple of harpers on the Thorncloaks.
* ...and three magic items, roll randomly.
* 5 signet rings for the Platinum Pigeons, worth N10.000

The Grave Order guy has a single uncommon relic on him, some good equipment, a masterwork rapier, and a single black coin with a skull on it that reads: Grave Order. 

3 of these sources were on the ground floor (1 healing potions, 2 repair runes.)

There's an Ogre here downstairs, it was shot by the Thorncloak with the harper.
