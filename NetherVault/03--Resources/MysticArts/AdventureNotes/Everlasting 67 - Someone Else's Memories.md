## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

### Session Description
Church's mind malfunctions as he lives moments from the life of the consciousnesses that make up his body and soul. Along the way he must confront the morality and ethos that makes up his entire being through the lens of those that came before him.

### Opening Monologue

> Every new age is defined by the one that came before.
> 
> This age of a tenuous peace in a barely functioning democracy is defined by the end of the Last War, the death of the Invincible Overlord, and the untold pain and suffering felt by millions around the world.
> 
> Days and months collide with years and ages as you feel a tickle at the back of your neck, you find yourself nervous, your mechanical sensors firing, and your non-existent heartrate spiking.
> 
> Something terrible has gone wrong. You're not sure what, or where, or even who you are. But the streets of this old city are begging to be explored, searched... there is an enemy hiding in here somewhere!
> 
> Roll for survival.


Throughout this session, Church is essentially experiencing a vibrant hallucination, feeling like he is these men, and these men are him. His own body is somehow both his own and the body of these men. The reflection changes and shifts between his form and that of the men. It's vibrant, and it feels real. 

## Draxicar, the Knight's Honor
*When is violence necessary?* is the question posed by the first part of the adventure. We'll explore it through the lens of Draxicar (red dragon knight) who must confront exactly that when his fellow first 10 created members of the [[Dragon Phalanx]] go rogue.

#### Trailing Them
There's a trail of blood leading through the streets of Castle Andruum. It's been left by fellow newly created members of the Dragon Phalanx. He's got to track them through the streets.

#### The Crowned Hog
It's a fight against **two knights**, **one veteran**, **one priest**, and a **mage apprentice**. And there are civilians there! (guards and Captain McDonald) Protect them! Jathril is the gold one, he's leading them.

#### Good King Omund
The Good King, [[Omund Francis]], personally asks Draxicar to help them with the Dragon Phalanx. He knows this will be painful, he knows this will kill Draxicar, and he doesn't intend to lie. But rather have a frank conversation with him.

Omund intends to convince Draxicar that his sacrifice will pave the way for a countless generations of good men. It will enable Omund to help people everywhere in Brightwell. A huge boon to all defenseless people.

What makes Draxicar special is his reasoning and intuition, to know good from bad. The Good King has been associated with that moniker for 80 years now, and before that he was the Good Advisor. He's given it a lot of thought.

> "People think good is in action, it is not. It's in reasoning."

* War, when fought for the right reasons, is good.
* Diplomacy, when done with wicked intentions, is bad.
* Therefor, GOOD is not a question of what, but a question of the why and the how.

> "That's the reasoning, and not everyone has it. But you do. I know you do."

#### Sacrifice, for the Greater Good
Draxicar is put into the chair, is plugged in and killed by Vitae. Omund just watches and then says:

> "You want to help people, soldier? Then close your eyes, and dream of a better world. Your dream will echo in eternity. That is my terminal order."

Then he feels the tickle turn into a sharp pain! It's the plugs!

## Atkins, the Father's Heart
*What is sacrifice?* is asked through Atkins when he decides to serve his nation, despite having a kid at home. Why he makes this choice is worthy of exploration.

#### Credge's Speech
David Atkins stands in Ailmon's Square listening to a speech by [[Wallace Credge]]. It's autumn in the year 1417, and Credge is running for reelection as the Chancellor for Citadel Precinct and there's talk of him being able to challenge Eddington in the leadership elections later this year. 

Credge is giving a speech and he's gathered quite the crowd in the rain. This speech would go on to be known as the "Canary Speech."

* I come before you today as Credge the Canary.
* Another act of tyranny has occurred. The people of Lost Isle were massacred by Dragon Riders and the man so-called Invincible has set his sights on the Frostmoore.
* The Eddington administration refuses yet again to raise defenses, to prepare the navy, to arm the population. We're told it's on the other side of the world.
* We are Atroxians. We were born with the privilege of having a benevolent Emperor, of living in peace-time, but we know it cannot last. We know a war is coming, even Eddington and Powell.
* There is no candidate for peace, there's just choosing to be conquered or choosing to prepare for the worst case scenario!
* I will not rest while Atroxians remain unprotected, I will not yield to tyranny, or to the Royalist party's apparatus of apathy. I will defend this nation!

> "When a canary dies in the coal mine, we can chose to pretend like we saw nothing... or we can prepare for the worst case scenario before we all choke on the fumes."

He then adds...

> "I will not rest while until the Administration takes my concerns seriously, and neither should you! Cast your ballot, yes. But furthermore, make your will known with your actions. Enlist and show your chancellors that you are not afraid. Enlist to say that you want to fight to defend this nation. Enlist, and you'll say you're prepared to face the Overlord himself!"
> 
> "My terminal order will be to ask that if you can help: help."

#### Sergeant-Major DeForterrio
David Atkins and his friend John Webb join a queue to sign up for the army. It's high time they protect their country. Webb has two teenage kids in the city, and he's terrified of them being in Blackguard when inevitably the Overlord strikes.

He's got a duty to his kids, and to anyone who can't fight, to make his own contribution. And Atkins feels the same way.

#### Sophia Atkins
David's wife, Sophia is in her mother's (Ruby) apartment, trying to calm Michael - a six year old kid - because he got the wrong kind of meatballs for dinner (he's picky). Sofia's out at a dinner party. So it's just Sofia and David here.

Sofia wonders why David signed up for the Army, what drove him to such madness. What if he dies abroad and Michael is raised without a father.

Why must he sign up?

>
