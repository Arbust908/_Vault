At my current job we wanted to implement A/B testing to anchor our UX/UI decisions more on user data that just Eng + UX them feeling. The thing is none of us had much experience in A/B testing with Vue. I had the base concept on my mind and my PM gave me the green light to build somthing.
So I went on and built it. At first it was a simple system, we generated a cookie on client side and drove conditional render from that. That wouldn't cut it, given most our site was served cached from server it would lead to a flicker. So I dug deeper. I added a server middleware that would lift the user cookies from the request, locate the ab-{test-name} cookie and if not used the GA id as seed for the generation. Lastly I had 4 different cached versions served depending on your ab-cookie. 
The rendering engine also hade backed in a GA event that would inform us GA id and the variant assigned to it. I used a Puppeteer script on QA to test over 10k mock users to see if the bucketing and rendering where on point. We had a 0.5% deviation. And we could easily re us the engine for future A/B test. 
This engine lead our team to make more informed decisions on our top converting surfaces and is planed to be Open sourced this year.
