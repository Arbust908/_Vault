People in the other post I made about necromancy keep asking "But what about societies that don't view necromancy as evil" and while they're mostly dozens of re-blogs deep and won't see this, here it is.

- Everything is of short supply. The labor of the dead is needed to keep society running.
- There's nothing special about a dead body so why not put it to good use?
- There was a mythical necromancer hero, so becoming a necromancer is seen as a nobel goal to be like him/her.
- The dead must return to the earth. It doesn't matter if that is immediate or if there's a delay due to necromancy.
- Reanimating the dead keeps their memory alive, which is good.
