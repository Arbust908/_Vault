# Phandalin Hexmap

[![Phandalin Hexcrawl Map (12 x 12 grid)](https://thealexandrian.net/creations/phandalin/phandalin-hexmap.png)](https://thealexandrian.net/creations/phandalin/phandalin-hexmap.png)1 hex = 12 miles

[**DOWNLOAD THE MAP**](https://thealexandrian.net/creations/phandalin/phandalin-hexmap.png)

Phandalin and the surrounding region of the Sword Coast have featured in three D&D adventures:

-   [_Lost Mine of Phandelver_](https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/49910/roleplaying-games/review-dd-starter-set)
-   [_Dragon of Icespire Peak_](https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/49938/roleplaying-games/review-dd-essentials-kit)
-   [_The Shattered Obelisk_](https://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0786969008/digitalcomi0a-20)

Here I’ve adapted Mike Schley’s excellent region map, featured in each of these adventures, into an accurate-but-simplified hexmap.

The reason for creating a map like this, of course, is to serve as a GM’s map while running a [hexcrawl](https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/46020/roleplaying-games/5e-hexcrawl):

-   The alphanumeric hex grid makes it easy to key content to the map.
-   The simplified iconography makes it easier to adjudicate terrain modifiers.
