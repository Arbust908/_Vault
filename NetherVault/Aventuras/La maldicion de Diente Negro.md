## Story overview

Ancient wards containing a dimensional entity were broken by greedy pirates centuries ago. A priestess sacrificed the crew to rebuild the wards, trapping the island in a pocket dimension. In the present day, an explorer investigating the "Ghost Island" has accidentally weakened the wards again. The heroes are called to rescue their friend, discover the truth, and perform the ritual to seal the entity away — possibly forever.

---

## Backstory (pre-adventure)

**Centuries ago:** An explorer/pirate figure (Black Teeth Keetes) discovers a hidden cave with treasures and ancient statues. By taking the treasure, they unknowingly break a ward that contains a powerful extradimensional entity.

That night, ~30 dimensional creatures (Shamblers) appear and begin abducting crew members nightly. A mystic/priestess traveling with the crew (Mama Flè) understands what happened. She secretly recreates the warding ceremony — but the price is the entire crew. She pulls the island into a pocket dimension and turns the surviving crew into undead guardians. She kills the captain and uses his remains (skull) to power a golden brazier at the heart of the ward.

**Two years ago:** A fishing vessel (the Sweet Mary Jane) is wrecked on the island, damaging one of the perimeter ward statues. This causes the island to phase in and out of visibility — locals begin calling it the "Ghost Island."

**One week ago:** A famous explorer (Howard Winchester), a friend of the player characters, chases rumors of the Ghost Island. He commissions a vessel and finds it — but unknowingly destroys more ward statues, releasing the dimensional creatures. He is captured and trapped in the entity's pocket dimension. His damaged boat and belongings (journal, map, letter, strange coin) are found by a fisherman, who telegrams the heroes.

## Detailed beats

**Beat 1 — Inciting contact.** The heroes receive a message (telegram, letter, raven, courier — whatever fits the setting) from a local contact saying their friend Howard Winchester has gone missing near a mysterious location known locally as the "Ghost Island." This is the hook.

**Beat 2 — Meeting the contact.** The local contact (Captain Cobb, or any NPC with maritime/wilderness knowledge) explains he found the wreckage of Howard's vessel. He shares handouts: a journal, a letter addressed to one of the heroes, a rough map, and a strange gold coin. He offers to guide them to the island in exchange for a share of any treasure. He's the only one willing to take them.

**Beat 3 — Investigation phase.** Optional but enriching. Heroes can research the island's history in a library or local records — finding old maps that show an island no longer on modern charts, and lore on the pirate Black Teeth Keetes. No other guides will take them to the island. This phase yields exposition and possibly additional handouts.

**Beat 4 — Arrival at the Ghost Island.** The island is unnatural in its silence — no birds, no insects. The mood is immediately wrong. Heroes land on a pebble beach. Optional environmental dressing: the rocky shore is littered with the wrecks of ships that struck the island over the centuries.

**Beat 5 — First contact.** Following the map, heroes find Howard's campsite, one of the ward statues (idol-like, with a socket in its forehead), a golden brazier, and a blackened skull. Then a dimensional creature (Shambler) attacks, attempting to drag a hero into an alternate realm.

**Beat 6 — Vision of the Other Realm.** Either by being grabbed by the Shambler, or through a rift that opens when the creature is killed: a hero briefly sees the pocket dimension. It looks like the island but filled with dead pirates and decaying bodies. Hovering above is a massive, alien, life-draining entity. Howard is there — haggard and desperate — and screams the mission: _"Get to the ship. Find her. There's no time."_ Then the hero is expelled back.

**Beat 7 — Navigating the island.** The island is patrolled by zombie pirates in varying states of decay — some nearly invisible, overgrown with moss and vegetation. The heroes must move across the island toward Pirate's Cove, dealing with patrols and close encounters. This is the exploration and combat phase. The dagger (received later) can be used retroactively or mid-session to compel zombies to follow the heroes.

**Beat 8 — The cave and the ship.** Pirate's Cove is a sea cave sheltering the ancient pirate vessel. The cave is strung with an elaborate network of ropes and traps. The ship itself is ancient, partially flooded, and full of atmosphere. A guardian creature may inhabit the water around it. The ship holds treasure and, in its hold or cabin, the sleeping Mama Flè.

**Beat 9 — Discovering Mama Flè.** The priestess awakens from centuries of suspended animation. She knows everything the heroes have done — she's been watching from her half-state. She explains the full backstory, the stakes, and what needs to be done. She gives one hero a magical dagger that grants the ability to perform the sealing ceremony and temporarily command zombie pirates with a willpower check. Then she dissolves into dust.

**Beat 10 — Looting and resetting the wards.** The heroes explore the ship and collect what treasure they can. They must also gather the five gold coins (each slightly different) and restore them to the ward statues placed around the island. The exact matching of coin to statue doesn't actually matter — but heroes won't know that until they receive or interpret the dagger's knowledge.

**Beat 11 — The ritual.** At the center of the island, the dagger-bearer begins the sealing ceremony in two stages. Stage 1 is a series of opposed willpower/power checks over several rounds — each round, a new Shambler appears for the rest of the party to fight. Stage 2 is an extended concentration check, with the number of remaining Shamblers increasing the magical cost per round. Some heroes may go insane. The caster may begin spending their own vitality if their magical reserves run dry. Three possible outcomes: success, success-with-sacrifice (Howard or another PC), or total failure.

**Beat 12 — Epilogue.** The island fades back into its pocket dimension. The dagger-bearer is now the Guardian — attuned to the island, able to return to it at will. Possible long-term hook: they must return periodically or risk the wards weakening. If they go insane, they may be drawn into permanent guardianship, entering suspended animation — a dramatic way to retire a character.

## Setting conversion notes

|Pulp Cthulhu element|Possible equivalent|
|---|---|
|Dimensional Shamblers|Demons / shadow creatures / vampiric spirits / fey hunters|
|The jellyfish entity (elder god)|A sleeping devil / an ancient vampire lord / a forgotten god|
|Mama Flè (Voodoo priestess)|Any arcane caster archetype; druid, witch, cleric|
|Zombie pirates|Undead, cursed soldiers, haunted remnants|
|The pocket dimension|The Feywild / the Shadowfell / a demonic demiplane / the Umbra|
|The ward statues|Binding seals, holy shrines, arcane monoliths|
|The gold coins|Key fragments, soul gems, ritual components|
|The magical dagger|A bound weapon, a divine focus, an artifact|
|Sanity loss|Madness/corruption mechanics, stress, Humanity loss|
|The Ghost Island|Any isolated, supernaturally hidden location|

The core structure — _call for help → investigation → isolated location → discovery of ancient wrong → ritual under pressure → epilogue with ongoing consequence_ — translates cleanly to nearly any system with minimal fiction changes.
