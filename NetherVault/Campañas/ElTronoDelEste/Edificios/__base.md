Here's how I'd map the lodging chain before writing stats: **Campsite → Homestead → Townhouse → Keep**. The Campsite is scrappy survival, Homestead is modest permanent housing, Townhouse is your multi-floor urban density, and the Keep doubles as lodging and minor defense — which also ties nicely into your military buildings thematically.
