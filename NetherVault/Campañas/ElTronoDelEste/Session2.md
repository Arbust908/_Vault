## El Viaje a las Montañas — Tres Encuentros

### Encounter 1: La Emboscada del Bandido

**Timing:** Day 5-6 of travel **Setup:** Bandits (or goblin raiders) hit the supply wagons at a narrow pass. This is where **Sylara's scouting pays off** — she spotted them half a day ahead, laid traps, marked positions with arrows.

**El Mecánico:**

- Combat starts with the bandit leader already wounded/pinned by Sylara's arrow
- 2-3 traps are active on the battlefield (caltrops, pitfalls, tripwires)
- Players get advantage on initiative because Sylara warned them
- The goblin player character could recognize bandits as rival clan or know their tactics

**Resultado:** Supplies are defended, the party gains confidence in Sylara, and they learn bandits _are_ active in the region.

---

### Encounter 2: El Desafío del Clima

**Timing:** Day 10-12 **Setup:** A mountain storm hits. Not combat — a **skill challenge**. The party needs to navigate treacherous terrain, keep the wagons from sliding off cliffs, protect supplies from water damage, find shelter.

**El Mecánico:**

- DC 12 checks: Survival (path-finding), Strength (securing wagons), Constitution (endurance)
- **Cael the Engineer advantage:** He can suggest engineering solutions — build temporary barriers, reinforce wagons, plan route
- **Bram the Herbalist advantage:** He can identify safe plants for shelter, medicinal uses
- **Sylara advantage:** She knows the safest route through the mountain pass
- Failure means lost supplies or injury — consequences, not death

**Resultado:** The storm passes, the party realizes they're genuinely dependent on these Experts, and they lose some resources (adds survival pressure later).

---

### Encounter 3: El Encuentro del Goblin Scout

**Timing:** Day 18-20 (near the mountain pass) **Setup:** A goblin scout from the Troll King's forces encounters the party. Initial hostility — but when they see the **goblin player character**, the scout stops.

**El Diálogo:**

- The scout recognizes the goblin PC's settlement/clan markings
- _"You're with the humans? Why?"_
- Potential outcomes:
    - **Info trade:** The scout shares what they know about Gorruk's movements, Clan positions, goblin settlements in the area
    - **Warning:** _"The Troll King knows there are humans coming. He doesn't care yet. But the Clans are watching."_
    - **Complication:** The scout has orders to report back. Does the goblin PC let them go? Capture them? Pay them off?

**El Mecánico:**

- This is roleplay-heavy, but if combat happens: the scout is alone and outmatched — they'll try to flee
- No combat advantage here — but **information advantage** if the players play it right
- The goblin PC's choice sets the tone for goblin relations going forward

**Resultado:** The party gets intelligence on the political landscape before arriving, and the goblin PC's choices matter immediately.
