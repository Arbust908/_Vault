# El Trono del Este — Mazo de Eventos del Asentamiento

## Trigger Mechanic

| Settlement Size | Buildings | Events Drawn per Conquest Turn |
|----------------|-----------|-------------------------------|
| **Campamento** | 1–5 | 1 |
| **Aldea** | 6–12 | 2 |
| **Pueblo** | 13–20 | 3 |
| **Ciudad** | 21+ | 4 |

Draw from a shuffled deck. Once drawn, an event is resolved and set aside. Reshuffle only when the deck runs out or when the settlement reaches a new size tier.

---

## ☀️ FORTUNA (Good Events) — 14 Cards

### F01 — La Caravana Perdida
A merchant caravan that was thought lost stumbles into the settlement, grateful and laden with goods.
**Effect:** Gain a one-time bonus of supplies (equivalent to +2 gold or +2 of any resource the players choose).
**Resolution options:**
- Welcome them and trade fairly → they may return as a recurring trade route
- Press them for a larger "toll" → more resources now, but word spreads that the colony is greedy

---

### F02 — Vetas de Fortuna
Workers discover a previously unknown mineral vein while digging foundations.
**Effect:** One empty square on the grid is now marked as an ore opportunity square.
**Resolution options:**
- Mine it immediately → +1 stone per turn but delays current construction by one turn
- Mark it for later → the opportunity remains but a rival faction may notice

---

### F03 — El Desertor
A soldier from another County (roll or choose which) arrives seeking asylum. They carry military intelligence.
**Effect:** Players gain information about one rival faction's troop movements or plans.
**Resolution options:**
- Grant asylum → gain intel, risk diplomatic incident if discovered
- Turn them away → safe politically but lose the intelligence
- Interrogate then release → partial intel, the deserter becomes a wildcard NPC

---

### F04 — Cosecha Abundante
An unexpectedly fertile season produces a bumper crop from the farms.
**Effect:** +2 people next conquest turn (settlers attracted by abundance) or +1 people permanently.
**Resolution options:**
- Share the surplus openly → attracts new settlers, boosts morale
- Stockpile for hard times → no immediate bonus but creates a reserve against future bad events
- Trade the surplus → convert into gold or building materials

---

### F05 — El Artesano Errante
A master craftsperson (blacksmith, carpenter, stonemason — match to campaign needs) wanders in looking for work.
**Effect:** One building currently under construction completes immediately, OR one existing building gets a free upgrade.
**Resolution options:**
- Hire them permanently → the benefit is ongoing but costs 1 gold upkeep
- Commission a single project → one-time benefit, they move on
- Ask them to train your workers → no immediate benefit, but future buildings of that type cost less

---

### F06 — Alianza Inesperada
A small barbarian clan (or displaced refugee group) sends an emissary offering cooperation.
**Effect:** Opens a diplomacy track with one of the Eastern Wilds factions.
**Resolution options:**
- Accept openly → begin alliance negotiations, other factions may react
- Accept secretly → alliance without political exposure, but trust is slower to build
- Decline respectfully → no alliance but no hostility either

---

### F07 — La Fuente Oculta
Explorers discover a hidden spring or underground water source within the grid.
**Effect:** One square gains a permanent water feature. Buildings adjacent to it get +1 to output.
**Resolution options:**
- Build around it → permanent bonus zone
- Consecrate it (if Temple/Shrine exists) → morale boost, minor divine blessing
- Keep it secret → strategic reserve for drought or siege

---

### F08 — Voluntarios del Camino
Word of the colony's success reaches the homeland. A wave of volunteers arrives.
**Effect:** +3 people immediately.
**Resolution options:**
- Welcome all → more labor but strain on food/housing
- Screen carefully → +2 people instead of 3, but guaranteed quality workers
- Send some back with a message → fewer people, but creates a propaganda pipeline to the kingdom

---

### F09 — El Mapa del Viejo
An elder from a nearby barbarian clan offers a hand-drawn map of the surrounding region in exchange for medicine or supplies.
**Effect:** Reveal 2–3 hexes on the wider exploration map, including one point of interest.
**Resolution options:**
- Trade fairly → map + potential ongoing relationship with the elder
- Copy the map and refuse payment → map but hostility from the clan
- Overpay generously → map + the elder becomes a recurring friendly NPC contact

---

### F10 — Noche de Estrellas
A rare celestial event — meteor shower, aurora, planetary alignment — is visible from the settlement. Settlers gather to watch.
**Effect:** Morale boost. If a Shrine or Temple exists, the clergy interprets it as a divine sign (+1 morale for 2 turns).
**Resolution options:**
- Declare a festival → morale bonus extended, but one turn of reduced productivity
- Let it pass naturally → standard morale boost
- The Wizard Tower (if exists) studies it → reveals a minor arcane secret or spell component location

---

### F11 — Madera Canción
Loggers report that certain trees in the nearby forest produce wood with unusual acoustic properties — perfect for instruments, resonant construction, or arcane focuses.
**Effect:** Lumber output gains a secondary "rare wood" tag. Can be sold for premium gold or used for special building upgrades.
**Resolution options:**
- Harvest carefully → sustainable rare wood supply
- Harvest aggressively → more immediate output but risk of angering Fey
- Report to the Wizard Tower → unlocks a special arcane building material

---

### F12 — El Refugio
A family fleeing a dangerous situation (monster attack, bandit raid, rival faction persecution) arrives begging for shelter.
**Effect:** +1 people, plus a quest hook related to whatever they're fleeing from.
**Resolution options:**
- Shelter them → gain people and a quest hook, but whatever pursued them may follow
- Help them move on → no people gained, but gain goodwill with refugee networks
- Investigate what they're fleeing → immediate scouting mission, possible early warning of a threat

---

### F13 — Pacto del Río
The river fishermen report an unusually productive season — schools of mountain trout fill the shallows.
**Effect:** Food surplus for 2 turns. If a Fishery exists, it produces double output.
**Resolution options:**
- Fish heavily → maximum food, but risk depleting the stock for next season
- Fish sustainably → moderate food bonus, guaranteed returns next season
- Smoke and preserve the surplus → creates a tradeable good worth gold

---

### F14 — El Camino se Abre
A natural landslide or clearing reveals a previously blocked path through the mountains.
**Effect:** A new expansion direction is unlocked (previously inaccessible squares become available).
**Resolution options:**
- Explore immediately → faster expansion but unprepared for what's on the other side
- Send scouts first → slower but safer, reveal what lies beyond
- Fortify the opening → defensive position on a new border, expansion delayed

## 🔥 ADVERSIDAD (Bad Events) — 14 Cards

### A01 — Lluvia Negra
Unseasonable storms batter the settlement for days. Flooding, mud, damaged foundations.
**Effect:** One building under construction is delayed by 1 turn. One existing building takes minor damage (reduced output for 1 turn).
**Resolution options:**
- All hands on repair → fix damage quickly, but no new construction this turn
- Prioritize critical buildings → choose which building gets repaired first
- Let the storm pass → damage persists an extra turn but normal operations continue

---

### A02 — Los Merodeadores
A bandit group has been raiding supply lines between the settlement and the kingdom.
**Effect:** No trade income this turn. If a Trading Post exists, it produces nothing.
**Resolution options:**
- Send a military unit to clear the road → costs 1 military action, road secured
- Pay them off → costs gold, road cleared temporarily, but they'll be back
- Reroute supplies → trade resumes next turn via longer path, costs extra

---

### A03 — Plaga de Ratones
Vermin infest the food stores. Grain rots, morale drops.
**Effect:** -1 people (settlers leave due to food shortage) or -1 food output for 2 turns.
**Resolution options:**
- The Herbalist creates a poison → problem solved in 1 turn, but poison is near food supply
- Bring in cats/hunting animals → slower fix (2 turns) but permanent prevention
- Burn the affected stores → immediate fix, but lose stored surplus

---

### A04 — Descontento
Settlers are unhappy — overworked, underfed, or homesick. Grumbling turns to open complaints.
**Effect:** -1 morale. If morale drops below a threshold, settlers refuse to work on non-essential buildings.
**Resolution options:**
- Hold a town meeting → Diplomático or Comerciante can resolve it with a speech (skill check)
- Grant a day off → lost productivity but morale restored
- Crack down → morale stabilizes through fear, but trust drops long-term

---

### A05 — El Derrumbe
A section of mountain slope collapses near the settlement. Dust, debris, blocked paths.
**Effect:** 1–2 squares on the mountain edge become permanently unbuildable. If buildings were there, they're damaged.
**Resolution options:**
- Clear the rubble → costs 1 turn of labor, squares become buildable again (but risky)
- Abandon those squares → permanent loss but safe
- The Engineer reinforces the slope → prevents future collapses in that area, costs stone

---

### A06 — Emboscada en el Paso
A patrol or supply convoy is ambushed in the mountain pass. Casualties.
**Effect:** Lose 1 military unit or -2 people.
**Resolution options:**
- Send reinforcements → rescue survivors, but leave settlement less defended
- Fortify the pass → prevent future ambushes, costs stone and 1 military action
- Investigate who attacked → reveals whether it was bandits, barbarians, or something else

---

### A07 — La Sequía
The river flow drops dramatically — snowmelt is less than expected this season.
**Effect:** Water-dependent buildings (Farm, Fishery) produce at half output for 2 turns.
**Resolution options:**
- Ration water → reduced output but no building shutdowns
- Dig a well → costs 1 turn and labor, provides backup water source permanently
- Pray at the Shrine/Temple → possible divine intervention (DM decides)

---

### A08 — Espías en la Niebla
The settlement discovers signs that someone has been watching them — tracks, a cold campfire, a carved symbol.
**Effect:** No immediate mechanical effect, but creates tension. Next conquest turn, a Bad event is guaranteed unless addressed.
**Resolution options:**
- The Explorer tracks them → identify who's watching (faction reveal)
- Set a trap → capture the spy, gain intelligence
- Increase patrols → costs 1 military action, prevents the guaranteed bad event
- Ignore it → guaranteed bad event next turn (could be an attack, sabotage, or theft)

---

### A09 — Fiebre del Valle
A sickness sweeps through the settlement — chills, fever, weakness. Not deadly but debilitating.
**Effect:** -1 to all building outputs for 2 turns. Workers are sick.
**Resolution options:**
- The Herbalist treats them → recovery in 1 turn instead of 2, costs herbs/supplies
- Quarantine the sick → prevents spread, but those workers are unavailable
- Push through it → normal timeline, but risk of the sickness becoming worse (draw another bad event)

---

### A10 — Fuego en la Noche
A fire breaks out — arson, accident, or lightning strike. One building is ablaze.
**Effect:** One building (randomly determined or DM choice) is destroyed unless addressed immediately.
**Resolution options:**
- All hands fight the fire → building saved but damaged (reduced output for 2 turns), no other work this turn
- Sacrifice the building to save adjacent ones → building lost, others safe
- If Watchtower exists → early warning, fire contained before it spreads, building only damaged

---

### A11 — El Tributo
A barbarian clan sends a delegation demanding tribute — food, gold, or weapons — in exchange for not raiding.
**Effect:** Pay the tribute or face a raid next turn.
**Resolution options:**
- Pay → lose resources, peace for now, but sets a precedent
- Refuse and prepare for war → raid incoming, but you keep your resources
- Negotiate → the Diplomático tries to establish a trade relationship instead (skill check)
- Pay but send a message → tribute paid, but the Militar organizes a show of force to discourage future demands

---

### A12 — Sabotaje
A building is found sabotaged — tools broken, foundations cracked, supplies spoiled. Someone inside the settlement did this.
**Effect:** One building is non-functional for 1 turn while repairs happen. Morale drops as suspicion spreads.
**Resolution options:**
- Investigate internally → identify the saboteur (could be a Condesa agent, disgruntled settler, or rival faction spy)
- Repair and move on → building fixed but saboteur remains active
- Declare martial law → saboteur caught quickly, but morale drops further and trust erodes

---

### A13 — La Bestia
A dangerous creature (mountain lion, wyvern, troll scout) is spotted near the settlement. Settlers are afraid to work outside the walls.
**Effect:** Resource-gathering buildings outside the core settlement produce nothing until the threat is addressed.
**Resolution options:**
- Hunt it → combat encounter, threat eliminated
- Drive it away → easier, but it may return
- Fortify and wait → production halted for 1–2 turns until the creature moves on naturally

---

### A14 — Grietas en la Alianza
Tensions between two Expert NPCs boil over into open disagreement during council. Their conflicting advice paralyzes decision-making.
**Effect:** Players can only build N-1 buildings this turn (one slot lost to internal conflict).
**Resolution options:**
- Side with one Expert → the other's morale drops, their associated buildings are less effective for 2 turns
- Mediate → Diplomático or a player with high Charisma can resolve it (skill check), no slot lost
- Let them fight it out → conflict resolves naturally next turn, but the "losing" Expert holds a grudge

---

## 🌀 LO EXTRAÑO (Weird Events) — 14 Cards

### E01 — Las Luces del Paso
Strange lights are seen floating above the mountain pass at night. They move with apparent purpose, circling the settlement before drifting east.
**Effect:** No mechanical effect yet. But if players investigate, they discover a Fey marker or ley line crossing.
**Resolution options:**
- Follow the lights → exploration encounter, possible Fey contact
- Ward the settlement → Shrine/Temple can create protections, lights stop but so does potential Fey connection
- Observe and document → the Sabio records the pattern, may reveal something useful later

---

### E02 — El Susurro de la Piedra
Workers quarrying stone report hearing whispers from deep within the rock — not threatening, but unsettling. The stone they extract has faint, naturally occurring rune-like patterns.
**Effect:** Stone gathered this turn has a "runic" quality. If used in a Shrine, Temple, or Wizard Tower, that building gains a minor bonus.
**Resolution options:**
- Use the runic stone → building bonus, but the whispers get louder
- Stop quarrying that vein → whispers stop, no bonus, but workers are relieved
- The Sabio investigates → reveals lore about the First King's original constructions (they used this same stone)

---

### E03 — El Árbol que Camina
A massive, ancient tree at the edge of the settlement has moved overnight. Not fallen — *moved*. Its roots have shifted ten meters to the south.
**Effect:** One grid square's terrain changes (buildable becomes difficult, or vice versa). The tree is now in a different position.
**Resolution options:**
- Leave it alone → it may move again (ongoing weird terrain shifts)
- The Herbalist examines it → discovers it's a dormant treant or Fey guardian, opens a dialogue
- Cut it down → the wood is exceptional quality (+bonus), but something in the forest noticed

---

### E04 — Lluvia de Peces
Fish rain from the sky for exactly seven minutes. Mountain trout, still alive, flopping on rooftops and in the streets.
**Effect:** Immediate food surplus (one-time bonus). Settlers are bewildered.
**Resolution options:**
- Celebrate → morale boost, settlers tell the story for years
- Investigate the sky → the Wizard Tower or Sabio discovers a planar thin spot above the settlement
- Check the river → something upstream displaced the fish (possible monster, dam, Fey prank)

---

### E05 — El Reflejo en el Lago
Settlers report that the lake's surface shows a reflection that doesn't match reality — buildings that don't exist yet, people who aren't there, a tower on the horizon.
**Effect:** No immediate mechanical effect. The reflection shows the settlement as it *could* be — a prophetic vision or a Fey mirror.
**Resolution options:**
- Study the reflection → the Sabio can identify one building that would be "optimal" to build next (free strategic hint)
- Ignore it → the reflections fade after a few days
- The Explorer investigates the lakeshore → discovers a submerged Fey artifact or old ruin beneath the water

---

### E06 — Sueños Compartidos
Multiple settlers report having the same dream — a woman's voice singing in an unknown language, a crown of antlers, a door in a tree.
**Effect:** Morale is unsettled. Workers are tired from restless sleep (-1 productivity for 1 turn).
**Resolution options:**
- The Sabio interprets → Aelindra connection, advances the Fey storyline
- The Herbalist brews sleeping draughts → dreams stop, productivity restored, but the message is lost
- A player character who sleeps at the settlement has the dream too → DM delivers a private vision (major lore drop)

---

### E07 — El Mercader de las Sombras
A merchant arrives at dusk with a cart full of goods that are *too* good — masterwork tools, rare herbs, enchanted trinkets. His prices are fair. His shadow doesn't match his movements.
**Effect:** Players can buy exceptional goods at normal prices. But there's a catch.
**Resolution options:**
- Buy freely → goods are real and useful, but the merchant asks for "a future favor" (DM bank)
- Refuse to trade → merchant leaves peacefully, goods gone, no consequences
- Investigate → the merchant is Fey, or a servant of someone in the Feywild, or a trickster spirit. What they learn depends on how they approach

---

### E08 — La Canción del Río
The river begins producing a low, harmonic hum — audible at dawn and dusk. It's beautiful. Some settlers find it calming. Others find it deeply unsettling.
**Effect:** Settlers are divided. Half gain morale, half lose it (net neutral). But the Fishery produces double this turn — fish are drawn to the vibration.
**Resolution options:**
- Let it continue → ongoing divided morale but great fishing
- Find the source → upstream investigation reveals a natural resonance chamber or something deliberately placed
- The Shrine/Temple harmonizes with it → if consecrated, the sound becomes uniformly calming (+1 morale)

---

### E09 — Huellas al Revés
Footprints appear overnight around the settlement perimeter — humanoid, barefoot, but walking *backward*. They lead from the settlement outward and vanish at the treeline.
**Effect:** No mechanical effect. Pure creep factor. But if ignored for 2 turns, something follows the trail back in.
**Resolution options:**
- Track them immediately → encounter at the treeline (Fey, spirit, or something else)
- Ward the perimeter → Shrine/Temple or Wizard Tower can prevent whatever made them from returning
- Set a watch → catch the visitor next time they come, on your terms

---

### E10 — El Niño Perdido
A child is found wandering near the settlement — alone, well-fed, clean clothes, but speaking a dialect no one recognizes. They don't seem distressed.
**Effect:** +1 people (if adopted). But the child knows things they shouldn't — layout of buildings not yet built, names of people not yet arrived.
**Resolution options:**
- Adopt them into the settlement → mysterious but harmless (or is it?), Fey connection likely
- Try to find their parents → exploration encounter, leads to a hidden community or Fey crossing
- The Sabio questions them carefully → the child reveals fragments of prophecy or history about the First King

---

### E11 — La Niebla que Recuerda
A thick fog rolls in that doesn't behave naturally — it avoids buildings, pools in empty squares, and settlers swear they see shapes of structures that aren't there within it.
**Effect:** Empty squares in the fog show ghostly impressions of what was built there *before* — ruins of the original kingdom, centuries ago.
**Resolution options:**
- Map the ghostly buildings → reveals the original layout of the First King's settlement (historical lore + building placement hints)
- Disperse the fog → Wizard Tower or divine magic clears it, but the information is lost
- Build on one of the ghost-squares → the building inherits a minor enchantment from its spectral predecessor

---

### E12 — Cosecha Extraña
The farm produces a crop no one planted — luminescent mushrooms, black wheat, silver berries. Edible, nutritious, but *wrong*.
**Effect:** Food surplus, but eating it causes vivid, prophetic dreams for 1 turn.
**Resolution options:**
- Eat it → food bonus + every settler (including PCs) has a prophetic dream (DM delivers individual visions)
- Sell it → rare goods worth premium gold to the right buyer
- The Herbalist studies it → identifies it as a Feywild crop, meaning the planes are bleeding together at this location
- Destroy it → safe, boring, but whatever planted it takes notice

---

### E13 — El Eco del Primer Rey
During construction, workers unearth a sealed stone box containing a single item — a signet ring, a journal page, a child's toy — bearing the mark of the First King.
**Effect:** Historical artifact found. Advances the Three Pillars storyline.
**Resolution options:**
- The Sabio studies it → lore revelation about the First King's reign, one of the three pillars is clarified
- Show it to the barbarian clans → massive diplomacy boost if handled respectfully
- Keep it secret → artifact secured, but its significance isn't fully understood yet
- Show it to the Condesa → she reacts... interestingly (reveals how much she knows or doesn't know about the true path to the throne)

---

### E14 — El Duelo a Medianoche
At exactly midnight, two spectral figures appear in the settlement square and fight a duel — swords, magic, fury. They're translucent, clearly echoes of the past. They fight until one falls, then both vanish.
**Effect:** The square where they fought is now a "haunted" location. Buildings built there gain +1 output but occasionally malfunction or produce strange results.
**Resolution options:**
- Build on the haunted square → bonus with risk
- Consecrate the ground → ghosts laid to rest, square becomes normal
- Observe nightly → the duel repeats with slight variations each time, eventually revealing the identity of the combatants (tied to campaign lore)
- The Wizard Tower studies the residue → arcane insight, possible quest hook to fully resolve the haunting

## Deck Management Notes

**Shuffling:** Shuffle all 42 cards together. Draw the appropriate number per conquest turn based on settlement size.

**Escalation:** As the campaign progresses, the DM can remove early-game cards (like F01 — La Caravana Perdida) and add custom cards that tie into the current political situation (Condesa's sabotage, Troll King's scouts, Fey incursions).

**Recurring events:** Some events (A08 — Espías en la Niebla, A11 — El Tributo) can be reshuffled back in if unresolved, representing ongoing threats.

**Linked events:** The DM can chain events narratively. If E06 — Sueños Compartidos is drawn, the next Weird event drawn is automatically E05 — El Reflejo en el Lago (the Fey influence is escalating). These chains are optional but create emergent storylines.

**Custom cards:** Leave 8–10 blank cards in the deck for the DM to add mid-campaign based on player decisions, faction movements, or story developments.
