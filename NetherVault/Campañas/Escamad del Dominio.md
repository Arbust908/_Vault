## 🐉 Scales of Domination

**Description** You didn't choose to wash up on Stormwreck Isle. But you all had the same nightmare before the ship went down — a shadow with five heads swallowing the sky. An old man greets you on the beach, the last keeper of a crumbling temple, with a story about a world that used to belong to dragons. Then someone goes missing in the direction he told you never to go. And the adventure begins before you've had time to dry off.

**Backstory** Dragons are myth. Most people alive have never seen one, and those who claim to have are old, and quietly doubted. But myths have roots, and something ancient lives in the hills of Stormwreck Isle — patient, territorial, and stirred. Meanwhile, across the sea in the mainland town of Greenest, a cult has been growing in the shadow of that same myth. They call her the Dragon Queen. They believe her return is not a threat but a salvation. They are organized, fanatical, and they have already begun. The island is a prologue. Greenest is where the real story starts — and the nightmare you all shared was not a coincidence.

**Theme** The age of dragons never truly ended — it only slept. A story about awe and terror, about what it means to stand between the world and something that believes it owns it.

**Player Buy-In** Characters should be people looking for a fresh start — drawn to the promise of safety, of belonging, of a place to call home. Whether that home is Greenest, the island, or just the people beside them is up to each player. Characters with ties to Greenest — family, history, roots — will feel this most keenly. Most importantly: you all shared that nightmare. A five-headed shadow eating the world. If your character takes that seriously, you belong here. This adventure rewards those who fight for something.
