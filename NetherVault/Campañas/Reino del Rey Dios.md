##👑 Against the God King

**Description** A town called Oreo sends word: people are missing. People are changing. Something is wrong in the streets at night, and no one who investigates comes back the same. You have ties here — family, history, a reason to care — so you go to find answers. What you find instead is a cult that worships something older than the town itself. And when you think you've won, when you think the threat is contained, you discover the cult was never the real enemy. It was just the first note in a much darker song.

**Backstory** The Lizard King has slept for centuries, entombed in the deep places of the world, waiting for the day his power would return. The cult of the Reptile God does not worship a god — they worship him, or what they believe him to be. They have been patient, gathering followers, preparing the way. The Naga generals who lead the cult are not monsters who stumbled into power — they are his chosen instruments, generals in a war that has already begun. And somewhere in the frozen ruins of an ancient keep, a weapon waits — the Silver Sword, forged to pierce what mortal steel cannot. The Lizard King is not undead in any way mortals understand. He is something older: part vampire, part lich, something that should not exist and yet does. And he is waking up.

**Theme** The horror of discovering that what you thought was the enemy was only the symptom. A story about curses that run deeper than blood, about ancient evils that were never truly defeated — only delayed.

**Player Buy-In** Characters should have a connection to Oreo — family, history, or simply a reason to care when things go wrong. More importantly: you should be comfortable carrying pieces of this curse with you. The threat doesn't end when the cult falls. It evolves. It persists. Characters who engage with the mystery, who push into the dark even when they're afraid, will find the answers they need. Those who turn away will watch the darkness spread.
