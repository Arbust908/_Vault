**Description** The frontier doesn't ask questions. Provision caravans come and go, settlements are sparse, and what happens between them stays between them. It's the perfect place to hide something catastrophic. Four cults have been here for years, working quietly, building toward something the world has no name for anymore. You've been drawn here — by rumor, by blood, by a half-remembered legend of heroes who sealed away an elemental god at the cost of everything. The seal is older now. And it has been feeling the pressure.

**Backstory** Ages ago, a coalition of heroes bound four primordial titans and sealed their combined power — the Elemental Cataclysm — beneath an old chapel outside a village so small it barely appears on maps. The victory became legend. The legend became myth. The chapel became a ruin. Now four cults, each devoted to a titan, have quietly taken root across the forgotten frontier — operating in the shadows of supply routes, using the isolation of the land as cover. Each has their own temple, their own agenda, their own elemental symbol — an artifact weapon of terrible power. They do not trust each other. But someone does — a fifth, hidden faction known only as the Cult of the Elemental Eye, who believes that unity is the only path to the Cataclysm. They are patient. They are watching. And they are closer to success than anyone knows.

**Theme** A world that forgot its greatest wound, and the rot that grew in that forgetting. A race against four clocks at once — and the creeping horror that stopping one may just give the others time to finish the job.

**Player Buy-In** Characters should have a reason to be in the frontier — a tie to one of the cults, a family connection to the region, or the pull of a legend about heroes who gave everything to stop the end of the world. This adventure rewards curiosity and urgency in equal measure. The world does not wait for the players to be ready — every choice to act is also a choice about what you're leaving unattended. Characters who engage, investigate, and take the threat seriously will thrive. Those who ignore the horizon will watch it darken.
