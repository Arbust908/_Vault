# Elle “Ice Queen” Von Haft

## Appearance
Standing at a deceptive 162cm and 60kg, Elle cuts a lean silhouette with piercing amber eyes. Her natural brunette hair is now dyed silver, complementing her pale complexion. Day to day, she wears a tailored light grey blazer over a crisp white blouse paired with a black pencil skirt and dura-glass heels. Frosted blue nails match her icy demeanor, while lilac lipstick and eye shadow complete the look—creating an almost frozen, untouchable appearance that lives up to her "Ice Queen" moniker.

## CyberMods
Elle sports a [[Neural Enhancer]] for complex multi-thought macros and a mostly esteticly used [[Cloaking Chameleon Dermis]].

## Personality

Elle is the epitome of corporate coldness. She is calculating, ruthless, and always three steps ahead in the game of corporate espionage. Her demeanor is icy, rarely showing emotion, which makes her a formidable negotiator and a dangerous adversary. She values efficiency and results above all else, often willing to make morally ambiguous decisions to achieve her goals. All these are a big and thick cover for her ture nature, an insecure girl that fear that if she shows any weakness she will be devoured by the corporate sharks that surround her.
