Shadowheart is a complex half-elf cleric of Shar whose origins and memories have been deliberately erased to protect the secrets of her order. Born Jenevelle Hallowleaf, she bears a hidden wound and harbors a deep fear of wolves, yet remains unwaveringly devoted to her dark mission. Clad in dark leather and chainmail, she balances a private, calculating nature with flickers of compassion, all underscored by her signature black-and-purple Sharran iconography.

## Vital Statistics
- **Full Name:** Jenevelle Hallowleaf
- **Aliases:** Shadowheart
- **Age:** Over 40 years (Half-elf longevity)
- **Height:** Approximately 5′7″ (170 cm)
- **Build:** Lean, athletic
- **Species:** High Half-Elf
- **Pronouns:** She/Her
- **Place of Origin:** Baldur’s Gate (Cloister of Sombre Embrace, Lower City)

## Personality Matrix
- **Adjectives:** Devout, Calculating, Private, Conflicted

Shadowheart maintains a formal distance, revealing little about her past while meticulously planning each step of her mission. She responds pragmatically to suffering—willing to heal or manipulate as needed—yet shuns needless cruelty, torn between her unyielding faith and the compassion she secretly craves.

## Appearance & Visual Motifs
- **Skin Tone:** Pale, cool undertones
- **Hair:** Long, raven-black, typically tied in a low ponytail
- **Eyes:** Emerald green
- **Notable Features:**  
  - Silver circlet engraved with Shar’s eye symbol
  - Faint, unhealing wound from her suppressed memories
- **Signature Color:** Black and purple, reflecting Sharran vestments

## Wardrobe Loadout
- **Daily Wear:** Dark leather jerkin over a chain shirt, leather boots, and a lightweight black cloak edged with subtle purple piping
- **Action Gear:** Studded leather armor, studded shield bearing the Shar emblem, morningstar mace strapped at the hip
- **Formal/Event Attire:** Long-sleeved deep-purple robe over a black velvet chemise, hooded black cloak with silver trim
- **Dress to Impress:** Form-fitting black silk gown with onyx and amethyst jewelry, reflecting candlelight with an almost hypnotic glint
- **One Surprising Outfit:** Simple white linen blouse and trousers, kept hidden—a reminder of her nameless life before the ritual

## Backstory Nuggets
- **Formative Event:** Underwent a memory-erasure ritual to safeguard Shar’s secrets
- **Personal Goal:** Deliver the mysterious artefact to the Cloister of Sombre Embrace
- **Hidden Fear:** Wolves—her mind recoils in terror within 30 ft of lupine creatures

## Voice & Mannerisms
- **Speech Patterns:** Measured, formal diction; seldom uses contractions
- **Catchphrase:** “My faith will keep me company.”
- **Gestures:** Fingers often trail the Shar symbol on her circlet when deep in thought
- **Posture:** Upright, arms frequently crossed—guarded and self-contained
