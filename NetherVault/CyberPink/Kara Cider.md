# Kara "Chimera" Cider

## Appearance
Standing at an impressive 186cm and weighing 87kg of pure muscle, Kara cuts an amazonian figure that commands attention in any room. Her short platinum blonde pixie cut frames a face marked by experience, drawing focus to her striking [[Providence Iris]] implants—deep blue with distinctive goat-like pupils that seem to glow with an inner fire. Her bronze skin tells a story written in scar tissue: a jagged line across her left collarbone, burn marks on her right shoulder, and countless smaller reminders of battles fought and survived. She wears these marks with visible pride. Her sleek black plated bodysuit clings to her muscular frame, reinforced at key points with combat-grade armor plating, while heavy metal boots announce her arrival with each deliberate step—a sound many have learned to recognize, and fear.

## Personality

Kara embodies raw bravado with a no-nonsense approach to life that cuts through pretense like her combat blades. She faces every situation with fierce confidence, whether negotiating a contract or diving headfirst into a firefight. Behind the intimidating exterior lies a sharp sense of dark-humor, self-deprecating, and often delivered with perfect timing to disarm both allies and adversaries. Unlike many mercs who hide their mistakes, Kara wears her failures as badges of honor alongside her physical scars, viewing each setback as a lesson carved into flesh and memory. This transparency makes her surprisingly approachable despite her fearsome reputation. Every action is driven by a singular ambition: to become a legend whose name is whispered with reverence long after her body gives out. It's not vanity—it's a hunger for immortality through deeds, a desire to prove that she mattered in a world that chews up and spits out the forgettable. Thats why she has allied with the [[Punga]] gang, becoming a guinea pig for their experimental cyberware in exchange for resources and priority on tech.
