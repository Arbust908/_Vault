**Description** The Rose Court hands you a charter, a map with very little on it, and a promise: conquer the Eastern Valleys and carve out a vassal state, and you will be rewarded with land, title, and power. What they don't tell you is that the valleys aren't empty. What they don't expect is that you might actually succeed. And what none of you can predict is what happens when the people who sent you start to realize they've made you too dangerous to keep.

**Backstory** The Eastern Valleys have resisted every incursion the kingdom has sent for generations. Not because of bad luck or poor planning — but because something already lives there. The Troll King rules his warband with cunning older than the kingdom itself. The Feys of the Grotto answer to no crown and never have. The Gigantkin of the Mountain Roots remember when men were a new thing in the world. These are not monsters to be cleared — they are civilizations, with grievances, with pride, with long memories. Somewhere beneath it all, buried in a valley no map names, an ancestral tomb waits in the dark. And in the capital, the Countess watches her brave little expedition with narrowing eyes, wondering what she'll do if they actually pull this off.

**Theme** What does it mean to build something in land that already belongs to someone else? A story about ambition, legacy, and the uncomfortable distance between founding a nation and becoming what you set out to fight.

**Player Buy-In** Characters should hunger for something — a name in the history books, a city that didn't exist before they arrived, a legacy carved from wilderness and will. This adventure is for players who want to plant a flag and watch something grow around it, who are excited by the idea that every decision they make shapes not just a dungeon but a civilization. Characters should be comfortable with moral complexity: the valleys are not empty, the court is not benevolent, and the right answer is rarely obvious. This is a story about what you're willing to build — and what you're willing to burn.
