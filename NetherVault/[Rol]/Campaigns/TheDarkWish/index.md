Here’s a four‐part, level-appropriate (PCs \~5–7) adventure outline inspired by Aladdin—flipped on its head—where an evil street‐urchin has claimed the lamp and is using its power to usurp the throne. The old Vizier (now exiled) enlists the party to storm the palace, break the enchantment on Princess Petal, and free the true Sultan.

---

## Adventure Background

Long ago, a lowly urchin named Razzick uncovered a fabled lamp and freed its Djinn of Envy. With Djinn’s power, he overthrew the Sultan, installed a Darling “Faux Prince” (his puppet), and has Princess Petal enchanted and guarding the palace gates alongside her enchanted royal tiger, Zahra. The former Vizier, Arimon, escaped with the real Sultan into hiding—but he lacks the force to retake the city. He begs the PCs to infiltrate the palace, break Petal’s mind-bond, defeat Razzick’s allies, and restore rightful rule.

---

## Adventure Hook

* **Wandering Market:** PCs witness strange flower-men stalking shoppers, led by a tiger. Arimon spots them and secretly recruits the party.
* **Missing Daughter:** The exiled Sultan’s daughter (Princess Petal) was last seen going to court—now nobody recognizes her. Arimon offers reward and titles.
* **Cursed Lamp Dreams:** Each PC has nightmares of a beautiful royal flower garden turning to ash, promising a legendary reward if they save the princess.

---

## Act I – The Vizier’s Plea & Investigation

1. **Secret Meeting.** In a ruined caravanserai, Arimon briefs PCs on the coup and enchants them with a protective ward against the Djinn’s magic.

2. **Gathering Intel.**

   * **The Sewers & Street Tunnels:** Fight Razzick’s street thugs (Bandit Scouts, CR 1/2) to capture a map of hidden palace tunnels.
   * **Market of Petals:** PCs must decode a floral cipher at the market (DC 15 Intelligence check) to find the palace’s secret gate.

3. **Side Twist – The Monkey Familiar.** In the market, PCs encounter a clever monkey (Familiar of the Faux Prince) who may be bribed or tricked (Animal Handling DC 13) into revealing a key to the palace’s back entrance.

---

## Act II – Infiltration & Inner Courtyard

1. **The Secret Gate.**

   * **Mechanical Bloom Trap:** A pressure‐plate opens giant metal petals (Poison Needle trap, DC 14 Dex save or 3d10 poison).
   * **Stealth Option:** PCs can avoid it with a group Stealth check (DC 16).

2. **Inner Gardens.**

   * **Hypnotized Guards:** 4 Royal Guards (CR 2 each) under charm. On sight, they issue a warning cry that could alert the whole palace (Perception DC 15 to spot them first).

3. **Puzzle of the Lotus Pools.**

   * Three pools with floating lotus: PCs must place colored petals in the right sequence (Arcana or Investigation DC 15) to deactivate a magical barrier protecting the throne hall.

---

## Act III – Confrontation in the Throne Room

1. **Princess Petal & Her Tiger Zahra.**

   * **Princess Petal, Enchanted Noble (CR 5):**

     * Melee: Enchanted rapier (1d8+4 + poison).
     * Spell-like: *Hold Person* (DC 15), *Aura of Hypnosis* (15-ft radius charm).
   * **Zahra, Enchanted Royal Tiger (CR 3):**

     * Pounce (DC 14 Dex to halve).
     * Roar: Frightening (Wisdom save DC 13).

   PCs can break the enchantment by shattering a crystal orb on the dais (DC 16 attack or Strength check), freeing Petal and Zahra mid-battle. If freed, Petal fights alongside PCs against the Faux Prince.

2. **The Faux Prince & His Djinn.**

   * **Faux Prince Salim (CR 6):** Martial swordsman with illusionary duplicates (Mirror Image) and smokescreen (Fog Cloud).
   * **Djinn of Envy (CR 8):**

     * Lair actions: swirling sands to blind (DC 15 Con), warp the chamber with illusory walls.
     * Legendary Actions: *Whirlwind*, *Dominate Person* (DC 16).

   PCs must prioritize destroying the lamp to weaken the Djinn (DC 18 Arcana or Tools (Tinker’s Tools) DC 15 to jury-rig a disabling ritual).

## Magic Items & Treasures

* **Lamp of Envy:** The source of the Djinn’s power. PCs can cleanse it to gain a one-use wish or banish the Djinn forever once shattered.
* **Blossom’s Tiara:** Grants wearer *Calm Emotions* once per day.
* **Sultan’s Signet Ring:** Allows safe passage through palace gates and command of one guard (once).
* **Gold, Jewels & Titles:** 2,000 gp reward, plus noble titles if they restore the Sultan.

---

## Optional Side Quests

* **Rescue the Royal Astrologer:** Imprisoned in the palace tower—knows a weakness of the Djinn (Advantage on saving throws vs. illusions).
* **Recover the Sultan’s Seal:** Hidden in the sewer temple, needed to endorse a decree restoring power.
* **The Flower-Queen’s Favor:** Benevolent dryad in the Oasis Garden can bestow *Plant Growth* to thicken palace grounds as a distraction.

---

### Running the Finale

* **Dynamic Environment:** Crumbling pillars (Dex save DC 14 or prone), shifting sands (terrain costs extra movement), guests fleeing through collapsing arches.
* **Moral Twist:** If PCs spare the Djinn instead of destroying the lamp, the Envy Djinn might vow a blood debt—hook for a future campaign.

---

Feel free to adjust encounter CRs, puzzle DCs, and treasure to match your table’s style and party level. Good luck—may your players outwit the urchin, rescue the princess, and bring the true Sultan back to his throne!

## Key Twists & NPCs

* **Razzick the Urchin (a.k.a. the Faux Prince)**
  The same cunning urchin who found the lamp has assumed the identity of “Prince Salim.” He uses both guile and Djinn magic to hold court.

* **Queen Amara (deceased, now Dryad Spirit)**
  Before her death, Queen Amara joined her husband in a secret pact with a powerful dryad to ensure the realm’s fertility and prosperity. When she fell ill, the dryad bound her spirit into the Grove of Whispers, where her voice can still aid—or haunt—the living.

* **Princess Petal (“Blossom”)**
  Enchanted by Razzick’s Djinn, she patrols the halls with her royal tiger Zahra, unaware of her mother’s sacrifice.

* **The Djinn of Envy**
  Bound to the lamp, it fuels Razzick’s ascent but hungers for more.

## Adventure Hooks (Revised)

1. **Whisper in the Grove:**
   In the Oasis Garden, PCs hear Queen Amara’s spirit urging them to break the curse—and pointing them to a hidden ritual site under the palace.

2. **Dryad’s Bargain:**
   The dryad, host to the queen’s spirit, offers aid (Plant Growth, Speak with Dead) if the PCs agree to restore a sacred grove desecrated by Razzick’s minions.

3. **Faux Summons:**
   Razzick publicly calls for challengers to “prove their worth” in a rigged tournament—an opportunity to breach the palace under cover of spectacle.

---

## Revised Act III – Throne Room Showdown

1. **Queen’s Echo:**
   As the battle unfolds, Queen Amara’s spirit can manifest through the dryad’s form, granting the PCs one momentary boon: **“Bloom of Remembrance”** (allies regain half their Hit Dice once).

2. **Single Villain Face-off:**

   * **Razzick the Urchin/Faux Prince (CR 8):**

     * **Lamp-Infused Blade:** 1d10+5 psychic damage, can cast *Dominate Person* (DC 16)
     * **Illusory Duplicates:** Mirror Image (3 images)
     * **Dark Pact Ritual (Lair Action):** Summon thorny vines (restrain, DC 15 Str)
     * **Legendary Actions:** Whirlwind Strike, Hypnotic Gaze (Wis save DC 15)

3. **Breaking the Pact:**
   To truly defeat him, the party must destroy the lamp **while** reciting the dryad’s blessing (Arcana or Religion DC 18), released from a scroll held by Queen Amara’s spirit.

---

## New Side Quest: “The Queen’s Grove”

* **Objective:** Restore the desecrated grove under the palace courtyard so Queen Amara’s spirit can fully aid the PCs.

* **Challenges:**

  * **Desecrated Statues:** Animated stone guardians (Guard, CR 3)
  * **Corrupt Roots:** Magical vines that drain life energy (each turn, 1d8 necrotic)

* **Reward:**

  * **Amara’s Blessing:** One-use **Revivify** effect on any fallen ally.
  * **Dryad’s Boon:** The ability to speak with plants (for the rest of the adventure).
