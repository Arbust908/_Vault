## Mistake categories

Use one category when a pattern repeats:

- `recognition`: Chose the wrong algorithmic pattern.
- `constraints`: Failed to clarify an assumption that changed the solution.
- `correctness`: Missed an invariant or edge case.
- `typescript`: Lost time on types, collections, or syntax.
- `complexity`: Misstated or failed to improve time or space cost.
- `testing`: Did not trace a case that would expose the bug.
- `communication`: Coded silently or explained decisions unclearly.
- `scope`: Over-engineered before establishing a correct baseline.
