To determine if your BRP Fork web app complies with Chaosium's ORC license, consider the following structured guidance:

1. ORC License Basics
Permitted Use: The ORC license allows modification, distribution, and commercialization of licensed content.

Requirements:

Attribution: Credit the original authors (e.g., "Based on Chaosium's ORC-licensed content").

License Inclusion: Distribute the full ORC license text with your project.

Separation of Content: Your original content (e.g., new rules, lore) remains yours and doesn’t fall under the ORC.

2. What You Can Do
Modify the BRP Engine: If Chaosium’s BRP mechanics are explicitly released under ORC, you can legally adapt them.

Monetize: Charge for your app or offer premium features, provided ORC-licensed content remains free (if required by the license; confirm specifics).

Host User-Generated Content: If users create custom content using your app, ensure their contributions don’t infringe on Chaosium’s non-ORC IP (e.g., Chaosium-specific settings like Call of Cthulhu).

3. Critical Restrictions
Avoid Chaosium Trademarks: Do not use terms like "Basic Role Playing," "Chaosium," or logos unless explicitly permitted. Rebrand your project (e.g., "BRP Fork" is likely acceptable if unrelated to their trademarks).

Non-ORC Content: Chaosium’s settings (e.g., Glorantha, Call of Cthulhu) are not covered by ORC. Ensure your app doesn’t include or reference these.

No Sub-Licensing: You can’t impose additional restrictions on the ORC-licensed content.

4. Action Steps
Verify BRP’s ORC Coverage: Confirm which parts of Chaosium’s BRP are under ORC (e.g., check Chaosium’s official statements or repositories).

Isolate ORC Content: Keep ORC-licensed material separate from your proprietary code/content in the app.

Add Disclaimers: Explicitly state that your app is not affiliated with Chaosium.

Consult a Lawyer: For a definitive legal review, especially if monetizing.

Example Compliance Checklist
✅ Attribution to Chaosium in your app’s credits/documentation.

✅ ORC license text included in your app’s terms or license file.

✅ No Chaosium trademarks used in branding.

✅ Only ORC-covered BRP mechanics are modified (no non-ORC settings/lore).

By adhering to these guidelines, you minimize the risk of legal issues. Chaosium can only "call you out" if you violate their IP (e.g., using non-ORC content or trademarks). Stay within ORC terms, and your project should be legally sound.
