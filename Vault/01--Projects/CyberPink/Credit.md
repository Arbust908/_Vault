The smallest division of digital currency. Colloquially called *Creds* or *Cs* , the average worker on [[New Astra]] earns 1350 credits per month while a mid-level [[Corpos]] may bring in between 20000 to 56000 credit per month. Mercenaries have no formally reported monthly credits but a small low level job is rated at 700 credits, a more complex job may go between 1000 to 5000 credits and a suicide mission job starts at 75000 credits
