The [[B-ld Corp]]’s Blackout Model, colloquially know as the “No [[Credit |Cred]] Motel” _(For it’s impossibly low price)_ , Is a pillar of the corporate ruination. A franchise of Motels found around [[New Astra]] , Mostly on the poorer parts for its cheap price. Still it’s price is couple with no infrastructure maintenance and a once a week automated clean up for each room.

The halls of most Blackout Motels are littered with sponsored vending machines for almost anything that can be sold.

The first floor is the main motel floor with small cramped rooms with a tiny bathroom and a small bedroom with a round bed that has mirrors both on the roof and the backing.  The first floor also usually has a hanging _”Discretion Guaranteed”_ that sound like a mocking to most clients.

The second floor is the “fancy” floor of the motel, getting a once every 3 day clean up rooms, that also have a bigger bathroom and bedroom. The bedroom also features a jacuzzi and a small kitchenette.
