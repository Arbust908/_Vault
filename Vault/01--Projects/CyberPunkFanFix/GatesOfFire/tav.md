## Vital Statistics
- **Full Name:** Sirina Vex’thara  
- **Aliases:** “Red Thorn,” “Whisper in the Dark”  
- **Age:** 23  
- **Height:** 5 ft 7 in (170 cm)  
- **Build:** Lithe, sinewy muscles with graceful proportions  
- **Species:** Tiefling (Infernal bloodline)  
- **Pronouns:** She/Her  
- **Place of Origin:** Outer City slums of Baldur’s Gate  

## Personality Matrix
- **Adjectives:** Cunning · Fiercely loyal · Restless · Self-effacing  
  
Though she strides into a crowded tavern with fearless swagger, she often ducks into corners to avoid prying eyes, betraying her need for privacy. Petty criminals and nobles alike find themselves laughing at her quips before she retreats, nursing doubts about her own worth. She tosses back challenges with a grin, often unpredictable, yet trembles behind closed doors, both craving the spotlight and terrified of its glare in equal measure.

## Appearance & Visual Motifs
- **Skin Tone:** Deep obsidian with garnet-red undertones that shimmer under torchlight  
- **Hair:** Waist-length charcoal waves fading to plum at the tips, usually half-braided  
- **Eyes:** Molten gold with vertical slits, flecks of ember in the irises  
- **Notable Features:**  
  - Curved ram-style horns rimmed in silver runes  
  - Pale scar tracing from left cheekbone to jawline  
  - Silver-inked infernal sigils winding down her right forearm  
- **Signature Color:** Crimson (accented in clothing linings and jewelry)

## Backstory Nuggets
- **Formative Event:** At ten, she unleashed latent infernal powers by swallowing a blood-touched relic during a heist gone wrong.  
- **Personal Goal:** To reclaim her family’s stolen crest from the Black Network and restore her bloodline’s honor.  
- **Hidden Fear:** That her infernal heritage will one day betray her friends—and consume her entirely.  

## Voice & Mannerisms
- **Speech Patterns:** Measured cadence, often laced with sardonic wit; occasionally drops words like “inevitable” or “unavoidable” for dramatic flourish.  
- **Catchphrase:** “Let’s dance with danger.”  
- **Gestures:** Twirls her tail when amused; brushes a lock of hair behind her horn when flustered.  
- **Posture:** Always upright, weight shifted confidently to one hip, ready to bolt at a moment’s notice.
