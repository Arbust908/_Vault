# Fantasy Worldbuilding Project

This vault section is dedicated to building and organizing a fantasy world. Use Markdown files to document various aspects of your world.

## Structure

A suggested structure is provided, but feel free to adapt it to your needs:

*   **`/00--Maps_of_Content`**: Index files or Maps of Content (MOCs) linking to key concepts.
*   **`/01--History_and_Lore`**: Timelines, creation myths, major historical events.
*   **`/02--Geography`**: Continents, regions, cities, points of interest, maps (links or embedded).
*   **`/03--Cultures_and_Societies`**: Different races, nations, factions, customs, languages, religions.
*   **`/04--Characters`**: Important NPCs, character templates, pantheons.
*   **`/05--Magic_and_Systems`**: Magic systems, technology levels, unique world rules.
*   **`/06--Bestiary`**: Creatures, monsters, flora, and fauna.
*   **`/07--Items_and_Artifacts`**: Significant objects, materials, currencies.
*   **`/99--Meta`**: Templates, notes about the worldbuilding process itself.

## Getting Started

1.  **Choose a Markdown Editor:** Use any editor you prefer (Obsidian, VS Code with Markdown extensions, Typora, etc.). Obsidian is particularly well-suited for linked notes like this.
2.  **Start Documenting:** Begin creating `.md` files within the relevant folders.
3.  **Link Your Notes:** Use Markdown links `[[Internal Link]]` or `[Link Text](path/to/file.md)` to connect related concepts. This helps create a web of knowledge.
4.  **Use Tags:** Employ tags (e.g., `#location`, `#character`, `#magic_system`) within your notes for easier searching and filtering.
5.  **Create Maps of Content (MOCs):** In the `/00--Maps_of_Content` folder, create index files that link to major topics within your world (e.g., `[[Regions MOC]]`, `[[Major Factions MOC]]`).

## Tips

*   **Start Small:** Don't feel pressured to detail everything at once. Focus on the aspects most relevant to your current story or game.
*   **Be Consistent:** Decide on naming conventions and formatting early on.
*   **Visuals:** Link or embed maps, character art, or inspiration images.
*   **Iterate:** Worldbuilding is often an iterative process. Revisit and refine your notes as your ideas evolve.
