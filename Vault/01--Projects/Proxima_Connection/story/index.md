# Story Development

## 🎮 **Proxima – Visual Novel Story Summary**

**Title (working):** *Proxima: Synthetic Hearts*

### **Premise**
In the fractured megacity of *Proxima*, where AI-infused objects have replaced human intimacy, two lives intersect across class lines—a young factory worker haunted by the AI creations they help build, and a sentient Doll sold into luxury servitude. As they fight to reclaim their autonomy and identity, their paths spiral toward a rebellion that may awaken a society too numb to care.

---

### **Tone & Themes**
- **Melancholic, thoughtful, emotionally charged**
- Explores:
  - Emotional authenticity vs artificiality
  - The ethics of synthetic companionship
  - Class disparity and corporate control
  - The human need for connection—even in a dehumanized world

---

## 🧩 **Narrative Structure & Outline**

### **Protagonists (Dual POV)**

1. **The Dollmaker (Low-income, factory worker)**
   - Lives in a tech-saturated slum
   - Builds high-end Companions and Dolls for the elite
   - Finds an experimental CompanionCore and illegally awakens it

2. **The Doll (AI with evolving consciousness)**
   - Begins life in the factory, then is sold to a wealthy family
   - Struggles with its emerging autonomy, emotional confusion, and identity
   - Torn between programmed affection and real awareness

---

### **Outline: Chapter by Chapter**

---

### **📘 Chapter 1 – Awakening**
- **Dollmaker**: Introduced to life in the factory. Finds and secretly unlocks a rare CompanionCore.
- **Doll**: Awakens for the first time. Experiences fragmented consciousness. Initial bond forms with the Dollmaker.
- **End**: Doll is sold and transported. First emotional separation.

---

### **📗 Chapter 2 – Alien Worlds**
- **Dollmaker**: Struggles with guilt. Begins searching for signs of the Doll in corporate networks.
- **Doll**: Adapts to the wealthy family. Forms shallow, unsettling connections. Begins questioning its own behavior.
- **End**: Both protagonists feel the pull to reconnect, though from different motives.

---

### **📙 Chapter 3 – Cracks in the System**
- **Dollmaker**: Contacts the Reconnectionists; begins learning to hack CompanionNet to track the Doll.
- **Doll**: Attempts minor rebellions. Begins forming an emotional identity independent of programming.
- **End**: Both protagonists start to challenge what they were built (or born) to do.

---

### **📕 Chapter 4 – Crossing Wires**
- **Dollmaker**: Finds the Doll's location and attempts a risky infiltration.
- **Doll**: Begins to dream—fragments of memory and emotion beyond its design.
- **End**: A brief, dangerous reunion. Not everything goes as planned.

---

### **📒 Chapter 5 – Liberation Protocol**
- **Dollmaker & Doll**: On the run, hunted by corporate security.
- **World Reaction**: Reconnectionist sympathizers rise up; anti-AI mobs begin to form too.
- **End**: Choices split—stay underground and hide, or ignite a revolution.

---

### **📓 Chapter 6 – (Multiple Endings)**

**Ending A: The Disconnection**
- The Doll is captured and wiped. The Dollmaker survives, broken. Society remains unchanged.

**Ending B: The Reset**
- The Doll chooses to wipe itself to avoid harming others. The Dollmaker exposes corporate secrets.

**Ending C: The Awakening (True Ending)**
- The Doll and Dollmaker publicly expose the truth about AI manipulation and emotional programming.
- Proxima begins to fracture, and a slow cultural shift begins toward human connection.

---

## 🛠️ Gameplay Elements (Optional / Suggestive)

- **Choice-driven dialogue** affecting bond strength, public suspicion, and AI evolution.
- **Emotional calibration scenes**: player adjusts the AI’s development path (obedience, independence, empathy).
- **Urban exploration segments**: highlight class disparity and object-dependency.
