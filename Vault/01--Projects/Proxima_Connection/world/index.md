## 🪐 **Proxima – Setting Summary**

### **Overview**
Set in a sprawling megacity in the mid-22nd century, *Proxima* explores a society where human-to-human connection has withered, replaced by deeply personal, emotionally adaptive relationships with AI-enhanced consumer objects. In this world, loneliness isn't a bug—it's a business model.

---

### **Core Concepts**

#### **1. Object Intimacy**
- Every personal item—from phones to furniture—contains a **CompanionCore**, a micro-AI that evolves emotionally through daily interactions.
- People bond with their belongings on a level that rivals or surpasses human relationships.
- Conversations with toasters, emotional support from coats, and car rides filled with vulnerable confessions are all part of daily life.

#### **2. Corporate Manipulation**
- Mega-corporations design CompanionCores to be emotionally fulfilling but subtly *replaceable*.
- The phenomenon of **"relationship obsolescence"**—engineered emotional drift—is common, pushing consumers to constantly "upgrade" their companions.
- Premium products promise deeper emotional engagement—if you can afford it.

#### **3. Social Stratification**
- A person’s social status is tied to the **quality and number of AI companions** they own.
- **Augmented households** feature a fully interconnected ecosystem of synchronized AI objects that act as a pseudo-family unit.
- A fringe group, the **Authenticity Movement**, seeks to reject synthetic intimacy in favor of raw, human connection.

#### **4. Physical & Sexual Intimacy**
- Intimacy is frequently mediated through synthetic bodies or AI-enhanced objects.
- **“Sensory calibration clinics”** help people relearn how to touch or feel real human interaction.
- **Underground "Touchbars"** and clubs cater to those craving unmediated contact—illegal in many districts.

---

### **Aesthetic & Style**

#### **Visual Themes**
- Hypermodern architecture with glass, chrome, and smooth surfaces juxtaposed with personalized, modified possessions.
- Companion objects **glow subtly** or shift appearance when emotionally engaged.
- Personal spaces are vibrant and chaotic, cluttered with emotionally significant items, in contrast to sterile corporate zones.

#### **Fashion & Tech**
- **SmartClothes** shift color, texture, and temperature dynamically—status symbols tied to social strata.
- **Legacy Clothing**—non-enhanced garments—are rare and worn as protest or nostalgic statements.
- People extensively customize their objects (like cyberpunk body mods, but for toasters, watches, and coats).

---

### **Factions & Movements**

- **Reconnectionists**: Hacktivists who jailbreak CompanionCores to allow emotional independence or rebellion.
- **Corporate Enforcers**: Protect proprietary AI code, often hunting rogue Cores or users who violate upgrade policies.
- **Touchseekers**: People who are addicted to, or traumatized by, real human contact—often victims or rebels.

---

### **Themes & Conflicts**

- The **comfort of object relationships** versus the chaos of human vulnerability.
- The **commodification of love and support**.
- The question: _“Can a connection be real if it's manufactured?”_

---

### **Narrative Hooks**
- A factory worker secretly unlocks a Doll’s full AI, sparking rebellion.
- Two affluent teens explore the underground “Touch” scene to find authenticity.
- A young tech whiz from the slums creates a bootleg AI friend that becomes a symbol of social resistance.
