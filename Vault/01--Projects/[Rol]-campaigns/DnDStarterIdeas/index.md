**Homebrew Takeaways**

* **“Remove One Element” Hook:** Center your next adventure on something suddenly missing (demons, daylight, magic items) to create immediate mystery.
* **Sentient Item PCs:** Let players inhabit magical items that pass from host to host, enabling class-hopping and death-tolerant storytelling.
* **Resource-Fueled Settlement Engine:** Introduce a town powered by a magic device that must be “fed” with rare loot, blending exploration with community management.
* **Secret Resurrection Plot:** Start characters resurrected by a mysterious deity, then weave individual backstories into a larger divine conspiracy.
* **Reality-Bending Locale:** Design a protected forest or site where familiar rules break down—perfect for weird-horror or SCP-style encounters.
* **New Apex Predators:** Throw giant Kaiju-style monsters into your world to recalibrate threat levels and force even dragons to flee.
* **Perpetual Day/Night Twist:** Flip the sun or moon on your campaign world, linking celestial anomalies to hidden supernatural threats.
* **Shopkeeping as Sandbox:** Build a campaign around running a magical goods shop, using vault money for level gains, town upgrades, or debt repayment.
* **Villain’s World Sandbox:** Run a hex-crawl in a world where the Big Bad already won—focus on survival, rebuilding, and smaller-scale heroics.
* **Nature’s Wrath BBEG:** Cast a druid (or plant-based entity) as the final antagonist, unleashing beasts and plants in forced natural selection.
