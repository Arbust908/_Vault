Here’s an outline for a **Sci-Fi Call of Cthulhu adventure** inspired by the movie *Underwater*, designed for **3 to 5 players**, with a pool of **12 pre-generated characters**. This adventure blends **claustrophobic deep-sea horror**, **Lovecraftian cosmic dread**, and **sci-fi survival** in a high-pressure underwater environment.

---

## **Title:** *“The Abyss Hungers”*

### **Tone & Themes**

* Deep-sea isolation and dread
* Corporate secrecy and scientific hubris
* Survival horror with escalating cosmic terror
* Psychological deterioration and paranoia

---

## **Setting**

**Location:** *KRONOS-47*, a massive underwater research and drilling facility located 11 km beneath the ocean’s surface in the Pacific’s hadal zone.

**Timeframe:** Late 21st century. Humanity has advanced deep-sea tech for energy extraction. Mythos entities slumber beneath the crust.

**Opening Situation:** A massive quake or unknown event has caused catastrophic damage to KRONOS-47. Power is failing, pressure integrity is compromised, and comms are down. Survivors must navigate the shattered complex to reach the escape submersible docks.

---

## **Act Structure**

### **Act I – Collapse**

* Opening explosion/quake damages the habitat.
* Surviving characters must regroup and make quick decisions.
* First deaths occur (encouraging early use of backup characters).
* Strange sounds and glimpses of alien sea-life.
* Objective: Reach the Control Center to evaluate damage and plan a route.

**Mythos Tease:** Hallucinations, flickering video logs, garbled audio with whispering in unknown languages.

### **Act II – Descent**

* Survivors must travel through partially flooded or collapsed modules: science labs, crew quarters, hydroponics bay.
* Environmental threats: crushing pressure, toxic leaks, decompression, unstable corridors.
* Hostile encounters: deformed creatures (Deep Ones, larvae, bioluminescent scavengers).
* Ethical dilemma: sacrifice a character to stabilize a bulkhead or retrieve an oxygen filter?

**Mythos Reveal:** A fragment of R’lyeh’s architecture is buried beneath the sea floor—disturbed by drilling.

### **Act III – Revelation**

* Survivors reach the drilling core and see what was unearthed: a massive structure, ancient and alive.
* The tremors were psychic shockwaves from a dreaming *Elder Thing* or *Great Old One* (e.g., *Dagon*, *Cthulhu fragment*, or a custom entity).
* Madness spreads. Sanity loss accelerates.
* Final choice: escape through a near-suicidal route or attempt to destroy the site.

**Endgame Scenarios:**

* **Escape Sub:** Dangerous but possible. Some characters might make it.
* **Self-Destruct:** Triggers destruction of the facility—possible heroic sacrifice.
* **Succumb to Madness:** Join the abyss, lured by the call of the Mythos.

---

## **NPCs & Mythos Threats**

* **Corporate Rep (NPC):** Has secret knowledge, may sabotage efforts to preserve evidence.
* **Cultist-in-Disguise (PC/NPC):** Secretly welcomes the awakening of the Old One.
* **The Deep Ones:** Begin as aquatic fauna, slowly grow humanoid and predatory.
* **The Slumbering God:** Dreams warp reality. Its awakening causes hallucinations, pressure fluctuations, and mutation.

## **Optional Mechanics**

* **Oxygen & Pressure Tracking:** Resources to manage across scenes.
* **Stress Cards:** Short-term mental effects from trauma and exposure.
* **Backup Character Drop-In Points:** Sealed rooms, survivor beacons, escape pods with one survivor inside.
