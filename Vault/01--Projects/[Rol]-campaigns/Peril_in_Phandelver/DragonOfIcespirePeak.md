## 🏘️ Starting Point: Phandalin

A frontier town serving as the central hub for adventurers.([D\&D Beyond][1])

* **Quest Board**: Located at the Townmaster's Hall, it provides missions that unlock progressively as players level up.&#x20;

* **Key NPCs**:

  * **Harbin Wester**: The nervous townmaster who posts quests.
  * **Toblen Stonehill**: Owner of the Stonehill Inn, a place for rest and information.

---

## 🧭 Quest Locations & Details

### 1. **Umbrage Hill**

* **Objective**: Convince midwife Adabra Gwynn to return to Phandalin for safety.
* **NPC**: Adabra Gwynn.
* **Encounter**: A manticore threatens the windmill.
* **Choices**: Negotiate with or combat the manticore; persuade Adabra or respect her decision to stay.

### 2. **Dwarven Excavation**

* **Objective**: Warn dwarves Dazlyn and Norbus about dragon sightings; assist in clearing the temple ruins.
* **NPCs**: Dazlyn Grayshard and Norbus Ironrune.
* **Encounters**: Ochre jellies and a trapped statue.
* **Reward**: Sending Stones. ([Cast Guidance][2])

### 3. **Gnomengarde**

* **Objective**: Investigate the disappearance of gnome inventors.
* **NPCs**: King Korboz and Queen Gnerkli.
* **Encounters**: A mimic causing chaos.
* **Choices**: Restore order diplomatically or through combat.

### 4. **Butterskull Ranch**

* **Objective**: Rescue Alfonse "Big Al" Kalazorn from orc raiders.
* **NPC**: Big Al.
* **Encounters**: Orc raiding party.
* **Choices**: Stealthy infiltration or direct assault.

### 5. **Mountain’s Toe Gold Mine**

* **Objective**: Ensure the mine's safety from threats.
* **NPC**: Don-Jon Raskin, the overseer.
* **Encounters**: Wererats and potential labor disputes.
* **Choices**: Negotiate with or confront the wererats.

### 6. **Shrine of Savras**

* **Objective**: Clear out orc occupiers from the shrine.
* **Encounters**: Orcs and ogres.
* **Choices**: Direct combat or strategic planning. ([fey ancestry][3], [Pinterest][4])

### 7. **Logger’s Camp**

* **Objective**: Investigate halted logging operations.
* **NPC**: Tibboh, a halfling overseer.
* **Encounters**: Ankheg attacks.
* **Choices**: Protect workers or eliminate threats.([D\&D Beyond][5], [D\&D Beyond][6])

### 8. **Dragon Barrow**

* **Objective**: Retrieve a magical sword from a dragon's tomb.
* **Encounters**: Undead guardians.
* **Choices**: Navigate traps and puzzles to secure the treasure.

### 9. **Woodland Manse**

* **Objective**: Disrupt the activities of evil druids.
* **Encounters**: Anchorites of Talos and a Gulthias tree.
* **Choices**: Combat or sabotage the druids' operations.([fey ancestry][3], [Scribd][7])

### 10. **Circle of Thunder**

* **Objective**: Investigate storm-related disturbances.
* **Encounters**: Anchorites conducting rituals.
* **Choices**: Interrupt rituals or gather intelligence.

### 11. **Tower of Storms**

* **Objective**: Explore the tower and its mysteries.
* **Encounters**: Sea hags and other coastal threats.
* **Choices**: Uncover the tower's secrets or cleanse it of evil.

### 12. **Falcon’s Hunting Lodge**

* **Objective**: Seek assistance or refuge.
* **NPC**: Falcon, a noble hunter.
* **Encounters**: Potential orc attacks.
* **Choices**: Defend the lodge or rally support.

### 13. **Icespire Hold**

* **Objective**: Confront the white dragon Cryovain.
* **Encounters**: Cryovain and his minions.
* **Choices**: Final battle; strategy and preparation are crucial.([Game Night Blog][8])

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Sidekicks & Companions

The adventure introduces sidekick rules, allowing for solo or small-group play. Sidekicks can assist in combat and exploration, providing support where needed.

---

## 🧭 Adventure Structure

* **Sandbox Format**: Players choose quests from the Phandalin job board, allowing for non-linear progression. ([Game Night Blog][8])

* **Level-Based Unlocks**: New quests become available as players reach certain levels, ensuring appropriate challenge scaling.

* **Culmination**: The adventure builds towards the climactic confrontation with Cryovain at Icespire Hold.([fey ancestry][3])
