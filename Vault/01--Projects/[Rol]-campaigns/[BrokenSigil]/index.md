This lets you:

# **DARK SIGILS – WORLD PRIMER (WORKING TITLE)**

*A grimdark fantasy setting of arcane dread, cosmic echoes, and unraveling memory.*

---

## **CORE PREMISE**

A dying star casts its final light over a world fraying at the edges of reality. The ancient and near-forgotten art of **Sigilcraft**—glyphic magic powered by alien truths—once held chaos at bay. Now, that knowledge is rare, feared, and tightly controlled. The **Magus Conclave**, a secretive arcane authority, rules from a **gothic floating cathedral**—**High Orvezan**—issuing glyph-laws from above.

**Magic is feared**, **mages are rare**, and most common folk understand arcane forces only through **misinterpreted leaks**—divine-seeming phenomena caused by **cosmic entities** beyond human comprehension.

---

## **COSMIC STRUCTURE**

### **Greater Entities** – Vast, unknowable beings whose essence leaks into the world, unintentionally warping reality.

* **Eteluth, the Fractal Kin** – Refraction, recursion, duplication. Causes mirrored dreams, twin births, recursive vegetation.
* **Yxir, the Unbeating Pulse** – Stillness, timelessness, suspended decay. Induces temporal stasis, soundlessness, and non-aging zones.
* **Vulm’zoth, the Indefinite Thirst** – Formlessness, ooze, identity collapse. Inspires merging cults, emotional erosion, and slime-touched fertility.
* **Nirenth-Azuhl, the Mother of Fallen Stars** – Meteoric power, psionics, ember-born mutations. Fertile rain, psychic children, and skyfall cults.
* **Ulvix, the Still Maw** – Reflection, surveillance, mimicry. Appears in water, births mirrored doubles, devours identity.

Each entity manifests in **multiple ways**, giving rise to **regional “gods”**, local cults, and contradictory myths.

---

### **Lesser Cosmic Forces** – Fragments, parasites, or leak-born beings mistaken for lesser gods.

Examples:

* **Vaeliss, the Hushed Blade** – Silence and assassination; boons tied to muting and invisibility.
* **Thrija, the Milk of Worms** – Wet rot and rebirth through decomposition.
* **Halenux, the Last Kiss** – Lost love and irrevocable moments.
* **Nen-Katha, the Flame That Stole Her Face** – Jealous mimicry through fire and false identity.
* **Kherum, the Coin-Tongue** – False promises and greed-born duplicity.

Each grants **specific boons** but exacts **curses** or identity shifts in return.

---

## **MAGIC: SIGILCRAFT**

* Glyph-based, logical, and **dangerous**.
* **Carved into specific substrates** (metal, stone, flesh—though the latter is banned).
* Only those trained and sanctioned by the **Magus Conclave** may legally craft or channel glyphs.
* Mistakes can result in **entity leakage, mutation, or madness**.
* **Witches** are outlaw glyph-users—feared, revered, and hunted.

---

## **ARCANE GOVERNANCE**

### **The Magus Conclave**

* Resides in **High Orvezan**, a floating gothic cathedral-city.
* Rules arcane law, issues **glyph decrees**, suppresses rogue magic.
* Rarely interacts with the ground except via **sigil servitors or agents**.

### **Public Perception**

* Mages are **rare and feared**.
* Most associate magic with **omens, curses, or “blessings”** from the gods.
* Witch-hunts and anti-mage riots are common in isolated regions.

## **RELIGION & CULTS**

* **"Gods"** are often misunderstood fragments of entity leaks.
* Villagers worship these gods for blessings: fertile crops, good births, safe hunts.
* Some cults are **folk traditions**, others **dangerous sects** maintaining contact with **lesser entities**.
* **Witches** often play the role of **secret clergy, midwives, or seers**, despite being outlawed.

## **THEMES & TONE**

* Grim, but **not hopeless**—quiet defiance persists in songs, rites, and old roads.
* Focus on **forbidden knowledge**, **mutation-as-miracle**, and **reality's fragility**.
* Glyphs are both **tools and truths**—to understand one is to risk breaking the other.

# **THE GLYPH WARS**
*A crumbling kingdom where forbidden magic bleeds through dying bureaucracy*

## **SANDBOX FOUNDATION**

### **REGION 1: VALDRIS CROSSING**
**Theme:** *The last "civilized" trade hub where Magus law meets frontier desperation.*

**Key Players:** 
- **Magistrate Korven** (Magus bureaucrat drowning in paperwork while real threats multiply)
- **The Ink Runners** (smugglers trading illegal glyph components)

**The Secret Threat:** The town's founding charter contains a binding glyph that slowly drains life force from residents to power the Magus Conclave's distant towers. The recent population decline isn't bandits—it's the glyph finally reaching critical mass.

---

### **REGION 2: THE THORNWOOD MARCHES**
**Theme:** *Abandoned logging camps where trees bleed ink and carve themselves.*

**Key Players:**
- **"Splinter" Maeve** (ex-logger turned Witch, hiding among the sentient trees)
- **The Bark Wardens** (forest spirits wearing human skin, poorly)

**The Secret Threat:** A crashed star-fragment 200 years ago infected the forest with cosmic awareness. The trees are slowly spelling out an eldritch ritual across miles of woodland—and they're almost finished.

---

### **REGION 3: GREYHOLD RUINS**  
**Theme:** *A former Magus stronghold where the walls still whisper institutional secrets.*

**Key Players:**
- **The Scribes' Ghost Collective** (undead bureaucrats still filing reports to no one)
- **Thane Blackquill** (treasure hunter who's been slowly going mad reading the wall-glyphs)

**The Secret Threat:** The ruins are actually a massive glyph-lock keeping something imprisoned below. Every "treasure" removed weakens the binding, and tomb robbers are unknowingly racing toward a catastrophic release.

---

## **ADVENTURE HOOKS**

• **The Magistrate's Dilemma** *(Levels 2-4, Social/Investigation)*: Korven secretly hires the party to investigate why his citizens are aging rapidly—he suspects poison, but the truth is far worse.

• **Ink Fever** *(Levels 1-3, Exploration/Horror)*: A new strain of Whisper Plague in Valdris makes victims carve glyphs into buildings. The town is becoming a massive spell array, and no one knows what it will summon.

• **The Bleeding Timber** *(Levels 3-5, Dungeon/Mystery)*: Splinter Maeve offers to trade information about the cosmic ritual for help assassinating the Bark Wardens—but she's lying about which side she's really on.

• **The Last Filing** *(Levels 4-6, Dungeon/Horror)*: The ghost scribes have one final report to complete before they can rest. It requires living witnesses to a specific historical event—witnesses who may not survive the interview process.

• **The Broken Lock** *(Levels 5-8, Apocalyptic/Boss Fight)*: Whatever Thane has been slowly releasing is almost free. The party has one chance to either reinforce the binding or face the consequences of a pre-Dimming horror unleashed.

---

## **IMPROVISATION TOOLS**

### **IF PCs BURN DOWN VALDRIS CROSSING, THEN...**
The life-drain glyph goes haywire without its population to feed on. It starts pulling energy from surrounding areas, creating a growing dead zone. Refugees flood toward the Thornwood, accelerating the forest's cosmic ritual.

### **IF THEY PARLEY WITH THE FOREST CONSCIOUSNESS, THEN...**
The trees offer a deal: help them complete their ritual to "fix" the dying star, but the process requires burning down every human settlement as fuel. They're genuinely trying to help—they just don't understand why humans object to being sacrificial components.

### **IF THEY IGNORE THE CORE CONFLICT, THEN...**
The three regions begin feeding off each other. Valdris refugees carry the Whisper Plague to Greyhold, awakening more dangerous undead. The forest's cosmic awareness starts infecting the Greyhold prisoners. Thane's treasure hunting accelerates as he tries to find weapons to fight the spreading chaos. Everything goes to hell faster.

**DM's Golden Rule:** When in doubt, have a Witch offer to solve the immediate problem in exchange for "just a small favor" that makes everything worse long-term.
