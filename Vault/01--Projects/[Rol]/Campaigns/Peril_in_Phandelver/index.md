## **1. Bundle Overview and Goals**

Your **Super Starter Bundle** is essentially a modular D\&D mini-campaign that:

* Eases new players in with a **tutorial/prelude adventure**
* Anchors them in **Phandelver and Below: The Shattered Obelisk**
* Provides multiple **story branches/hooks** into other adventures, like *Dragon of Icespire Peak* and homebrew side quests
* Builds toward an eventual larger narrative (e.g. the Obelisk/Multiverse arc)

---

## **2. Prelude/Tutorial Adventure (Level 1)**

### Purpose:

* Teach basic rules: ability checks, combat, exploration, roleplay
* Establish light character backstories and motivations
* Create a reason for traveling to Phandalin

### Suggestions:

* **Homebrew Scenario**: A small-town mystery or bandit attack in a village on the road to Phandalin.
* **One-Page Dungeons**: Use or adapt mini-dungeons like:

  * *The Delian Tomb* (popular Matthew Colville intro)
  * *The Wild Sheep Chase* (goofy but fun)
  * *The Wolves of Welton* (free beginner module)

Include NPCs or factions who later show up or are referenced in Phandalin for a tighter worldbuilding arc.

---

## **3. Phandelver and Below (Main Arc)**

Start players around level 2, then run the core *Lost Mine of Phandelver* portion. From there:

### Key beats:

* **Cragmaw Hideout**
* **Phandalin as hub** (drop side hooks here)
* **Wave Echo Cave climax**
* Transition into *Shattered Obelisk* content (levels 5+)

---

## **4. Integrating Other Stories**

### A. **Dragon of Icespire Peak (DoIP)**

* Great side quests for levels 1–6
* Use **job board in Phandalin** to seed DoIP quests organically
* Make **Gnomengarde, Butterskull Ranch, Umbrage Hill** optional warmups or detours
* Let Cryovain and orcs be rising threats concurrent with Wave Echo Cave plot

### B. **The Shattered Obelisk**

* Naturally follows Phandelver as a continuation
* Drop early **hints of weirdness/obelisks/time distortion**
* NPCs in Phandalin can speak of lost memories or strange visions
* Use **Glass Staff** or **Nezznar** as entry points to obelisk knowledge

### C. **Other Hooks**

* **Baldur’s Gate: Descent into Avernus** – refugee or cult activity in Phandalin
* **Storm King’s Thunder** – rumblings of giant activity, skyfire, or stormwrecked caravans
* **Homebrew Factions** – seed Harpers, Zhentarim, or Red Wizards as side plotlines

---

## **5. Recommended Bundle Structure**

### Sessions 1–2: Tutorial Adventure

* Teach rules, introduce setting
* End with “You must head to Phandalin…”

### Sessions 3–12: Phandelver Core

* Integrate 1–2 DoIP quests during downtime or travel
* Introduce Glass Staff, Wave Echo Cave, Black Spider

### Sessions 13–18: Post-Phandelver + DoIP Climax

* Players return to loose ends
* Fight Cryovain or Orc warbands

### Sessions 19–30: Shattered Obelisk

* Start with spooky events and lead toward the obelisk arc
* Let it feel like the world is unraveling

---

## **6. Practical Tips**

* **Use Milestone Leveling** to stay flexible
* **Recap regularly** to help new players connect events
* **Reward exploration**—show players that following side threads enriches the main arc
* **Foreshadow your endgame early**: have visions, dreams, or relics hint at the Obelisk or multiversal threats

* An injured refugee speaking of a white dragon attack (*DoIP hook*)

## **2. Phandelver Core Hub (Levels 2–5)**

### Main Content:

* Lost Mine of Phandelver

## **3. Side Quests & Hooks (Levels 4–6)**

Add side content that subtly steers the players toward higher-level arcs:

* NPCs becoming warped mentally/physically

* A white dragon attack in the distance
* Ranchers from Butterskull need aid
* Orc raiders fortified near Icespire Hold

### **Tyranny of Dragons**

* Cult of the Dragon infiltrating Phandalin
* Mysterious emissaries offering "salvation from the skies"
* A captured kobold speaking of “the Queen below the world”

### **Temple of Elemental Evil**

* Cultists seen near ancient ruins
* Elemental-themed disasters (floods, fires, tremors)
* A sealed door in the Stonehill Inn’s cellar reacting to nearby relics

## **4. Branching Paths (Level 6–8)**

When the players resolve Phandelver’s main story, the world opens up. Offer **forks** based on faction, events, or choices:

| Arc                          | Location                       | Themes                        | Key Conflict               | Suggested Start                           |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Shattered Obelisk**        | Underdark + beyond             | Time, madness, ancient relics | Reality collapse           | Phandalin itself transforms               |
| **Dragon of Icespire Peak**  | Icespire Hold, Sword Mountains | Exploration, draconic threat  | Defeat Cryovain            | Job board + NPC call for help             |

* If they investigate strange relics: obelisk madness deepens
* If they answer a local’s plea: they chase Cryovain
* If they explore quake ruins: the Temple awakens
