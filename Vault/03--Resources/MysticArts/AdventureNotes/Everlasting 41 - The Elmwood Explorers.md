## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

## The Morning After
On the morning after, the characters cross the river, make their final preparations for their expedition into the forest and then leave. Here's our opportunity to set up some plot hooks and establish Crosskeys a little further.

#### Welcome to Crosskeys
Only once east of the river do the characters get a sense of the town, that little outpost on the west side is sort of Crosskeys-light. In here, a few things become evident;

* The houses are packed to the brim, there's a desire for more housing, but it's considered too expensive. Common for a household to contain three or more generations of people.
* There's a church here, a relatively new one by the looks of things.
* There's a general store called the Import House.
* There's a logging mill owned by a Candance Eaton, she's a religious woman and a local business leader after her husband died and left her the business. She's working alongside two brothers Sampson and Angel Donnell who've been doing the majority of the logging.

#### The Import House
Run by a vulpin named Thisbaud Desmarais, he's eloquent and charming, and enjoys the relatively open community, preferring it to Sommet Rouge where he grew up in Drypond, or any more organized parts of Atroxia too. His family is here with him, around 13 more vulpin live in his house and help maintain the store.

The prices here are quite expensive, at 150% the normal market value, since they get exports rarely and only by ferry. He explains that a considerable part of his business is managing imports so, that's what he named the store.

#### Church of the Mother
There's a church here that's being built by a group of missionaries, they're struggling and it's going to be a while before they're ready. These are the [[Federici Missionaries]].

**Bishop Ennio Federici**, who lives and works here in Crosskeys and has done for a couple of months now. He's been to Roachport and intends to build a church there once this one's up and running.

**Sister Dorotea Tasanella**, Bishop Federici's right hand woman and primary charity worker, characterized by her kind heart and her instinctual nature to serve. 

**Rector Saladino Ruberti**, who had been a proselytizer in Regent's Peak until he was driven out of town by anger and hatred. Local citizens threatened him with death. Federici went to pick him up and they're hiding away from the Cult of the Cloven.

**Sister Laura Morabito**, Rector Ruberti's right hand in Regent's Peak, saw Weiss kill a person by tearing their arms off, cackling while she did it. When she reported it to the police they called her "hysterical" and she was followed by the police every day thereafter.

**Friar Paulie Angelico**, a 16 year old who intends to become a rector in Loggers County. Maybe in a town that's yet to be settled.

#### About Father Altieri
Federici is no fan of Father Alfredo Altieri, but would be saddened to learn of his passing. Ultimately, he and Alfredo are two men cut from the same cloth. Pun intended. They both  spent time in Domatris, Caelia City, and they boths know the [[Grand Nuncio]], [[Veronica Di Phaedra]], and the [[Pontifex]] [[Cassini Bertucci]].

They are part of an over-all missionary goal of the [[Church of the Mother]] to spread the religion across [[Atroxia]], but Federici sees it as his goal not to change existing communities, but to grant resolve to places where its missing, like Loggers County.

There's a bit of sinister intent there, those in need are indeed more likely to become loyalists.

#### The Pub
The Pub is owned and operated by **Frank Kirk**, a Last War veteran who fought tooth and nail to defend the town the last time the anarchists attacked, he's also been organizing a "gun day" with the locals, teaching them to aim, load, and shoot. To defend from the Anarchists.

Prices here, are at a 150% mark-up.


## Exploring Into the County
The following morning, an exploration trip into the county should come next, it shouldn't overstay it's welcome, but it is our introduction to [[Hexploration]] in some shape or form. Let's make sure it feels like a roleplay based introduction.

The camp is 5 leagues into the forest, so it would take something like two and a half days to find it.

#### Tracking
Here's when we look a bit into the hex crawl rules, let Maeve, Emily, and Barton lead the expedition into the forest. Someone's got to make survival checks to follow the tracks. There's a risk of getting lost!

#### Day One
They can talk while they're marching. But primarily I just want to impress a few things here about the flora and fauna of the region:

* The trees here are huge, and the underbrush is thick. Often requiring long routes around.
* There's squirrels, loads of birds, loads of insects. It's teeming with wildlife.
* It's a thick canopy, very difficult to see stars or the sun.
