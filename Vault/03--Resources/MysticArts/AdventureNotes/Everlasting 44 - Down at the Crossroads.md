## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

## In Crosskeys
The group's in [[Crosskeys]], and that's where they're staying until tonight when they plan to regroup at the tavern. Here's a list of stories they might have between then and now.

#### Election Announcement
Jane Tranter reiterates to Barton that Crosskeys is about retail politics. Small scale, individual connections. While it's important to tell everyone about his policies, it's far more important to understand what voters want and find a way to give it to them. He's in the service industry at the moment.

This game is won by playing to the **Power Voters**, people who shape the opinion others. She wants him to get to know five people and figure out how to make them happy, they are; Thisbaud Desmarais, Jared Ansel, Marmaduke Sweetcoke, Horatio Rowan, and the local Bishop. The bartenders are important, but they're only important to not anger, as opposed to specifically woo.

**Thisbaud Desmarais** is a local importer and shop keeper, his job is made is made unbearable by the lack of a bridge and poor fording infrastructure. With more infrastructure, he'd be able to reduce his prices, or at the very least avoid taking a loan to stay afloat.

**Jared Ansel** is a logger who's watching his community disappear, without population growth, he will eventually be forced to move to some other place, likely work for Standard Pulp, which disgusts him. Ultimately he won't be happy until Mrs. Sampson decides to stay.

**Marmaduke Sweetcoke** has always wanted to move further into Loggers County, he'd be happy to set up an enterprise there somewhere, but he's terrified of leaving the civilized side of the river. With security and roads, he'd be happy to build a business.

**Horatio Rowan** believes Loggers County will never thrive until they can produce their own food, any elected official failing to make food production a top priority is just a "big city idiot".

**Bishop Ennio Federici** thinks these people need an improved sense of community, that the church is a guiding light to bring everyone together.

#### Clean Water Provision
In the [[County Cultivation Act of 1447]], it says that clean water must be guaranteed to all municipalities in the [[Atroxian Republic]]. The Department of Essential Amenities is supposed to take care of digging wells and providing water as needed. 

Who's failing the people? Well, it's a bit of both. The Department should've handled it. Department Director, [[Proletarian]] Phil Johnson, should've mandated a team be sent out there by now. It's likely an oversight on the [[Civic Commission]]'s part. But the Sheriff could've dug a well and demanded reimbursement. It's well within his rights.

A well would take 1d4 weeks to build and cost -n5 p/cap, which equals about N18.000 a week. And N72.000 would be nearly be equivalent to the county's weekly expenditure, forcing its bankruptcy up one week. The Sheriff needs the federal government to pay for it.

#### The Church of the Mother
There's a church here that's being built by a group of missionaries, they're struggling and it's going to be a while before they're ready. These are the [[Federici Missionaries]].

**Bishop Ennio Federici** (italian high pitch), who lives and works here in Crosskeys and has done for a couple of months now. He's been to Roachport and intends to build a church there once this one's up and running.

Federici is no fan of **Father Alfredo Altieri**, but would be saddened to learn of his passing. Ultimately, he and Alfredo are two men cut from the same cloth. Pun intended. They both  spent time in Domatris, Caelia City, and they both personally know the [[Grand Nuncio]], [[Veronica Di Phaedra]], and the [[Pontifex]] [[Cassini Bertucci]].

They are part of an over-all missionary goal of the [[Church of the Mother]] to spread the religion across [[Atroxia]], but Federici sees it as his goal not to change existing communities, but to grant resolve to places where its missing, like Loggers County. There's a bit of sinister intent there, those in need are more likely to become loyal followers.

**Sister Dorotea Tasanella**, Bishop Federici's right hand woman and primary charity worker, characterized by her kind heart and her instinctual nature to serve. 

**Father Saladino Ruberti** (gruffer voice), who had been a proselytizer in Regent's Peak until he was driven out of town by anger and hatred. Local citizens threatened him with death. Federici went to pick him up and they're hiding away from the Cult of the Cloven.

**Sister Laura Morabito**, Rector Ruberti's right hand in Regent's Peak, saw Weiss kill a person by tearing their arms off, cackling while she did it. When she reported it to the police they called her "hysterical" and she was followed by the police every day thereafter.

**Friar Paulie Angelico**, a 16 year old who intends to become a rector in Loggers County. Maybe in a town that's yet to be settled.
