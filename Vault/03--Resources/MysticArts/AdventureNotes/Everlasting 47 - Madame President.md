## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

#### Mr. Horatio Rowan
Horatio Rowan is the beating heart of this town, and he doesn't like Unionists. He believes Loggers County will never thrive until they can produce their own food, any elected official failing to make **food production** a top priority is just a "big city idiot".

But at the moment, he's more concerned with the loss of his godson, he hates Church and the Bridgemen, and is conflicted about a warforged avenging his son. Reckons maybe that's all they're good for.

He took on Aaron Braunstone after his mother, Fusilier Skylar Braunstone died in Gracklstugh. He fondly remembers Aaron's wedding with his love Alfie.

#### Jenny Clark, Whitesmith
Going to talk to Jenny, Barton would meet Church and Seraph who are using her forge. She's mostly experimenting with tin cans that she believes can be used to store food much more cheaply than glass jars. She's happy to lend anyone use of the forge, and is frankly considering selling it and pursuing this tin can idea if she can get funding.

She estimates funding would be around N30k for a few months to build up a the capability to make a small factory that makes these cans worthwhile. That's not what it'll cost to make it into a large business, it's basically R&D money.

#### The Lumber Mill
Greta Eaton is the proprietor of a large carpentry store, but there's no money to run it anymore. She's been trying to get the town to buy it and run it instead.

In the town's current state, getting this thing operation is going to cost around N30.000 a week. She would be happy with selling it for N30.000. Or maybe even less, since at the moment it's just slowly gathering dust.

Basically, this is an inactive improvement, and it can't be active without money spent on it. And Greta's broke.

#### Mrs. Sampson
Mahala Sampson isn't much interested in local politics since she assumes she won't be here come election day. Unionist seems better than the other two candidates, Mahala's pessimistic about his chances though. Saying she assumes he won't win against the special interest people.

The Sampson family is responsible for about half the logging in the town, it's matriarch is Mahala Sampson, who inherited the business from her husband after he was killed in the last attack. She's considering selling and moving her family to Regent's Peak.
