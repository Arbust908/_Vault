## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

## Roachport Rumblings
The campaigners arrive in [[Roachport]], and I cannot stress this enough: this is where the adventure begins! So far, we've done the prologue, and we've done the tutorial town with the tutorial expedition and the tutorial downtime. Now, we start the massively open sandbox adventure by laying on a ton of options, plot hooks, problems, and adventure opportunities!

Really, there's nothing subtle about this, just start laying out quests like its your job!

Let's start by getting everyone to the **tavern**, where the whole world is located at this moment. Because the town is dead quiet.

#### The Wake at The Little Ferryman
When they arrive at the Little Ferryman, they see a somber mood and a dour atmosphere. It's a wake, and at the small stage in one corner of the room is the host of the wake, and the local bard, a smallfolk woman named **Abitha Loafwood**.

She plays a somber melody, singing beautifully, and when she and the applause stops, she says.

> "Thanks everybody. Let's give a warm welcome to Gideon Barnet to say a few words."

**Gideon Barnet** is a strange old cook who wants to make a new port here, he believes that there's a possibility to clear the sediment at the river mouth and the crags at the bay, and once that's done they can have a huge port here.

> "Never once did Tom dismiss or dismay. He listened to all of us. Not just the folks in Lone Cabbage, not just worrying about the money or telling us he was busy. Oh yeah he made his trips down south to plead with the President, but most of all, he told us everything about those conversations. He was here for us. Right until the very end."

Then we give way for Abner.

**Abner Willis** is a young bespectacled dreamer who says that Roachport has the potential to be a major hub, he believes the port, the river, the mountain is all signs of pure luck. He wants to rename the town **Serendipity**.

> "Tom knew, like we all do, that Roachport is the edge of civilization. But not to mean the border of civilization, or the boundary of it. But the bleeding edge; that possibilities for magnificent greatness is all around us."

After that, Constance.

**Constance Hawkins** is a woman in her sixties with a strong southerner's accent. She moved here from Flintwood during the Secession and hasn't looked back since. She was a wildly successful flint merchant, turned stock broker in the post-war booms, and she sold out before the recession. So she's loaded.

She's only out here for the separation from civilization. She found relative peace in this town and has preferred it to noisy cities. She has little interest in seeing this place explode in population, and no interest in investing in a failing economy regardless, but isn't averse to making good bets when they come along.

> "Mr. Peterson always had a way with words, he was a charming man with a brilliant sense of humor and an enviable work ethic. Which I always liked him for, because he worked hard so the rest of us wouldn't have to."

After that, Callum.

**Callum Rose**, a nicely dressed stick-insect looking man steps up, he's actually an employee of [[Fulburne Consulting]] and is here in his capacity as CAS' man in Loggers County, intending to investigate the [[Quexil Caste]]. He's personally investigated Gaxek Point and made contact with the violent members of the tribe's inner circle. He intends to parlay with the [[Nightfont Nomads]] to find out more.

> "Tom was always welcoming, even to the strangest requests. It's what you wanted in a leader, reliable, stern, but understanding."


#### The Roachport Bugle
The author, Giles Nichol is happily scribbling away in a corner of the pub, taking notes. He types these up on a typewriter.

**THE DEATH OF A TITAN** - County Commissioner Tom Peterson was found dead by Deputy Degory Whitelock in his office home at 41 years old. Sheriff Love Gillcrest says an investigation has been launched to determine the cause of death. A wake will be held at the Little Ferryman tomorrow morning. Much is spectulated about how he died.

**BANKRUPTCY LOOMS** - There's a looming threat of bankruptcy, with hope of a balanced budget gone. The county looks into alternatives to fund the state. On the less palletable side, there's considerations of selling more land to Standard Pulp, taking a loan from the Bank of Blackguard. On the more palletable ones, though unlikely to happen, there's a possibility of a loan from Ms. Hawkins, or applying for a Presidential bail out. Neither of which seem likely to happen.

**MONSTER SIGHTINGS ON THE RISE** - Security becomes a primary concern among residence as sightings of "monsters" continues to go up. Sheriff Gillcrest reassures citizens that they are safe, despite the recent death of the County Commissioner. Gideon Barnet swears he knows Watermen's tactics, saying this is Waterfolk behavior.

#### Who's In Charge Now?
When Tom died, law recognizes his deputy, **Degory Whitelock** as the sole executive of the county. Degory is an 80 year old man who's been running this county since he was mentored by Angus Roach.

Degory has no intention of staying in this position, no ambition to run again, and is concerned about Pierce and Nevin making a deal, allowing one to run for Commissioner and the other for Parliament unopposed. So he wants to appoint a deputy and then resign. Effectively handing control to a capable individual, but he doesn't know who.

If Claire were here, he'd appoint her on name recognition alone. But without her he's skeptical of newcomers.

#### What Happened to Tom?
He was killed! Found dead in the night! The Sheriff, **Love Gillcrest**, a non-binary elf, is mounting a full thorough investigation. Every citizen in town will be interrogated, and they're halfway through already.

Gillcrest is convinced the killer was nobody in town, but wants to make certain of it. If Altan makes his presence and job known to Gillcrest, he'll reveal that he was stabbed in the torso, a non-fatal wound... but the blade was poisoned.

There's no toxicology report or anything like that, they don't know what poison it was. But it was Purple Worm Poison. The killer was the Whisper. Galeath Nathaniel. But nobody knows that yet.

Love has some reports he doesn't know what to do with:

* Some water-men coming up river, covered in corals.
* Siege tunnel up north, people seen up there.
* Ghost stories about a mill three leagues south.
* Lights at the Old Docks. Maybe there's folks there.
