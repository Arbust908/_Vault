## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

### Session Description
The expeditioners have taken a Koalinth prisoner with the intent of interrogating them, and now gray morality must meet tactical acumen as the decision for what happens next rests in the hands of the group.

## Interrogating Koalinth
Maxley looks at Church with a raised eyebrow and a bit curious.

> Sir Rotheric Maxley leans in towards Church, who's holding the Sergeant, and says:
> 
> "There's something to be said about *interrogating Koalinth*, like *herding cats*, or *rolling boulders up-hill*. Are we in the business of inventing idioms?"

Emily and Altan are pursuing a line a questioning against a Koalinth named Porret, a spotter that worked under Sergeant Holg. Porret had agreed to talk until sundown (2 hours), in return he could keep the Bloodstone.

He's at full hit points, and can swim 30 ft, so his plan is to grab the stone and dash, which will bring him underwater in one turn. Readied actions might kill him before he's underwater, but he's willing to take that risk. Because once he's underwater the bullets will be ineffective.

There's two schools of thoughts on the bullets impacting water, and I'm inclined to use the latter interpretation.

* RAW: "A ranged attack roll with a weapon underwater automatically misses a target beyond the weapon’s normal range, and the attack roll has Disadvantage against a target within normal range."
* IRL: "Bullets slow to non-lethal speeds after travelling underwater for 3-8 feet."

#### What Porret Believes
Porret is in a pinch: he won't be allowed to live and leave unless he talks. And the Warchief Mulk isn't fond of yappers who give away military secrets. Porret would get more respect if he died today. That's what the Sergeant is going to do. Kill himself on the spot.

Porret thinks he's only got to find a window of opportunity and then bolt. Until then, he'll answer questions. But he's still trying to shake up this group of adventurers.

He knows that if he gives away information about the Koalinth, he'll never be forgiven, and he'd rather die than give enemies strategic information.

> "Tales about common enemies is one thing, puppet. Koalinth security, however, is a whole 'nuther beast."

#### What Porret Knows
Porret, and the Koalinth in general, are territorial to the bay. They consider its waters and coasts their own domain. They do not recognize the rights of Atroxian settler scum. The Koalinth relocated here four years ago from the Clover Delta, and have been here shorter than the settlers, but that's beyond the scope of Porret's point.

Porret knows that the entire forest is carved up into domains, each claimed by a warlord of some kind. Koalinth don't venture far into the land simply because they don't dare. The rivers are tough to wade through and the land isn't friendly to them.

> "Further in ya go, the worse the land gets, like it hates ya. It tries to throw you off of its back." he smiles, adding: "...there are warlords in there. They've acclimatized to the deadly land by being tougher than it."

He knows that there's Anarchists, Nightfont Nomad elves, Atroxians, and the "Dog Men" he's heard rumors of from elves that they retreated from. Koalinth see nothing wrong with retreat if it's tactical. They're zealous, but not dumb.

#### Raising the Stakes
After outlining those things, he wants to raise the stakes.

> "You want to know about the Bloodstone, or the Lich?"

He's willing to talk about those topics, in return for another two bloodstones, or the murder of the Sergeant. It's up to the players. If they start arguing, he uses the time to get the hell outta dodge.

#### The Lich
> "I don't know much, but there is a lich out there. Stories say it lies beyond the Bleeding Tree, across the Boneyard of Fossils. There, at the treeline, there is a colossus, inside; the Dead One resides... or so the stories go."

He doesn't know much about this lich, but stories say he's been there longer than anyone. Porret doesn't know if that's true. But he knows that Warchief Mulk reckons he's the only one who knows *what* Bloodstone is, really.

#### About Bloodstone
For Porret, "Bloodstone" is as much about the [[Bloodstone Tribe]] as it is about the [[Bloodstone]] itself. He and Holg's squadron faced off against a couple of Bloodstone tribesmen on a march across the coastline for some reason. They killed them.

He knows the following things about Bloodstone:

* The Bloodstone Tribe is controlled by **The Immortal Thane**. 
* It's a gang of thugs that control Bloodstone, the Bloodstone Pass, and more.
* They have Bloodstone weapons, like technology powered by dark magic.
	* Bloodstone Injectors crush the gems and squirt them into mad spellcasters.
	* Bloodstone Launchers crush the gems and use it as magic ammunition.
* It's some kind of fuel. The Koalinth Warchief wants it.
* Porret says it comes from a "dead god."

> "Everything disgusting about Atroxians and everything disgusting about spellcasters, all rolled into one disgusting enemy. There is no greater foe in the Elmwood."

"What is Castle Bloodstone?"

> "It's a fairy tale, the kind ya tell to scare the lil' ones. Can't be real. Nothing that stupid sounding is. A castle that's also a mine, built on a vein of a dead god, ruled over by an immortal thane. Yeah, right."

#### Other Things
In general, Porret might've heard about other things the PCs could ask about, but in general the Koalinth just don't know that much about the land.


## Hexploration!
Now, it's an expedition session, let's lean into it. This time roll for random encounters, like every other night or something! We'll do survival, weather, the works!

**Random Encounter**, don't be afraid to let random encounters happen, roll and see what happens! They can be roleplay or action, it's all good by me.

**Ankheg Attack!**, with all the set-up, you can't go wrong with an Ankheg!

**Roach's Tombstone**, A forgotten gravestone in the forest, marking the resting place of Angus Roach. His epitaph reads: "Brave new horizons can be found at home." Locals say the wind always picks up near the stone. There's a bed of wildflowers at the base of it.

**Old Docks**, the remains of a wooden dock, once used by fishermen during the early days of the county. The wood is rotting, and most of the structure has collapsed into the water, but the posts still rise out of the lake like skeletal fingers.


#### Soda Springs
Then if they find [[Soda Springs]], it's a question of what happens with the [[Hungry Mutts]] and the others in town. It's a fairly open ended question for the PCs to tackle.
