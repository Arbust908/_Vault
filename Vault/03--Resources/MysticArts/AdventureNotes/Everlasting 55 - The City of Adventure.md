## Introduction
> "No joking around, this is Kingmaker!"

**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

### Session Background
In 1444, [[Lanis Delaney]] murdered her father, Timothy Delaney. Emily found out and got herself a job at a bar in [[Freeport]]. Trying to track down her mother and her father, Emily moved into Freeport, but has since hit a snag and unable to make progress for a few years.

### Session Description
When rumors of the Jolly Roger start swirling around Freeport's seedy underbelly, Emily begins a journey that will eventually take her to her fated encounter with her mother on-board the Skull & Crossbones.

#### Atroxian Wrought Destruction
Going through the Old City, she'll see battle scars mark sections of the city here, bullet holes that were never fixed. Shelled houses that were never repaired. It's a mess. To upend the Navy Marines from the docks took Freeport weeks.

In the end, [[Sea Lord of Freeport|Sea Lord]] [[Keighan Rumspire|Rumspire]] captured a great many of them as hostages and it began the [[Second War on Piracy]], which is coming to an end now thanks to returned hostages and an agreement with [[President of Atroxia]], [[Robert Domilius]].

#### Marquis Moon
She can chose to buy [[Etherflux]] off of Ficca, the muttering bartender. He'll warn her not to stack her lethargies or it might be an overdose. She could also buy a beer here at the Marquis Moon.

The Marquis Moon is a plain door behind a shop that sells Estarrian Tea, but it's got a carving of a Goliath Marquis and a crescent moon.

Ficca suggests that if she wins the fight in the Social Club later, he could take half her earnings instead.

#### Meet the Smallfather
Smallfather Falgin Capstone, the Chairman of the [[Smallfolks Benefits Association]] is looking to back someone in the upcoming **[[Bountyfair]] Games**. He's thinking that Emily could be a good target for a stolen win. He could even supply her with Etherflux for her fight.

He's also going to ask if Emily is related to Lanis Delaney. Smallfather Falgin remembers doing business with a woman that looks a lot like Emily. Lanis was here searching for information a while back.

> "Like she had a bone to pick with Lady Jade."

* The Brethren Court violently collapsed in Freeport back in 1433, largely as a knock-on effect of the [[1432 Recession]].
* Lady Jade tried to reassemble the court with her as Pirate Queen back in 1443 to take the fight to the Atroxians during the [[Second War on Piracy]].
* Then Lanis came along looking for her in Freeport and then the whole Brethren Court never really assembled. Odd that.
