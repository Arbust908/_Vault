## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

## What's Next for Crossroads
After Rapid and Emily talking, we move onto tonight's main course: planning.

Sir Rotheric is keen to point out that the company stands at a crossroads, no pun intended. There's a countless options ahead. At some point along the way they'd do well to have...

> "...a bit of a strategic overview." he puffs. "I'm sure you all know the difference between strategy and tactics?" he pauses. "Church?"

Sir Rotheric's point is that while they have their immediate next plans ahead, there's a great deal undiscussed. He wants them to start drafting plans for what the goals of the company are, in what order, and how best to achieve them.

Basically, he thinks they have to talk about goals in order to guide their decisions.

> "A great deal of energy is spent on what went wrong. My humble suggestion is that you're still deciding your goals. Still deciding your values." He says, then adds: "Only once your goals are set in stone can you use them to guide all future decisions."

Rotheric wants the party to take this time to outline their goals and ambitions, to really discuss what's important to them, how to get that done, and **PRIORITIZE.**

## Long Rest Scenes
Things that could be attempted. Or stuff that could happen. Also, downtime.

#### Training a Mastiff
It won't be possible to train a mastiff in three days, but a few lucky rolls of Animal Handling could *calm* a mastiff enough so that they could continue training it later. Or leaving it with Rotheric who can train them further.

#### Remember the Tiefling
There's a Tiefling in town named Remember, her life was saved in the forest and she's discovered some magic that lies within her. She receives visions of a city burning, or covered in smoke. Of Atroxian soldiers in masks. Doesn't know what to make of them.

She's a warlock (like all my tieflings) and has a connection to a patron she doesn't know, likely Dispater.

- **SS 07. Pickart Loggery.** There's a carpentry business built here, inherited by a tiefling named Remember Leeper, an expert carpenter, she's a tiefling who came here from Bugtussle with her family. They died on the way here. She nearly died herself but was reincarnated in the forest as a tiefling.

#### Cyrus' Fizzy Project
Cyrus wants to make Fizzies and hopes to sell them one day if they ever get into contact with the rest of the world. He'll need some thousands of notes a week for some time to figure out how to do that. But this could be a **corporation** one day!

#### Who's In Charge?
Isiah, the tavern keeper, and Job the mason are sort of the "town elders" in that they're important and basically run the town already. But there isn't much to run, people get along okay. 

They wonder if Seraph isn't technically their boss and Atroxian Law does technically dictate that someone has to be made Sheriff, and the town has to spend loads of money on unemployment benefits and more. Right now, it's best to leave all this unofficial, but one day this will matter.

There's also the question of what this town should focus on whenever they legally register the settlement. All of this should be a roll for Maeve probably.

#### The Tower and House
There's nobody in town who wants to claim ownership of the tower and house, it was built by the town for defense, and the consensus is that it should be used for defense by "town officials" what ever those are.
