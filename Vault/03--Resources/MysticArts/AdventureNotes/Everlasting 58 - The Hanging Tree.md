## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

## Hanging Tree
This occupied Hall serves ale to travelers, and the bar is tended to by a huge burly orc named Roscoe. He's happy to talk to these "Settlers" and happy to serve them, though it's all for a price. He's just looking to make as much money as possible. At the moment, that means working for the factions, because working for the settlers will likely rob him of all business.

The patrons here are by an large monstrous races; orcs, goblinoids, and others. None are natives to other factions.

**Khut Orcslayer** is a former Starkhelm dwarf, he abandoned them after one of many skirmishes with the Nightfont brutally killed his crew. He and his crew were sent to the Fallen Monastery to take it, despite the dwarves having no claim to it. He now resents King Garrig Starkhelm and all his politics. - all he wanted to do was research the Stone Maw.

**Shortpaw** is Quickpaw's more courageous brother, a couple of scars, and a chipped scimitar. He's got a Diplomat's Pouch he uses to communicate with Quickpaw. This is how they know so much. Shortpaw could shed insight on the following bits of info for N50,000:

* The location of Utopia
* A bit of a faction map layout, at least for the north of Starkcrags
* A rough location of Starkheim and Bastion of Nightfont.
