## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

## Litlahofn
We begin at a serene little town a little less than a month after CC left Blackguard, all the way up in Dunnecaster. 

> It was on the 28th of Evernight, 1448, 27 days after CC left Blackguard and ran up north. Where you find yourself waking up for the fifth consecutive night at the same tavern. In the same bed. Something you haven't done since your apartment in Blackguard.
> 
> The rays of the sunlight crept through the window, indicating you had slept in after your late bedtime the night before.
> 
> You wake up, as you've done a few times now, with the noises of a handful of fisherman debating how best to unload their morning's catch.
> 
> "Oh curse all the Forgotten Gods!" shouts a dwarf from outside as you finally snap awake properly!

#### Serene Little Town
There's a light hustle and bustle of a town at work in the morning, it's almost serene however, instead of a clamor of loads of people, she's begun to recognize the people's individual voices from the waves.

In this village live around 3.000 people, spread out across the hills and the bay. They work in fishery and forestry.

The tavern is owned by a Mr. Henrik, it's called the Giant's Rest.

* Arnault, the former Tradian fisherman.
* Vorsod, a former Blackmoorish fisherman.
* The trio of Caterian brothers: Finn, Erik, and Oddur.

They can say hi to her.

Vorsod asks if she's okay after last night. Asks if she wants to drink the next night. Everyone is super welcoming and there's no rude questions being asked.

#### The Blackguardian
She'll see a man in a suit! Suspicious! But it turns out to be a representative from the [[Civic Commission]]'s Poverty Affairs named Arthur Clarke (a rude coincidence to her family names) asks CC how much she's making and tells her she should meet with the Baliff's office to claim her N100 a week stipend in accordance with the [[County Cultivation Act of 1447]].

#### House for Sale
There's a house for sale for N150.000, she could easily buy it if she wanted to. It's nice too. The owner, Stefan Norland, is happy to show her around it. It's going to take a day to finalize paperwork before money can legally exchange hands.

The house has:

- A large kitchen with a sturdy table perfect for gatherings.
	- Haraldur from Pinebrook carved the wooden table.
- A second-floor balcony with a breathtaking view of the water.
- A garden that could use some love but has potential.
- Back entrance that leads into another residential street.

Stefan (high pitch American) offered to lease the house for 4k a month if he could show it to people while she lived there.

He could recommend she finds her way to the **Bank of Liltahofn**, a bank operating and a part of the [[Fellhorn Estate]]. They could easily lend her the rest of the money for 5% annual interest if she pays upfront 50k.

#### Dating the Haworth Girl
While making her way around town, CC spots a cute girl. Her name is Andie de Grieu and she's a fire genasi. She smiles at CC as she's walking around town.

CC wouldn't have seen Andie before, because Andie's a traveler, she's the travelling secretary of Arthur Clark. She's a lot like CC, posh - of the Caelaventis de Grieus, her legacy goes to the town's founding. She was raised on an estate, rode horses, trained dogs, and got bored of the whole thing.

Her education afforded her a job that travels so she took it. But dreams of being able to quit it and just travel untethered. "See the world."

She's a genasi! But not from an outer plane. "I was just born like this." and "We never got an explanation." She's curious by nature, and wants to go digging. Wonders if her mother cheated on her father, wonders if that's something her mother did that she never knew about. Or her dad. Or maybe she's made some bargain she didn't know about.

#### Date Goes Wrong
In the middle of a date at a nice fish restaurant called the **Fjord & Feast**, where they serve expertly prepared cod and imported white wine. It's a stunning place. And things are going well with Andie. She likes CC, but then the doors open and Raoul Vogel enters the restaurant.

**Raoul Vogel** was one of Benedict Boyle's capos, always by his side in the VIP part of the room. He seems to make eye-contact with CC, but doesn't recognize her at first.

Andie asks what's up, but eventually things go awry as Raoul will recognize CC eventually, and Andie will likely want to grab her coat and purse and more. Raoul sits by the front door, so CC might need to sneak out the bathroom window.


## Haworth
Haworth is a town with a population of 6.000, and while its uncomfortably close to Blackguard, there's also no signs of Boyle or his men. Maybe Haworth is just unimportant enough? Maybe this route north is just forgotten land. It's good to be here.

> It wasn't until much later, on the 17th of Coldwinds, in 1449, when CC saw that girl again, it was just moments after she discovered a perfect target for burglary.

#### Perfect Score
The mansion stands out in Haworth, a symbol of the quiet wealth that permeates the town. It’s a stately two-story building with whitewashed stone walls, tall, arched windows, and a red-tiled roof. 

The front yard is neatly maintained, bordered by wrought iron fencing, with a small garden featuring purple flowers and neatly trimmed hedges. A brass plaque by the gate reads **The De Grieus Residence.**

**The Client: Quentin de Grieus** is a nice soft-spoken man in his early 50s. He has silver-streaked hair, kind blue eyes, and a habit of fidgeting with his pocket watch when nervous.

Quentin owns a hunting rifle he needs fixed up before he goes on a long carriage ride to Regent's Peak. He doesn't want to be caught unawares if they go for a mountain hike and they're attacked by bears.

**His wife** Lydia de Grieus (née Voss) is a striking woman in her late 40s with sharp features and raven black hair. She's obviously noble, haughty and critical. Will judge CC's attire and attitude. Catch her abbreviations.

> "Aren't you an observant little girl."

They're a lot like her parents.

#### Andie's Back
While at the Manor, while CC is working on the gun, Andie is investigating her mother's occultism.

#### The Fling
CC and Andie could finally have a fun date. One that "ends well." They can talk about what happened in the interm. Andie wants to get to know CC why things ended badly before.

Andie meanwhile could tell CC about how she ratted out Arthur Clarke for his deliberately misfiled tax reports, and he had her fired. So, now she's trying her dream of living on the road. Only its expensive and she figures she's always being mistreated, might as well try a bit of light burglarly... especially from a woman who can afford to be robbed a bit.

#### Ground Floor
The front door opens into a grand entryway with polished wooden floors and a chandelier. The main hall leads to:

* A cozy **drawing room** filled with books, a fireplace, and a bar cart where Quentin spends his evenings.
* A formal **dining room** with an expensive oak table and an assortment of imported silverware and china.
* A **kitchen** where the cook and maid are usually found, gossiping about Lydia’s latest outburst.

#### Second Story
Getting the gold.

The private quarters of the Allendales:

* **Master Bedroom**: Lavish, with a massive four-poster bed and an ornate vanity. Lydia’s jewelry box, containing her collection of gemstones and pearls, is prominently displayed.
* **Quentin’s Study**: Tidy and utilitarian, with rows of schematics, diagrams of firearms, and a locked display case containing rare pistols. A safe is hidden behind a painting of a hunting scene.
* **Guest Room**: Rarely used, but always impeccably prepared—Lydia insists on appearances.

#### Attic
The attic is mostly used for storage but holds a darker secret. Lydia, it seems, has ties to a **Forgotten God** cult. Among the dusty trunks and forgotten furniture are:

* Ritual objects wrapped in cloth.
* An altar carved from obsidian, with faint glowing runes.
* A strange, faint hum that seems to grow louder the closer one gets to the altar.

Lydia is here and she accidentally summons a huge smoke demon dog monster. It breathes staggered breaths as it materializes, kills Andie, and CC must escape or be arrested!

(That's the threat! Does CC end up with a criminal record?)

#### The Inevitable Escape
CC makes her escape, and sets out onto the road. And that'll be that.
