## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

## Santa Clara
At the heart of [[Santa Clara]]'s old city is the **Palacio Dorada**. Which has been destroyed and rebuilt in a countless ways until it was constructed in its current image.

Serpah's staring at the word Tucheth on a piece of paper in his father's study. Right on his desk between his business papers and his government papers.

What Seraph doesn't know is that Tucheth is the name of the Cloaker Squadron controlled by House Kenafi, one of the [[Eight Houses of Menzoberranzan]]. And his dad isn't going to address it either.

#### Faith Clay
Faith is concerned that Seraph doesn't have a plan for the future. She thinks their dad is keeping them in the dark about something and wonders what's going on. She has been working on electricity and wonders if Seraph needs to do something equally revolutionary.

#### (Rylros) What's Next?
> As you recount this story, you see Rylros. "Tell me, Mayfly. What is this Faith woman working on?"

Seraph is in his prison cell in the Underdark, telling Rylros the story of what happened. Rylros can in return talk about his value is also tied to his input. If he stops being useful, he'll be dismissed by his house.

## Stormgold Clay
> "Let's focus on the task at hand, kiddo."

What's happening? Well, [[Underrail Transport & Cargo]] is in a bind. Their first and only route, through the Underdark, connects Crow's Nest and Santa Clara is working fine but passengers are few and far between.

Advertisement is an issue, and passengers even more of an issue. Stormgold is considering investment from a private equity company if this continues. He's been in contact with Sven Clasuen, the CEO of a company named Walker and Wright. He's however got another trick up his sleeve before he falls into that trap.

Stormgold is hatching a scheme: he wants to campaign for a railroad that runs from Westhelm to Redford. It'll require an investment of around one point three billion notes.

He thinks he can convince Ty Kamren if he can first guarantee Georgie Benson, the CEO of the [[Mallory Corporation]]. Once those two are on-board, they'll need approval from the [[President of Atroxia]], but that can be achieved at that rate.

Why is it so complicated? It all comes down to how the [[Corporation/Areston Rail Company|Areston Rail Company]] functions, they need a majority of three votes from the Board of Directors to either pressure, or more likely replace CEO **Cooper Orville**.

He wants Seraph to do a bit of dirty work. When Georgie and the others are here, Seraph has to find out if all the [[Mallory Family]] really are Yuan-Ti. Because if negotiations go south, he'd like to have a back-up plan. 

He's supposed to go interrogate Thaddeus, since he's the dumbest, and if everything goes south, he has to run.

#### Dusty
Dusty feels excluded about this whole thing, how come Seraph gets to be on the inside whenever they're dealing with the Mallory's? What does Seraph know that Dusty doesn't? Dusty feels dejected his father seems to favor Seraph. Wonders what he's got to do to stick out, worried about being left behind.

#### (Rylros) Family Competition
Rylros understands what it's like to compete so aggressively with family, it's always a constant struggle in Drow society. He wonders if their competition hurts them or hurts them. Maybe if the Drow were aligned, they could conquer the world. It's never been tested.

## The Dinner
Georgie Benson comes over along with Thaddeus, his kid from a previous marriage. Georgie's a bit thick, but not as thick as Thaddeus. Magnolia is the one Seraph saw die.

#### Thaddeus
Thaddeus can easily be interrogated if caught out, he will go so far as to say: "I'm not supposed to talk about it." And other equally confirming non-confirmations.

#### (Rylros) Spy Work?
Rylros asks is Seraph is used to doing spy work, he's relating to everything about secrets. It's always all about secrets, what's unnerving he finds, is that secrets are a sign of war. All warfare is based on deception, so can't we expect deception to be the first sign of warfare?
