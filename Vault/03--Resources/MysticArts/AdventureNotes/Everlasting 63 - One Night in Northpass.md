## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

## A Drunken Mistake
Maeve wakes up in Cedric's tight little student apartment, there's dirty clothes everywhere and stacks of half-read legal journals across the room. He's snoring, still fast asleep. She finds in her pocket a large silver coin, that says "Tyrfang".

It's a Casterian Crown, essentially serving the same purpose as a gold piece, it's worth 10 silver coins, but made entirely of silver instead. She cannot remember why has it.

#### Cedric Thorne's Bedroom
This is an awkward college experience, let's lean into the logistics of it. There's a ton of questions to be answered:

* Does she wake up Cedric?
* How does she feel? 
* What did she wear to the mixer?
* Will she go home to change?
* What's that walk like?

If awoken, Cedric will blush and not know where to look. He's all flustered. I don't know if Maeve will immediately make it clear that this was a one-time thing, or if that's my job later. We'll see.

#### The University of Northpass
Or as it's sometimes called, Northpass University! It's a huge building that used to be the King's Palace before the Interregnum. During the Interregnum, large chunks of it were bombared by Litholf Domilius. In the aftermath, Litholf founded the university and moved the noble's residence to the Royal Palace.

The building is massive, and there's a huge campus. You can study all kinds of stuff here, law, statecraft, history, you name it! There's even a medical wing run by Panacea.

#### Isabella Medina
Isabella is studying biology and failing, she wanted to work with animals, but is beginning to think she's not well suited for scholarly stuff. She's dyslexic and easily distracted. But she wants to do something with her life.

Isabella will inquire about Maeve, and try to reconstruct last night together with her. Isabella remembers her and Cedric arguing non-stop. Wonders what happened there.

Isabella thinks she's going to drop out soon, her grades are abysmal and she's getting broke. There's a job in a nearby [[Wimbleton Shipping]] outpost that she's thinking of taking up to get some real world experience. Maybe she can be useful to Maeve when she graduates.

> "I promise I'll come here and have lunch until you graduate!"


## Julian Blackwell
At this point, let's introduce the hottie. Julian Blackwell is a new student in school, showing up a couple of months late in the semester. And he's got it all. He's 6' 2", barely 30, lean but athletic, angular face, a bit of an unkept stubble with deep hazel eyes and a smooth baritone voice.

He's also tired looking, casually wealthy (Wright Time watch), and a little rugged.

#### In Class!
Professor Elvor Duadottir is teaching a class, she asks: "The Charter of Justice, signed in..." and under his breath Blackwell says "1341" audible only to Maeve, until the Professor dissapointingly says: "1341. Pay attention, folks!"

She asks: "Here's an easy one, the Committee of WHAT decides the Bar Exam?" he just smiles and looks around like he's wondering if no one's getting it. There's this dejection in his eyes.

She asks: "Three Charters of Trade define the existence of corporate entities in the Republic. But only one of them is enshrined in the constitution, can anyone tell me which one." He makes a little "2" symbol with his hand under the table, but doesn't say anything out loud.

It's depressing to him how far he's fallen.

#### The Cafeteria
After class, it's lunch time and Maeve and Isabella can talk again. Isabella is a little teary eyed, because the headmaster (Prof. Gulsvig) told her off for slacking. She's better, but worries her life is being thrown away.

Isabella might spot Blackwell come in and ask Maeve about him. They could do some girly gossip. But then Thorne shows up. That should all be awkward, him sort of bumbling around, and accidentally touching Maeve's chest.

Blackwell looks at Maeve across the cafeteria and smiles, and if she doesn't make a move, he will. Approaching her asking:

> "Can I buy you a coffee after school?"

#### Coffee After School
At a local but nice coffee-shop called Nordhaven Roasters, the prices are somewhat high, but he wants to know Maeve. He'll know about Anthony Lockwood, asking about Maeve's connection.

[[Lockwood Commodities]] was one of his clients, they are usually dealing with the [[Bureau of Customs Duties]]. Julian can talk about the extensive protocals shipping companies are facing. There's complicated tariffs, noodly trade agreements, it's insane how much proof is required. It makes it nearly impossible for smaller corporations to compete. It takes an army of lawyers to make it all work sometimes.

Julian is fascinated with Maeve, finds her attractive, and wants to date her. Simple as that.

He'll ask Maeve if she's ever been to court, if she's interested in being a lawyer and nervous about seeing the inside of a courtroom.

He talks about his ambition of going back to Blackguard and regaining his status and respect. How he intends to be a private lawyer fighting for the little guy when he can.

#### His Story
Julian Blackwell was one of the greatest litigators in the largest lawfirms in the nation: [[Kenmore Associates]]. He took on a case representing the [[Worker's Union]] of [[Blackguard]] in a class-action lawsuit for the Railway Workers, against the [[Rowe Corporation]]. The case was simple, the corporation hadn't been calculating overtime correctly and owed billions in unpaid salaries.

###### Casework
He won the case handily in [[District Courthouses]], and the Corporation was set to pay 3 billion notes in damages. The case made its way to the [[Courts of Appeal]], which was expected move for Rowe, but likely to fail since it was an open and shut case. But -surprisingly, in a 7-6 decision, the Court ruled in favor of Rowe and refused to elaborate.

[[Llewellyn Rowe]] was off the hook, and bragged about it in the paper, calling Blackwell a hack in the [[Blackguard Journal]]. Thinking his career was over, Julian was drinking away at [[The Preston Building]].

###### Intrigue
Then his friend, Monique Toussant, and the Rowe rep on the case, found him. She drunkenly admitted to illegally tilting the court. Apparently there was a memo from the top requesting it.

When Julian sobered up, he went into the office and dug around and found confidential papers between Toussant and Hanin Yousef, the head of the Banner Ventures legal team, Banner Ventures being one of the owners of both the Rowe Corporation and Kenmore Associates. Hanin Yousef explained his intention to pressure and bribe the court to their side.

###### Leaking
Julian leaked all of this to the [[Blackguard Journal]], even if it was a break of Attorney-Client Confidentiality. It was all over the news and now the [[Supreme Court]] is considering stepping in, but they were unsure of how to proceed since there's lawbreakers on both sides. In the end, [[Lord Executive Magistrate]] [[Stuart Brownhill]] decided to disbar Julian and hear the extents of the case again.

In the end, Rowe was made to pay a comparatively low fee that amounted to a few hundred million notes. 

###### Regaining his Litigator License
The [[Arthusian University]] turned him away since he was such a controversial figure, so he fled here where his name hadn't appeared in the paper as much.

So, that's why Julian is here, retaking the Bar exam to eventually build up credibility and be able to go back to practicing law. Maybe not working for a major corporation again.

#### The Staghorn Terrace
Julian wants to take Maeve on a proper date, he offers to take her to the Staghorn Terrace, a rooftop bar where she can rub shoulders with the elites. He says there's a certain dance you have to do in order to excel in business. You've got to get familiar with the powerful people, but fight for the powerless.

He wants to buy her a drink and introduce her to some folks!

* [[Unionist]] Arnie Bolton, who people are already calling the next Celia Powell.
* Prof. Henk Vilgar, an orc professor who's in grief about a friend of his who died a few months ago. Warns against adventure.
* [[Casterian]] radical named Sigtryg Greppsson, recognizes the coin and says it's a standard currency in Tyrfang. Doesn't know how it got here, it's worthless in these parts. He believes Casteria is effecitvely a subject of Atroxia even if the law disagrees. 
	* Seeing the coin, he'd assume Maeve was on his side.

Julian asks Maeve what she thinks about adventure, if she likes a bit of excitement, because if she does. He's got a dare:

> "I've got this key from a friend of mine. It's the back entrance into the Northpass District Courthouse. How'd you like to go have a look inside?"

He wants to dare her!


## The Midnight Dare
They break into the courtroom, here's a few scenarios that would make this segment enjoyable:

* Breaking in should be a series of checks of some kind getting there unseen.
* Maeve and Julian find a courtroom and can roleplay a "court scene"
	* "Objection, Ms. Maeve. You're dangerously distracting the prosecution!"
* They're heard by a guard and they squeeze into a tiny broom cupboard where they're barely able to move without touching one another.
* They run out, being chased!
* If things go sideways, the cops get involved and they run through the streets.

At any point, three skill checks failed in a row will get them spotted and cornered by local security guards. Julian will cover Maeve's escape and get himself arrested instead. This is the "reward" for tonight's session. Can she have a powerful ally in Blackguard if she gets him out of all this.

If everything goes well, they could spend the night together.

#### Casterian Coin
Blackwell can shed some light on the coin. It's a "dogwhistle" for Casterian extremists when shown to certain people WHILE IN ATROXIA.

He doesn't know these extremists, but they believe they're being strangled by Atroxia. They likely intend on doing something crazy one day. He doesn't know.
