## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

## Back in Roachport
The Expeditionaries have returned to [[Roachport]], and the town is largely unaware of it! Do they not know that the conquering heroes have returned? Evidently not, after all, what difference does it make? They're about to learn just how big of a difference it makes.

Scenes in Roachport should be about local politics, money, and perception, but also... a little break? We'll see if that's possible. There's a fair few scenes we could do here:

* **Gideon Barnet** is excited to see the Expeditionaries return, 
* **Abner Willis** feels like the return of the Expeditionaries is a sign there may be hope for this county after all. "It's all Serendipity isn't it?"
* **Love Gillcrest**, a member of the Precient Few, has skipped town and is preparing to meet with his fellow members soon enough.
* **Abitha Loafwood**, is a [[smallfolk]] bard and is happy to help the Expeditionaries with PR.
* **Degory Whitelock** is amazed at the success of the party and thinks that windfalls like the one they just had could be the success they need to save this place!

#### The Tannery and the Heroes!
The Tannery is run by a man named Tobias Brock, and he's hosting his **monthly barbeque**! The whole town comes down here. Toby is a bit misanthropic, but he knows that nobody can thrive entirely alone so, in his words:

> "I set the stage for the kind of socializing I'm comfortable with, at the pace I like it."

He doesn't like long conversations, private moments, or physical touch. He came here to escape Blackguard.

The barbeque is huge, and the whole town will be here by nightfall! And the townsfolk would happily make Crossroads the guests of honor!

> "If I don't do this people start coming over asking: 'Hey Toby, how are you doing?' and 'How come you never visit?' and this way, they don't."

#### Agents of Big Government
Giles Nickels wrote up an article for the Roachport Bugle, and it's inflammatory to a degree bound to make Crossroads uncomfortable. There's a countless reactions this could spark, but most of them would be foolish.

H. Barton Cleveland, a noble-born elite and personal friend of Domilius and Powell, has arrived in Roachport to campaign on his big government platform. The platform includes promoting the County Cultivation Act, that he wrote himself! 

With him is an entourage of unknown individuals that so far have eluded further investigation, they're well funded, and preparing to chart the forests for construction. According to sources in Crosskeys, they've already begun funding on a massive lodge for a corporate entity that legally doesn't exist!

Who can say what's next for this band of operatives that have come here from Blackguard to tell us how to live our lives? I certainly cannot!

Giles Nickels.

#### The Blackguard Journal
Elias Altman brings two copies of the Blackguard Journal to [[Roachport]]. One to Giles Nichol at the [[Roachport Bugle]], and another to Jerry the barman at the Little Ferryman Pub and Inn. Crossroads could get a look at either copy.

This is an issue from 10 days ago.

###### SINDRUK INTRODUCES STEEL TARIFFS
The King of [[Sindruk]], Kharnik Drohart, has promised to get tough on [[Atroxian]] trade dominance and issued tariffs on all steel imports passing through the Wild Sea. 

[[Secretary for Commerce]], [[Lynn Foxworthy]], has called this the uninformed political maneuver  of an unpolitical mind, saying it will likely incur unintended consequences for the Sindruki people. He's directing the [[Office of Trade Policy]] to devise an aggressive counter and directing the [[Office of Economic Development]] to halt progress on the Sindruki School Construction project until an agreement can be reached.

Llewellyn Rowe, Chancellor for the Argent Sector and Leader of the Totalitarian Party, has said that this is yet another sign that the Domilius Administration is perceived as weak by our geopolitical rivals. Experts cannot agree on if this will start another trade war or recession like in the '30s.

###### DIABOLISM INVESTIGATION REACHES PARLIAMENT
The Oasby Attack Taskforce, consisting of members of the [[Bureau|Bureaus]] of Counter-Intelligence, Mage Enforcement, and Criminal Analysis, has reportedly sent investigators to interrogate Chancellors in Parliament on the attack. Even ones that were not present on the President's Train to Oasby.

Celia Powell, who spent her life being critical of the executive branch and its frequent misuse of departmental security services, like the [[Bureau of Counter-Intelligence]] during the [[Atmos Five]] proceedings, has refused to condemn the investigators actions. Saying: "The President is acting well within his constitutional right and what's more, well within the interests of the Atroxian people. And we do not want a government where some people are above suspicion."

Investigators refused to elaborate on why they were interrogating Chancellors.

###### CARL MOORE CALLS FOR EDUCATION REFORM 
[[Proletarian]] Carl Moore has called for [[First Chancellor]] Celia Powell to honor her pledge to the electorate to "put education first" on the agenda while there's still time. The chances of the government losing in the up-coming [[General Election]] has Moore concerned. He's demanding a compromise be reached to create a strong, powerful, and robust Department of Education within the [[Civic Commission]]. 

## The Hawkins Dinner
Constance Hawkins is a woman in her sixties with a strong southerner's accent. She moved here from Flintwood during the Secession and hasn't looked back since. She was a wildly successful flint merchant, turned stock broker in the post-war booms, and she sold out before the recession. So she's loaded.

She's refused to lend the County money because she doesn't think they're solvent. But she'd hitch her wagon to the Clevelands because at least they could stake real estate on it. That is, if she gets to like them and trust Crossroads.

A loan could be structured, one where interest payments worth 10% annually, paid in monthly installments. She'd lend as much as 1 million notes if she gets a business plan.
