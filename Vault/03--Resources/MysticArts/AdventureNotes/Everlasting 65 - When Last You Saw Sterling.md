## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

Altan's secret mission looking into the Grave Order

## Finding Sterling
Altan hasn't seen Sterling in ages, he doesn't know what he's up to, but maybe he's in deep shit. Dee is willing to help! Use Dee to ask questions about Sterling. Let Altan explain the character and use that to render him.

Srg. Lancaster gives him the file and asks him to "see if any of this is anything"

Detective Sahid Ramy, as well as Detectives Gale and O'Brien, all get similar assignments. This happens about once a month. Huge file dump and no guidance. Just "go see if this is anything."

It's collected and collated by the [[International Intelligence Committee]].

#### What is the Grave Order?
Nobody knows, it's something that's just showed up in an intelligence report and now they're supposed to figure it out.

#### Where Could He Be?
Maybe Altan's dad, Purev, knows? He and Sybil Sterling, David's mom, still keep in touch. They could go to the bazaar and Purev might share that he's working in logging at the pigfarms in the swinewood by the city limits.

#### David's Help
A flashback within a flashback! His family's government issue house is on fire, burning brightly. David sits with Altan and watches as the fire fighters rush in, David puts an arm around Altan. The next few days, Altan spent a lot of time with David. What did they do?

- "Dad died and he started working" said Altan. Likely refers to David's stepdad Duncan.
- He's in charge of taking care of his grandma, Harvey's mom Marion.

## Sterling's World
Dee wonders why Altan never talks about Sterling.

**Who is David Sterling?** He's a slobby looking bum. This is a childhood friend of Altan's named **David Sterling**, a cockney crook who knows a lot about the Blackguard underbelly.

David's the son of [[Bureau of Counter-Intelligence]] **Special Agent**, Harvey Sterling, who was killed during [[Project Atmos]] crisis. He's born in 1422, and never knew his dad. Though he eventually learned what he did, and that's turned him off of law enforcement forever.

Sterling refers to Altan as "Bolor", it's some kid thing. He's missed him.

#### The Pigfarm in the Swinewood
Then they can find themselves at the edge of town and meet Sterling among his co-workers. These are all laborers who work for around 10 notes a day. Real poor sods.

They all give Altan and Dee a look, like "oh you think you're better than us."

It only takes a bit of poking around to find David Sterling, who's happy to see Altan. He'll tell them anything they need to know as long as it doesn't trace back to him. As long as he's off any lists.

#### Debt Collectors
Two debt collectors show up (**thugs**) and Sterling begs Altan and Dee to not "be cops" and try and just deal with it amicably. The debt collectors work for "the syndicate" and they're not listening to David's excuses anymore.

Eventually, a gun gets pulled and David runs off! Into the sewers if need be!

#### There are no Sewer Rattigans
Going through the sewers, Sterling insists that Sewer Rattigan are a rumor. But they'll get ambushed by some kobolds anyway!

Why sewers, Sterling says that it's "the syndicate finds me otherwise"

#### Navigating
Skill challenge to navigate the sewers successfully.

#### Who is the Grave Order?
Sterling knows of the Order since he sold them some difficult to acquire reagents for spells. Hag's eye, witch's toenail, harpy's claw, and a diamond. (making a golem) 

He got paid through the roof, but ratted on their location to a band of "adventurers" called the Golden Valor. Last he knew, the Valor is dead.

Grave Order consists of a few people, it's a small club, like a party of adventurers. There's: 

* Halorin Yandres, a dead commander from Andrian.
* Jedar Pike, their undead priest from Bloodcreek.
* Lestheris Soldrei, an elf wizard corpse from Thalanil.
* Mycete, a tiefling plant thing from Redyard.
* ...and their trainee, Aubrey Vlask, a Blackguarder, who seems like she's destined to die an unceremonious death.

13 Eastview, is an apartment in Whitford.

## The Smelly Apartment
They find the Grave Order's abandoned basement apartment, connected to the sewers, inside there's a few things of note:

* A Teleportation Circle they've drawn on the ground.
* A foul odor of corpses and death.
* A handful of trinkets and boubles, reagents for spellcasting.
* There's a couple of scrolls he could find: let him roll for 3 if he searches.
* A single black coin with a skull on it. GRAVE ORDER.

There's also several **Crawling Claws** and a single **Ghoul** guard dog. And one thing is clear, they've packed up and left. No intentions of coming back... but they could at any point.

#### Tease for the Future
They did all this for a poorly drawn map, it describes the Promised Land, the land the Grave Order is searching for, this Promised Land they intend to conquer: 

* Inside there's the Broken Spire that the Prescient Few took.
* There's the Boneyard and its Palace of Death that belongs to Count Rodolph.
* The Colossus of Ruin and the Skull King (Death Tyrant).
* The Temple of the Blood Moon, which they think Elves took.
* And the lair of the Dracolich.
