## Introduction
**Flow Comes First.** Let's play a game where things get done.
* We'll keep rule arguments to a minimum, stick to rulings where possible.
* If you have a hard time with focus, try keeping notes.
* Have your dice out, your character sheet available.
* Your phone on silent.

**Immersion is King.** Let's play a game where people are immersed.
* Respect the scene. Keep meta talk to a minimum.
* Respect the characters. Don't joke when people are roleplaying.
* Respect the ignorance. Artless exposition might be worse than focused ignorance.
* Respect the player. Players can ask for what they need, don't volunteer it unprompted.

#### Abandoned Watermill
This is a single run-down structure with little in it. The shack is run down in absolute desolation and there's little else going on here. It used to belong to Toby's parents.

But there are a few scuff marks that stick out to anyone making a DC 12+ investigation check. It's incomprehensible, but the important bit is this: there might be something to be discovered here, if time is spent.

Spending two hours searching this hex will yield the following discovery:

> Tucked into the hills, a mile north of the mill, beneath a mound of grass, there's a single humanoid body, obsidian colored skin and white hair and it's still.

There's a dead drow here! He's a rank-and-file soldier of House Kenafi and seems to have been killed by a pair of well-placed barbed arrows. One of which has been pulled out - a mistake that cost this drow his life.

Searching the body yields the following: a *shortsword*, a *hand crossbow*, five vials of *drow poison*, a coin purse with 124 silver pieces, and Boots of Elvenkind.
###### Drow Poison *(Injury Poison)* 
A creature subjected to Drow Poison DC 11 Constitution saving throw or have the poisoned condition for 1 hour. If the saving throw fails by 5 or more, the target is also unconsious while poisoned in this way. The target wakes up if it takes damage or if another creature takes an action to shake it awake.
