Based on the video, the creator's main critique is a lack of consistency in character design. Writers often flip-flop between two distinct interpretations of a "Sin Demon" without realizing they are contradictory.

The two categories established in the video are:
1.  **The Embodiment/Sufferer (The Addict):** The character is a victim of the sin. They are obsessed with doing it themselves.
2.  **The Instigator/Drawer (The Dealer):** The character inspires the sin in others. They are the source or tempter, often remaining in control themselves.

Here is the breakdown of the 7 Deadly Sins using this framework, including the examples from the video and new examples for the rest.

### 1. Gluttony
*   **The Sufferer (The Addict):** A character who cannot stop eating. They are ravenous, messy, and likely monstrous.
    *   *Video Example:* A guy who loves eating.
    *   *Archetype:* The Blob, generic zombies, No-Face (Spirited Away).
*   **The Instigator (The Dealer):** A character who creates irresistible food to make others fat or sick.
    *   *Video Example:* A 5-Star Chef.
    *   *Archetype:* The Witch from Hansel & Gretel, Willy Wonka.

### 2. Lust
*   **The Sufferer (The Addict):** A character who is desperate for intimacy or gratification but is usually pathetic, creepy, or unfulfilled.
    *   *Video Example:* A "gooner sex pest" (someone addicted to pornography/voyeurism).
    *   *Archetype:* Johnny Bravo, Mineta (My Hero Academia), Quagmire (Family Guy).
*   **The Instigator (The Dealer):** A character who is fiercely attractive and manipulates the desires of others.
    *   *Video Example:* A seductive "Girlboss."
    *   *Archetype:* Succubus/Incubus, Poison Ivy, Jessica Rabbit.

### 3. Greed
*   **The Sufferer (The Addict):** A character who hoards wealth, is paranoid about losing a penny, and lives in squalor despite being rich because they refuse to spend.
    *   *Archetype:* Smaug (The Hobbit), Scrooge McDuck, Gollum (coveting the Ring).
*   **The Instigator (The Dealer):** A character who offers you wealth to corrupt your soul. They are often generous, but with strings attached.
    *   *Archetype:* The Devil at the Crossroads, a predatory Loan Shark, a Casino Owner.

### 4. Sloth
*   **The Sufferer (The Addict):** A character who physically cannot or will not move. They sleep all day, are covered in dust/moss, and represent stagnation.
    *   *Archetype:* Snorlax (Pokémon), Sloth (Fullmetal Alchemist), The Dude (The Big Lebowski).
*   **The Instigator (The Dealer):** A character or entity that does everything for you, removing your purpose and making you dependent and lazy.
    *   *Archetype:* The Robots/AI from *Wall-E*, a hypnotist, a drug dealer selling tranquilizers.

### 5. Wrath
*   **The Sufferer (The Addict):** A berserker. They are screaming, foaming at the mouth, and cannot control their own violence. They are in pain.
    *   *Archetype:* The Hulk, Kratos (early God of War), Doomguy.
*   **The Instigator (The Dealer):** A cold, calculating manipulator who whispers lies to start wars. They remain calm while everyone else kills each other.
    *   *Archetype:* The Joker (The Dark Knight), Palpatine/The Emperor (Star Wars), Iago (Othello).

### 6. Envy
*   **The Sufferer (The Addict):** A character who is miserable, insecure, and hates anyone doing better than them. They are usually "ugly" inside and out.
    *   *Archetype:* The Evil Queen (Snow White), Salieri (Amadeus), Gollum (again).
*   **The Instigator (The Dealer):** A character who is physically perfect and flaunts it to make everyone else feel inadequate. They exist to be looked at.
    *   *Archetype:* Regina George (Mean Girls), the "Perfect Influencer," Helen of Troy.

### 7. Pride
*   **The Sufferer (The Addict):** A narcissist who demands worship. They are arrogant, loud, and cannot handle criticism. Their ego is their weakness.
    *   *Archetype:* Homelander (The Boys), Gaston (Beauty and the Beast), King Joffrey (Game of Thrones).
*   **The Instigator (The Dealer):** A sycophant or "Hype Man." They puff up your ego, telling you you're a god until you make a fatal mistake due to overconfidence.
    *   *Archetype:* Grima Wormtongue (Lord of the Rings), a corrupt stylist, the devil whispering that you are too good for the rules.
